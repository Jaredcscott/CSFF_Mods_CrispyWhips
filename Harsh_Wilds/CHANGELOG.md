# Changelog

## 0.11.6 (2026-10-15)

A balance pass to keep Harsh Wilds' rewards in step with the base game. The threats are unchanged.

### Fixed

- The Mud Volcano's **Shield yourself** can now splash you (60 of 100), and the Nixie's and Selkie's **Hold fast** now really decides between resisting and being drawn in (40 to 60); before, the splash and both of those outcomes never happened.
- **The Lynx, Musk Ox and Wisent Coats now protect you in a fight.** They carried an Armor bonus but none of the armour a base-game coat has, so a hit to the torso went straight through. Each now has Torso armour 10, as the Bear and Wolf Coats do, and wears like them.
- **Fairy Stones have a use.** The stones from the Fairy Ring, the Faerie Realm and the Fairy King did nothing. Drag one onto the Threshold Stone (**Offer a Fairy Stone**) and its offering lasts about six days instead of three.

### Changed

- **Musk oxen are a winter treat, not a larder.** A carcass gives about 62 meat (was about 75), a stag's and a third for the harder fight, and a killed ox returns after about 30 days (was about 14). The herd is still three.
- **Beast Brew and Earth Brew build affinity at the Sacred Oak's pace:** +0.5 a tick (was +2), so a draught gives about 24 over half a day (was 96). Each now takes 8 hours to research (was 2).
- **Athame rites:** Let Blood gives Beast Affinity +100 (was +150); beside lit incense +150 (was +250). Call Rain beside incense thickens the clouds by 300 (was 350). Every incense rite now burns up one point of that incense's fuel.
- **Coats match their base-game tier:** the Lynx Coat's Climbing Aid and Ambush Aid are halved to 8.4 each, the size of the Wolf Coat's two aids, and the Musk Ox Coat warms +7.5 (was +10), as the Bear Coat does. The Wisent Coat is unchanged.
- **The lindwurm pays like a large beast:** its Butcher now gives 150 meat (was 20), about what a bear gives.
- The README describes a gentler setup: the switches to turn off for the forest's powers without its harshest surprises.

## 0.11.0 (2026-10-12)
- New: the **Faerie Realm**, the mod's first place beyond the forest. **Enter the Ring** at a fairy ring, at night under a full moon (no time), and you stay up to four hours before the ring sends you home to its grove. **Step Back Through the Ring** takes you home at once. No raid rolls for your food while you are away, Harsh Wilds animals cannot attack you there, and the base game's wildlife still can. **Wander the Moonlit Meadow** (two hours) finds Fairyweed, and by chance Spirit Mushrooms, Mistletoe or a Fairy Stone. New `[Threats] FaerieTricks` switch (default on): about hourly, one in five, **Pixie-Led** costs 10 Stamina and never keeps you from going home. Checked in a test harness, not yet in real play.
- New: the **Moon Realm** and the **Underworld**, with the Faerie Realm's rules and a three-hour stay. Build a **Moon Basin** (Magic > Shrines; a clay bowl and two small stones; only beside a Moon Stone) and **Step into the Moonlight** at night under a full moon. A Stone Cairn's **Descend Beneath the Cairn** goes down at night. With Forest Lore, its Ancient Cairn offers **Go Down the Tomb Stair** too. **Search the Silver Shallows** finds feathers, moon moss, Niter Crystals or Flint. **Search the Barrow Halls** finds Bones, Ash, Bird Bones or a Sulphurous Stone. New switches `MoonGlamour` (**Moonstruck**, adds 20 Stress) and `RestlessDead` (**The Restless Dead**, costs 14 Stamina and adds 12 Stress), both default on. Checked in a test harness, not yet in real play.
- New: the **Fairy King** and **Fairy Queen** hold court in the Faerie Realm. **Speak with the King** or **Speak with the Queen** offers one bargain each, once a run, and only while you can pay. The King gives two Fairy Stones for 60 Willpower. The Queen takes away 40 Stress and gives a sprig of Mistletoe for 16 Stamina. A realm entered before this update gets them on the next visit. Checked in a test harness, not yet in real play.
- New: **casting bones** (Magic > Implements; three Bones and a Charcoal, 30 castings). **Cast the Bones** drops a card saying whether the eagle, the black bear, both or neither can come for your food within a day. The reading is always true, though a raid it names may still not come. It says nothing about the wolverine, the heron or world events. Not yet confirmed in a running game.
- New: the **athame**, a ritual knife (Magic > Implements; a Bone Knife, charcoal and twine) that also butchers and has 40 uses. **Let Blood** (an hour) gives you an Arm Laceration wound and Beast Affinity +150, fading by itself, and is refused while an arm wound is open. **Call Rain** (two hours, once every two days) thickens the clouds so the weather refreshes at once, likely but not certain to rain. A lit Herbs and Fungi Incense Bundle or a lit Community Mod Chest Incense, Heather or Juniper Burner nearby adds a stronger button for each rite; without those mods only the base buttons appear. Not yet confirmed in a running game.
- New: the **wolverine**, a fourth raid, behind a new `[Threats] Wolverine` switch (default on). In winter, at most once every three days, a "Wolverine at the Traps" card may appear when a sprung trap stands in your place (about one chance in twenty per sprung trap). **See what it takes** removes one Fox, Badger, Boar, Doe, Stag or Wolf Carcass, or a tied Boar, from a placed structure there, so a catch kept in a placed chest or shelf can be taken too. Small game is the eagle's, so a visit can find nothing. It is not covered by `RaidPressure`.
- New: the **wandering shaman**, a trader with no switch. He comes to the Green Glade on the eighth day of every season for one day. He restocks with dried spirit mushrooms, dried fairyweed, dried fly agaric, the three spirit charms and winter mistletoe, sold at half again their worth. Once you have bound a spirit, he teaches one calling for a spirit you have not bound yet, once a run. The charms and mistletoe now have a trading value, so no trader hands them over for nothing. Not yet confirmed in a running game.
- New: the **selkie**, a fourth fair-folk night threat, behind a new `[Threats] Selkie` switch (default on) and only with Far Territory installed. At night on Far Territory's Driftwood Shore she may come ashore and sing. Turn away (Courage helps) or follow, and you stay soaked and cold for about four hours. A Threshold Stone offering lowers her odds. Not yet confirmed in a running game.
- New: the **lindwurm**, a fifth rare beast, behind a new `[Threats] Lindwurm` switch (default on). About one day in a hundred and twenty, one lairs in the Great Cave for about a week and a half and never leaves it. In the cave it can come for you once a day and fights harder than a bear. Its carcass is butchered in one go and holds a spirit: **Release the Spirit** gives Beast Affinity, and its Summoning Ritual binds the **Dragon Spirit** (Strength and Alertness Aid), which joins Magic > Calling Rituals. Not yet confirmed in a running game.
- New: the **lake beast** and the **frost demon**, two more rare beasts, behind new `[Threats] LakeBeast` and `FrostDemon` switches (default on). About one day in sixty, a lake beast comes to the lake and its banks for about three days. At night it can come for you on a bank, once a night, and fights as hard as a bear. On about one winter evening in fifteen, a frost demon comes down to the northern hills until about 05:00 and can hunt you out of doors there, once a night, with Frostnip and Frostbite for bites. Each leaves a carcass.
- New: **fair folk**, three rare night threats behind new `[Threats] Banshee`, `Nixie` and `WildHunt` switches (default on), and a **Threshold Stone**. The banshee keens at night while you bleed badly. The nixie sings on 27 river and lake banks; follow it and you stay soaked and cold for about four hours. The Wild Hunt rides on a winter night for about five hours, adding stress outdoors and sometimes bruising you. The Threshold Stone (Magic > Shrines; a heavy stone and three small ones) takes an offering of salt, a flatbread, honeycomb or cheese curds and lowers all these odds for about three days. Not yet confirmed in a running game.
- New: the **fairy ring**, found by foraging (about 3 percent a forage) in seventeen oak and birch places. It works in saves you already have, and only one stands on a board. **Pick Toadstools** (an hour) gives two or three fly agaric, and one time in twelve a Fairy Stone, once every two days. **Dance in the Ring** is offered at night only. Not yet confirmed in a running game.
- New hazard: the **dancing plague**, behind a new `[Threats] DancingPlague` switch (default on). A dance in the ring at night may leave you unable to stop. For about four hours you lose stamina and food energy faster, up to eight if two take hold. It wears off by itself, or **Burn the Smudge to Break the Dance** ends it at once at a lit fire. Switching it off hides Dance in the Ring. Not yet confirmed in a running game.
- New hazard: **spirit sickness**, behind a new `[Threats] SpiritSickness` switch (default on). When a Lynx, Goat or Blood Spirit you called is angered, a daily roll may leave you spirit-sick, in three stages that lower Focus by 10, 20 and 30 and raise stress. It never fades by itself. Cure it with the **Cleansing Smudge** (Magic > Rituals; bog myrtle and Dry Juniper Needles): **Burn the Smudge** at a lit fire takes off a stage. Not yet confirmed in a running game.
- New: the **Stone Cairn** and the **Ancestor Spirit** (Magic > Shrines; three heavy stones, then twelve stones; one to a place). Keep a vigil of about a day at the cairn and the Ancestors answer. Then its Blessing gives Courage and less Loneliness for a day or so, and the **Ancestor Enchantment** puts their essence into an enchantable item. Unlike the West Stone's blessing, it gives no combat armour and does not wear down your mental structure. Not yet confirmed in a running game.
- New: **ritual objects**, made on purpose. A **Spirit Jar** keeps a spirit's essence. A **Spirit Mask** raises Beast Affinity while your mind is open. **Lynx, Goat and Blood Charms** each hold half their spirit's gift. **Lynx, Musk Ox and Wisent Coats** (Tailoring > Leather, Leatherworking 15) give warmth, armour and their beast's help. A **Beast Totem** raises Beast Affinity at camp. Not yet confirmed in a running game.
- Changed: the bandit no longer takes Roasted Pine Nuts or Roasted Acorns, so he can take seven travel foods, not nine. Recipes in other mods use those items.
- Changed: 25 card pictures are now cropped to their subject, like the base game's art, and take about a sixth of the space. The Beast, Earth and Muscle brews now show a clay bowl filled in each brew's own colour. New art for the Black Bear and Eagle events, the Chewing Stick and the bandit's body.

## 0.7.0 (2026-10-01)
- New afflictions: two small ones, each a visible status with an icon and an alert at each of three stages, each behind its own `[Threats]` switch (both default on). Neither gets better on its own. Every chance, number and length is a first guess that has not been balanced against play.
- **Fleas and lice**: you can pick them up by sleeping or napping with an Improvised Bed, Improvised Shelter or Hideout in the room (about 1 sleep in 10) or a Bedroll or Log Bed (about 1 in 20), by being very dirty (Filth 250 or more, about 1 day in 8), and by keeping a Dog Friend, or with Sirus23's Mod Collection a tamed wolf or fox (about 1 day in 20 each). Each hit moves you up one of three stages (Flea Bites, Fleas, Infested): Comfort down by 10, 25 and 40, and stress building faster by 0.25, 0.5 and 1.0 every quarter hour. Cure them by staying clean (Filth 100 or less: a quarter of a stage every six hours), with the smoke or rub of bog myrtle or juniper or Bug Repellent Salve (4 every quarter hour while the base game's protection lasts), or with the new Herbal Wash (bog myrtle, dry juniper needles and a kept Clay Bowl; "Wash with Herbs" takes off a stage and a half and 100 Filth). With Herbs and Fungi installed, drag one of its herbs onto the Herbal Wash for "Wash with Herb Infusion", which clears them completely.
- **Tooth decay**: it comes from eating enough honeycomb or sugar to reach the game's "tired of eating sweet things" level (about 1 time in 3 each time you reach it), from eating a lot of fruit in a short time (about 1 in 6), and from plain time (about 1 day in 60). Three stages, Sore Tooth, Toothache and Rotten Tooth: Pain up by 30, 60 and 90 (on its own it stays below the base game's first Pain band, but it adds to any pain you already have) and stress building faster by 0.25, 0.5 and 0.75 every quarter hour. A new Chewing Stick (one Willow Twigs; "Chew" takes off half a stage and is used up) treats it, and with Herbs and Fungi installed a herb dragged onto it takes off a whole stage. A new Tooth Puller (Bones and Twine, three uses) offers "Pull the Bad Tooth" only in the worst stage: half an hour, clears the decay, and adds 40 to your stress at once. Sweets and time can start it again.
- Known and not prevented: Infested fleas (stress +1.0) and a Rotten Tooth (+0.75) together add more stress than the game takes away near the top of its range, so stress keeps climbing toward Depression until one is treated. Either one alone can do the same when something else is already adding stress: in a test game where other things already added about 1.1, the game's own pull near the top was only 0.15, so fleas alone still added 0.85 every quarter hour and a bad tooth alone 0.6.
- Catching fleas from a bed was confirmed in a test game in a real nap and a real sleep, each rolling once with the chance made certain. An earlier test nap that caught nothing was a fault in the test, which had already spent that sleep's roll, not in the mod. The other flea sources and every cure were confirmed in test games too.
- The recipes for the Herbal Wash, Chewing Stick and Tooth Puller are in Survival > Medical, researchable from the start, and each has its own card art.
- New config switches `[Threats] FleasAndLice` and `ToothDecay` (both default on). Setting one to false stops it and clears any you have at the start of the run, after a full game restart. The recipes and cards stay in the game and have nothing to treat.

## 0.6.0 (2026-09-30)
- New world events: six rare ones, each with its own switch under `[Threats]` (all default on) and each announced by a Moderate alert with an icon in the status strip. Every chance, hour, range and length is a first guess that has not been balanced against play.
- **Drought**: in summer, after at least four days without rain (a character with the Everrain perk never has one), a drought may set in. For about a week no rain falls, so rain catchers, cisterns and barrels stop refilling and rain stops watering garden plots; wells, ponds and rivers do not depend on rain. It does not make anything dry faster. It ends by itself and rain returns as the clouds build up again.
- **Insect plague**: in summer, biting swarms may descend for about three days. Outdoors you are bitten far more than usual and the bites add up; the swarm does not follow you into a cabin, mud hut, cave or mine. Rain, a fire at your camp, snakegrass fed to a hearth, a worn Large Cloth or Hoodie (each takes one step off) and staying indoors thin it out.
- **Crop blight**: outside winter, while a garden plot stands where you are, a blight may spread fungus for about two days through the vanilla planted crops in the garden plots of that place (growing and mature stages; not dormant crops, not the Fields, not Herbs and Fungi plants) and take health from crops that are already badly infected. Fungicide or garlic water on the plot, and not overwatering it, fight it. A check at load switches the blight's damage off, with a warning in the log, if the game loads its crop list differently from what the mod expects.
- **Blood moon**: at dusk on about one full moon in three the moon turns red and two more lynxes than usual prowl the lynx's four areas until dawn. They are ordinary lynxes (the same stalk, fight and carcass) that exist only while the blood moon runs and the `Lynx` switch is on; with `Lynx` off it still rolls and shows its alert but brings no extra lynx.
- **Eclipse**: at noon on a new-moon day in the first ten days of summer, with about an even chance, the light falls sharply for about an hour (measured on a clear noon: 100 down to 20, which no vanilla light check refuses, so it is an alert and a lower light reading rather than a task block). It changes the Light stat; the screen did not dim in the one reading taken.
- **Aurora**: on dark winter nights (a new moon or a waxing crescent) an aurora may appear for up to ten hours. While you stand outside under the open night sky your mood rises a little every quarter hour, pausing while you are indoors and never carried past about 100. It does nothing else.
- New config switches `[Threats] Drought`, `InsectPlague`, `CropBlight`, `BloodMoon`, `Eclipse` and `Aurora` (all default on). A drought and an eclipse already under way run their course when switched off; for the others the effect stops at once and only the alert may linger.

## 0.5.0 (2026-09-29)
- New threat: the **bandit**, a ragged man with a spear who roams the river corridor (Southern Rapids, Rocky Bank, Northern Rapids and River Pass) between 6 in the morning and 8 in the evening. None exists during the first ten days of a run (a save this mod is added to counts those ten days from its first load with this version), and the first one turns up about a day after that. He only ambushes a player who is on his own place, between 7 in the morning and 7 in the evening and at most once a day. You can also walk up and use Approach to start the fight yourself, and "Back away" at the start of any fight costs nothing.
- Winning: a beaten bandit leaves a Fallen Bandit. "Search the body" pays one of three small hauls (Dried Lean Fish Meat and a Springberry Fat Cake; a Flint Knife and Dried Lean Fish Meat; a Flint and a Rope) and the body is gone afterwards. A killed bandit is replaced after about a week.
- Escaping mid-fight, backing away, or beating him until he runs costs nothing. When he runs, when you escape and when he robs you, he then moves to one of his four river places.
- Losing: if you cannot fight on, the bandit goes through your pack. He takes up to three pieces of travel food from your hand row and from what is inside any bag or basket you carry, and a "Robbed by a Bandit" popup lists what he took. Only nine foods can be taken: Dried Blood Sausage, Dried Rich Blood Sausage, Billberry Fat Cake, Springberry Fat Cake, Hard Cheese Wedge, Aged Cheese Wedge, Meat Cake, Roasted Pine Nuts and Roasted Acorns. He never takes a tool, anything you wear, anything a recipe or task needs, or anything in a store or chest you have set down. If you carry none of those nine he takes nothing, and nothing is said.
- New config switch `[Threats] Bandits` (default on). Setting it to false removes the bandit and the theft after a full game restart.
- Now requires CSFFModFramework 2.28.0 or later (was 2.27.0). Its attack duty waits for the end of your current action, so an ambush no longer cuts a trip short.

## 0.4.0 (2026-09-29)
- New raid: the **eagle**. Now and then, while you are outdoors, an "Eagle Overhead" card appears, and its "See what it takes" button shows the one piece of small game the eagle carried off from the ground of the place you are in. It only takes game lying loose on the ground (dead hares, partridges, ducks, squirrels and mice, frogs, bird meat, trout, perch, pike, char and crayfish), never anything in a container, a trap or your hands, and it does not come at all when there is nothing for it. It stays away for roughly the first two weeks and comes at most once every two days.
- New raid: the **black bear**. In autumn a "Black Bear at the Stores" card now and then appears, and its "See what it takes" button shows the one piece of meat, fish or sausage the bear carried off from a drying rack, barrel, shelf or other store standing in the place you are in. It is a raid announced by a card, not an animal you can meet: it cannot be fought, killed or butchered, and there is no Approach button. The first five days of each autumn are quiet, it comes at most once every three days, only while you are outdoors, and a tame dog on the board lowers the odds. Food lying on the ground or in a basket or sack set down on the ground is out of its reach.
- New config switches `[Threats] Eagle` and `[Threats] BlackBear` (both default on). Setting one to false stops that raid after a full game restart.

## 0.3.0 (2026-09-28)
- New creature: the **wisent**, a huge wild ox of the oak glades around Green Glade. It never attacks first. Approach it and you may leave it alone or fight; its charges and horns hit harder than any boar's. Its carcass is bled, skinned for a Very Large Fresh Skin, and butchered in four cuts for a great deal of meat, organs, bones and two horns. A killed wisent is replaced after about two weeks.
- New creature: the **musk ox**, a winter-only herd ox of the northern clearings around Pine Meadows. It never attacks first, and in a fight it is hard to surprise, hard to hit from a distance and slow to give up. Its carcass is bled, skinned for a Large Fresh Fur, and butchered like the wisent's.
- New config switches `[Threats] Wisent` and `[Threats] MuskOx` (both default on). Setting one to false removes that animal from the world after a full game restart.
- Changed: the lynx now counts like any vanilla animal. Fighting or butchering one satisfies the Wolf Urge. Butchering its carcass takes your butchering and skinning skills and perks into account, and butchering it indoors without a butchering mat dirties the room. Its carcass now carries vanilla's own carcass tags, and plays vanilla's sound when it appears.

## 0.2.1 (2026-09-27)
- Fixed: the lynx never stalked you. Its stalking range was set in the wrong units, so the lynx only ever cared about a player already standing on its square. It now comes for a player one or two squares away at night, and leaves one three or more squares away alone. Found by an automated test run.

## 0.2.0 (2026-09-26)
- New creature: the **lynx**. It roams the rocky pine forests from dusk until dawn and leaves hard-to-find tracks. It stalks a player who is near it at night and attacks at most once a night. You can also approach it and fight. Its carcass butchers with a knife for fur, meat, fat and bones. A killed lynx is replaced after about a week.
- New config switch `[Threats] Lynx` (default on). Setting it to false removes the lynx from the world after a full game restart.
- Now requires CSFFModFramework 2.27.0 or later, which lets a roaming animal come and go with a switch like this one.
