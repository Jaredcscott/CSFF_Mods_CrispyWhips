# Forest Lore

**Version:** 0.1.3
**Author:** Jared
**Requires:** CSFFModFramework 2.28.0 or later
**Status:** pre-release, early development.

Forest Lore adds the forest's own landmarks, ruins and hidden places. Each is a place on the map with something to do there, and everything you take away is an ordinary vanilla card. Every count, chance, refill rate and bonus below is a placeholder, not a balanced value. The mod has no settings.

| Place | What you find |
|---|---|
| Barrow Wood (south of the Old Glade) | Ancient Cairn, Standing Stones, Circle Stone, Line of Petrospheres, Tree Hollow shelter |
| Old Battlefield (north of the Moon Hills) | A field to search six times, a Fallen Watchtower to clear |
| Abandoned Homestead (east of the Clay Banks) | A ruin to salvage and repair into a shelter with storage, plus a Grinding Slab |
| Mud Springs (south of the Peat Bog) | Mud Volcano, Salt Crust, Tar Seep |
| Guano Rock (east of the Lake Island, a swim away) | Guano Ledge for Manure |
| Rock Shelter and the Sunken Caves (west of the Old Hollow) | A shelter, three cave chambers, a lake to drink from and fish |
| Bog Body | A rare find while digging peat at the vanilla Peat Bog |
| Climbing Aids | Stairs and ladders that make vanilla climbs safer |

**Compatibility:** Far Territory puts one ring of Standing Stones on its Wildrye Plain when both mods are installed. Nothing here needs Far Territory.

**Language:** English and Simplified Chinese.

## Barrow Wood

An old oak wood south of the Old Glade, reached by a new South exit. It is a copy of the vanilla Old Grove, so its oaks, foraging and woodcutting work the same.

**Ancient Cairn.** A grass-grown burial mound.
- **Clear the Entrance** (1 hour, three times) clears the brambles and fallen stones.
- **Open the Tomb** (30 minutes, four times) brings out Bones, often Bone Pieces, sometimes a bronze nugget and rarely a Blunt Rusty Axe. A Stone sharpens the axe into a usable Rusty Axe.

The bronze nugget, here and everywhere below, is a vanilla Metal Nugget of Tin Bronze at Metal Quality 40. A Tin Bronze crucible needs six, so one cairn will not fill it.

**Standing Stones.** A ring of tall stones, one carrying the runes "Halfdan was here". **Read the Stones** (15 minutes) is free and repeatable. **Search the Centre** (30 minutes) works once and gives Flint and a bronze nugget.

**Circle Stone.** **Read the Stone** repeats freely. **Search Beneath the Stone** works once and gives Stone and Flint.

**Line of Petrospheres.** **Read the Line** repeats freely. **Search Along the Line** works once and gives Stone.

**Tree Hollow.** A hollow oak to shelter in.
- **Nap** (4 hours) and **Sleep** (8 hours) work like the vanilla Hideout's.
- While you are in Barrow Wood it gives +1 Rain Protection and +1 Sun Protection.
- It never decays and cannot be repaired or deconstructed.

An emptied cairn stays empty for good. Finds go straight into your hands, and anything you leave in Barrow Wood stays there.

## Old Battlefield

A field north of the Moon Hills, reached by a new North exit. It is a copy of the vanilla Badger Hill without the Moon Hills' shrine.

- **Search the Field** (30 minutes) works six times. Each search gives Bones, often Bone Pieces, sometimes a bronze nugget and rarely a Blunt Rusty Axe.
- **Clear the Rubble** on the Fallen Watchtower (1 hour) works once and gives Stone, Wood and Long Sticks.

Both stay spent once used.

## Abandoned Homestead

A homestead east of the Clay Banks across the river meadow, reached by a new East exit. It is a copy of the vanilla Clay Banks, with its river, trees, reeds and water.

**Ruined Homestead.** Salvage it, then repair it.
- **Salvage the Ruin** (1 hour) works four times and gives Long Sticks and Twine, and sometimes Stone and Wood.
- Once nothing is left, drag a Wooden Plank or a Rope onto it (**Repair the Homestead**, 30 minutes). Six repairs are needed, in any mix, and each item is used up.
- **Raise the Roof** (1 hour) then turns the ruin into the Homestead.

**Homestead.** Nap (4 hours) and Sleep (8 hours) work like the vanilla Hideout's. It gives +1 Rain Protection and +1 Sun Protection while you are there, has six storage slots that keep their contents, and never decays.

**Grinding Slab.** A fixed Saddle Quern by the door. Put a millable card in the hollow (edible acorns, rye grains, charcoal, greenstone, bog iron) and **Mill** (30 minutes) it into flour or powder.

## Mud Springs

Warm mud south of the Peat Bog, reached by a new South exit. It is a copy of the vanilla Fetid Bog, so Dig Peat works here too. Each patch refills slowly while you are on the board.

- **Mud Volcano.** **Scoop Mud** (15 minutes) gives a Mud Pile, eight scoops from a full crater. While you stand here it also warms you (+8 Perceived Temperature).
- **Salt Crust.** **Scrape Salt** (15 minutes) gives Salt, two scrapes from a full crust. It regrows about ten days per scrape, so salt stays scarce.
- **Tar Seep.** **Collect Tar** (15 minutes) gives Heated Tar, four collections from a full pool at about five days each. The tar cools into vanilla Cold Tar by itself.

A spent patch keeps its button and says there is nothing to take yet.

## Guano Rock

A bare rock in the lake east of the Lake Island, reached by a swim. It is a copy of the vanilla Willow Ways.

- The new East button is the Lake Island's own "Swim West" action. Crossing takes 15 minutes in every season, soaks you (Wetness +100, Stamina -10) and washes off filth and mud. The way back is an ordinary travel button.
- **Scrape Guano** (15 minutes) on the Guano Ledge gives one Manure, eight scrapes from a full ledge. Use it in the Compost Bin (**Throw Inside**) or on fields.
- The ledge refills at about a day and a quarter per scrape.

## Bog Body

A rare find in the vanilla Peat Bog north of the Mud Springs. Each **Dig Peat** with a shovel there has about a 1 in 33 chance to turn up a Bog Body. Dig Peat still gives its six Peat every time.

- **Search the Body** (30 minutes) works once and gives a bronze nugget and two or three Bones.
- Only one body turns up per save. A save that already ran the mod can still find it.

## Climbing Aids

Three things to build where the game has a climb, from the board's Improvements tab like a vanilla road. Each raises the vanilla Climbing Aid stat while you stand on that board, so a climb there is less likely to end in a fall. Skill still counts, and the aid also helps a large tree's Climb on the same board.

- **Carved Stairs** (Climbing Aid +40) fit the eleven vanilla boards with a climb of their own: Damp Hollow, Dead Hollow, Secret Grove, Crow Pines, Mountain Grove, Wind Needles, Greenfalls, Southern Rapids, Shady Thicket, Cliff Thicket and the Pine Cliffs. They cost 8 Stone and 2 hours 30 minutes, with a mining tool (pickaxe, hammer or Stone) and a shovel held. Both tools wear a little.
- **Fairy Hole Ladder (Top)** goes on Rocky Grove and helps the climb down. 4 Wooden Planks and 2 Ropes, 1 hour 30 minutes, Climbing Aid +120.
- **Fairy Hole Ladder (Foot)** goes on The Shaft and helps the climb back up. Same materials and time, Climbing Aid +120.

Each one appears in the Improvements tab the first time you stand on one of its boards. A scripted run built each and saw the raised stat, and the stairs stayed built across a save and reload. A human check in a normal game is still pending.

The vanilla Shaft's Climb Up used to ignore Climbing Aid. The mod now adds it to that action's success odds, so a skill-0 climb goes from about 53 to about 77 percent with the ladder built. That is the mod's second edit to a vanilla card.

## Rock Shelter and the Sunken Caves

A rock overhang with a cave under it, west of the Old Hollow, reached by a new West exit. The shelter is a copy of the vanilla Pine Grove.

**Overhanging Rock.** Nap and Sleep work like the vanilla Hideout's, with +1 Rain Protection and +1 Sun Protection while you are there. It never decays.

**Cave Mouth.** **Enter the Caves** takes you down into the Painted Chamber.

Three chambers lie in a line, reachable only through the Cave Mouth and not drawn on the paper map. Each is a copy of the vanilla Waterfall Caves without its exit, shrine and flint vein.
- **Painted Chamber.** **Leave the Caves** climbs back to the shelter, and West leads to the Ossuary. **Study the Paintings** as often as you like; there is nothing to take.
- **Ossuary.** West to the Sunken Lake, East back. **Sift the Mounds** once for Bones and Bone Pieces, then they are spent.
- **Sunken Lake.** The end of the caves.

**The Squeeze.** The crawl between the Ossuary and the Sunken Lake only fits a light load. While your Encumbrance is above 1000 (eight Stones read 100), its buttons are hidden both ways and return when you carry less. A **Size Up the Squeeze** card on each side explains this. No other button in the caves is gated.

**Cave Lake.** A still lake that never freezes or floods.
- **Drink**, **Fill** (drag a water container onto it) and **Wash yourself** work like the vanilla pond's. The water is unsafe: it quenches thirst but can bring a stomach infection or parasites.
- **Fish** (drag a fishing rod or line onto it) gives Eels. **Spearfish** (drag a spear onto it) gives Eels and Crayfish, and needs light and stamina. Catch chances rise with Fishing and Spear Fishing skill.
- There is no ice, no frogs or leeches, and no fish stock to run down.
- The caves are lit like the Waterfall Caves: daylight by day, dark by night.

## Known limitations

- **Items left in cave chambers.** Before framework 2.28.0, an item left in a chamber was lost on reload. The required framework fixes that, and the fix is confirmed in another mod's cave, but not yet in these chambers.
- **Each place is seeded once per save,** when its board is first created. A save that visited Barrow Wood before its newer sites were added will not see them, and the same holds for later additions. Start a new game to get everything.
