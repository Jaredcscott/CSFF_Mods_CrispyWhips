# Herbs and Fungi: Player Guide

**Version:** 1.14.8
**Author:** Jared (crispywhips)
**For:** Card Survival: Fantasy Forest (EA 0.69c)

---

## Overview

Herbs and Fungi adds foraged mushrooms, medicinal herbs, wild fruit and vegetables, hemp farming, mushroom logs, oil pressing, pickling and drying. You forage, grow and dry, press oils, brew medicinal teas and seal pickles in clay vats.

It ships 499 cards (267 items, 27 liquids, 79 placed structures and growth stages, 126 blueprints), 16 character perks, 13 custom stats, 16 flavour spices, 3 triggered events, 2 encounter guards and a set of world-map locations.

Many numbers added since 1.14.0 are starting values and may change, and a few cards still share another card's picture. Features marked "not yet confirmed in game" have not been played through by a person.

`PLAYER_GUIDE.md` has a longer walkthrough and `CHANGELOG.md` the full change list.

### Herbs and Roots
- **Yarrow**: dry and grind it into a wound powder. Grow it with **Plant Yarrow**.
- **Wild Ginseng**: dry and grind it for Ginseng Tea. Grow it with **Plant Ginseng**.
- **Chamomile**: dry the flowers for a calming tea. Grow it with **Plant Chamomile**.
- **Dandelion**: eat it fresh or dried, or brew dried flowers into tea. Grow it with **Plant Dandelion**.
- **Ginger**: a foraged spice. Dry it, cut it into Cut Ginger, or process it.
- **Common Plantain**: dry the leaves for a Plantain Poultice. Grow it with **Plant Plantain**.
- **Wild Flowers**: dry them and make a Flower Garland.

### Garden Vegetables and Crops
- **Kale, Carrot and Leek** are found foraging from spring to autumn (4 to 5% a forage). Kale at the Foraging Path and Highland Meadow, Carrot at Alder Woods and Highland Meadow, Leek at the Foraging Path and Alder Woods.
  - Eat them raw, or drag one onto hot water to stew it.
  - Holding one unlocks **Plant Kale**, **Plant Carrot** or **Plant Leek** (Farming > Agriculture): 2 of the vegetable and a hoe, which loses 1 use.
  - Kale grows in 5 days for 3 to 4, Carrot in 8 days for 3 to 4, Leek in 10 days for 2 to 3. **Harvest** uses the plant up.
- **Hops, Grapes and Oats** are found foraging in summer and autumn (3 to 5%). Hops at Alder Woods and the Oak Clearing, Grapes at the Oak Clearing and Foraging Path, Oats at the Highland Meadow and Foraging Path.
  - **Hops** are not eaten. Drag them onto finished rye ale (**Steep in Ale**) for **Hopped Ale**: Bitter and Floral, drinks like rye ale, spoils half as fast. Fresh wort will not take them.
  - **Grapes** can be eaten. **Crush into Must** on a container of water makes Grape Must, which becomes **Grape Wine** after 4 days in any container. It needs no Fermentation Bin and does not freeze or age in a barrel.
  - **Oats** can be eaten and count as livestock feed. **Grind** them with a stone mortar, a grinding tool or Water Driven Infrastructure's Grinding Mill into **Oatmeal**, then drag it onto hot water for porridge.
  - **Plant Hops** and **Plant Grapes** take 2 of the crop and a hoe that loses 1 use. Hops give 3 to 5 after 10 days, Grapes 4 to 6 after 12 days.
    - **Oat Field** takes 6 Oats and a hoe that loses 3 uses, needs light and stamina, and gives 8 to 12 Oats after 9 days.
- **Broad Beans** are found in spring and summer at the Highland Meadow and Alder Woods (4%). Eat them or stew them. The **Broad Bean Field** (6 Broad Beans, a hoe that loses 3 uses) gives 8 to 12 after 10 days.

### Hemp
- **Hemp Seeds**: eat raw or roast them, or plant them with **Plant Hemp Seeds** and **Hemp Field**.
- **Hemp Stalks** give fiber. **Hemp Flower** is used fresh or dried.
- **Hemp Flower Powder**: ground from dried flower. You can smoke it.
- **Hemp Butter**: fat plus powder. Heat activates it. A mild dose is about three times a pipe, stronger doses more.
- **Hemp Seed Oil**: pressed from seeds in the Oil Press.

### Mushroom Log Cultivation
- **Start a log** from a Tree Log, a Metal Spoon Auger, wood shavings and 10 matching mushrooms. It works for Shiitake, Lion's Mane, Reishi, Chicken of the Woods, Golden Oyster and King Oyster.
- **It colonizes** for about 5 days as a Growing Fungal Log, then becomes the ready log.
- **A ready log** gives 20 mushrooms, then breaks down into 2 to 3 wood shavings and a piece of firewood.
- **Spore Prints**: each harvest also gives one print of that species. **Inoculate ... Log from a Spore Print** (Farming > Agriculture) starts a log from 1 print instead of 10 mushrooms.
- **Grow Bags** (Golden Oyster, King Oyster): **Pack Golden Oyster Grow Bag** and **Pack King Oyster Grow Bag** take 1 wood shavings, 5 mushrooms and 1 Small Cloth, no log or tool.
  - A bag can sit indoors, colonizes in about 2.5 days and gives 12 mushrooms and 1 to 2 shavings.

### Crafted Items and Structures
- **Drying Tray**: a 6-slot tray for drying herbs, mushrooms, hemp flower or meat. Meat, fish, organs and sausages dry at a quarter of the speed (about a day for Bird Meat).
- **Drying Stack**: furniture that holds up to 3 trays.
- **Wooden Pantry**: cool storage that slows spoilage to 75% of normal. Wild animals can break into it.
- **Apothecary Shelf**: filtered storage for herbs, fungi, powders and medicines, slowing spoilage more than the pantry. Wild Ginseng and Wild Flowers count as herbs. The Herbal Incense Bundle does not go on it.
- **Herb Pipe**: a carved wooden pipe, reusable with herbal powders.
- **Herbal Incense Bundle**: dried flowers and herbs you burn to ease stress and raise Sanctuary across your board for about three hours. You can snuff it out to keep the rest.
- **Flower Garland**: made from dried wild flowers. Hang it to ease Stress by 8.
- **Herbal Bath Soak**: dried chamomile and yarrow. In hot water in a pot it makes an **Herbal Steam** that eases stress for about half a day.
  - In a filled Copper Bathtub (Advanced Copper Tools 1.18.0 or later) it gives a soak of about a day. The soak is used up and does not wash you.
- **Death Cap Ward**: 5 Death Caps lashed to 2 long sticks, placed at a location.
  - While you are there, it turns away 45% of attacks by a bear, wolf, wolf pack, primeval wolf or boar, and 45% of other wild-animal encounters that start without a creature on the map.
  - It never stops people, a fight you start, or other mods' animals.
- **Wild Bees' Nest**: a honeycomb source at four forest locations (see World Map Locations).
- **Forage hotspots**: a Lingonberry Patch at the Oak Clearing, a Cloudberry Patch at the Pine Clearing and a Chamomile Patch at Alder Woods. **Pick** gives 1 to 2 of the plant and uses a quarter of the patch.
  - The Ripeness bar refills in about 7 days from empty, not in winter, and Pick is hidden below a quarter. Not yet confirmed in game.
- **Forager's Notebook**: 3 Hemp Paper and Twine (Support tab). Carried in your inventory, it records each wild plant or mushroom from this mod the first time one turns up, under the place you found it.
  - Open the card to read the list. Not yet confirmed in game.
- **Hemp Paper and the Botanical Field Guide**: Hemp Stalks make Hemp Pulp, which soaks for about a day and presses into Hemp Paper. 6 paper and Twine bind the Field Guide, whose **Read** action lists every plant this mod adds with its seasons and places.
- **Oil Press**: a multi-stage workstation that presses oils from seeds, truffles and herbs.
- **Pickle Vat**: a clay vessel for long preservation.

### Cooked Foods and Drinks
Blueprints are in the Cooking tab unless noted. No vanilla card changes.

- **Forager's Pasties**: drag a foraged mushroom or berry onto greased rye or wheat dough in daylight to make a raw pasty, then bake it in an oven into a **Mushroom Pasty** or **Wild Berry Pasty**.
  - Food values and flavours are copied from vanilla's rye bilberry and rye meat pasties.
- **Breads**: 2 Rye Grist and 1 Rye Dough bake into **Sprouted Rye Bread**. 1 Dried Nettle Leaves and 1 rye or wheat dough bake into **Nettle Bread**. 2 Pea Dough bake into **Pea Bread**. Food values are copied from vanilla rye flatbread.
- **Flours**: **Split Peas** (2 Dried Peas) and **Turnroot Chips** (1 Dried Turnroot) **Grind** into Pea Flour and Turnroot Flour with a stone mortar, any grinding tool or Water Driven Infrastructure's Grinding Mill.
  - Drag a flour onto a bowl or pot holding at least 150 of cool fresh water to **Soak** it into dough. Turnroot Dough bakes into **Turnroot Flatbread**. Camas Flour makes Camas Dough and **Camas Flatbread** the same way.
- **Ful**: 3 Broad Beans and 1 Peanut Oil make a bean mash you **Eat**. You get the clay bowl back.
- **Nut Milk and Nut Tofu**: drag **Peanut Meal** (2 Roasted Peanuts) onto a bowl of cool fresh water to **Soak** it into **Nut Milk**, which sours in about three days. **Nut Tofu** takes 1 Salt and a bowl holding 150 Nut Milk.
- **Tendon Soup, Fishcake and Haggis**: **Tendon Soup** is 2 Tendon, Dried Yarrow and a Clay Bowl, and you **Drink** it. **Fishcake** is Raw Fish Filling, rye or wheat flour and Dried Dandelion, cooked near a fire.
  - **Haggis** is a Clean Stomach, Liver, Heart, 2 Rye Grains and Dried Common Plantain, cooked near a fire.
- **Liver Sausage**: Liver, Raw Rich Meat Filling or Raw Minced Meat, Cleaned Intestines and Dried Yarrow make a Raw Liver Sausage.
  - Cook it near a fire, boil it in a pot, or dry it on a drying rack into the long-keeping **Dried Liver Sausage**. The raw one risks parasites.
- **Fish Sauce**: Salted Fish Mash (2 raw lean or fatty fish, 1 Salt) dragged onto 100 or more water makes Young Fish Sauce, which becomes **Fish Sauce** after about 5 days. **Add** it to a plateable food (Savoury, Salty) or **Drink** a mouthful.
- **Lutefisk**: a dried fish and a bowl of Lye soak for about 2 days. Rinse the Lye-Cured Fish in water, then cook the Rinsed Lye Fish over a fire.
- **Bark Meal**: a famine food. 2 Oak Bark (unlocked by holding it) make a meal that gives a little food and costs a little Stress and Gratification.
- **Iced Teas and Shaved Ice**: each needs a vanilla **Ice Block**, which melts in about 2 hours.
  - Drag one onto Chamomile or Dandelion Tea (**Chill with Ice**) for an iced tea whose **Drink** adds **Cooled**, which lowers Perceived Temperature for about 9 hours.
  - **Shaved Ice** (an Ice Block and a Berry Preserve, two bowls) gives a little food, water and Cooled, and melts in about 6 hours.
- **Gruit Ale**: a Gruit Bundle (Dry Bog Myrtle Leaves, Dry Heather Flowers, Dried Yarrow and Twine) steeped in finished rye ale spoils half as fast as plain ale. A Needle Bundle (2 Dry Needles and Twine) steeped the same way makes **Needle Ale**.
- **Herb-spiced wines and ales**: drop Dried Chamomile, Yarrow or Ginger on finished billberry wine, birch wine, springberry wine, rye ale or wheat ale to make a Herb-Spiced version.
- **Forager's Seasoning**: Black Trumpet Powder, Shiitake Powder, King Oyster Powder and Salt make a 3-pinch pouch. **Add** it to a plateable food.
- **Berry Leather and honeyed sweets**: four **Mash** blueprints turn 3 berries of one kind into **Berry Pulp** (spoils in about 6 days). Dried on a Drying Tray it becomes **Berry Leather**, which keeps about 75 days.
  - With Honeycomb, **Honeyed Nuts** (3 Roasted Peanuts or Roasted Pine Nuts) and **Honeyed Berries** (one of each dried berry) each make 2 sweets that keep about 90 days.
- **Wax Leather Dressing**: drag Linseed Oil onto Beeswax (you keep the clay bowl), then drag the dressing onto a leather garment such as a tunic, trousers, coat, gloves, shoes or helmet.
  - Its Quality rises by 5, up to the maximum, and the dressing is used up. Beeswax comes from honeycomb. Not yet confirmed in game.
- **Maggots**: Rotten Remains give 1 to 2 Maggots with **Collect Maggots**, except in winter. **Apply Maggots** on an infected open wound lowers its Infection by 180 and raises Stress by 15.

## Gameplay Systems

### Drying and Preservation
1. Craft a **Drying Tray** (Support tab).
2. Optionally craft a **Drying Stack** (Furniture tab), which holds 3 trays.
3. Put herbs, mushrooms, hemp flowers, ginger or meat on a tray. Meat dries slowly here, at a quarter of the herb speed; the vanilla Drying Rack is the faster way while you keep it smoking.
4. Items dry in place and their quality builds up.
5. Dried items keep longer and can be ground into medicinal powders.

The vanilla Drying Rack can also dry Tendon, and the vanilla Pouch can hold powders. The Drying Stack needs a Drying Tray on the board before you can research it.

### Medicinal Processing
1. **Dry** the herb or mushroom on a Drying Tray.
2. **Grind** it: drag a grinding tool onto it, or use the Rotary Quern.
3. **Brew** the powder in a bowl of hot water (**Mix into Hot Water**). Brewing does nothing to you. The tea's effect lands once, when you drink it.

Making medicines trains **Herbalism**: Herbal Salve, Plantain Poultice, Herbal Honey Syrup, Herbal Tincture, Healer's Moss Tincture, Death Cap Tincture, Pain Relief Powder, Sleep Tea, Stimulant Tea and Anti-Nausea Tea. So does harvesting Mature Ginseng, Flowering Yarrow, Chamomile, Plantain or Dandelion, or a ready mushroom log.

Grinding dried Black Trumpet, Chaga, Ginseng, Reishi, Hemp Flower, King Oyster, Lion's Mane, Shiitake or Yarrow gives a little too, as do harvesting a Grow Bag, building a Cress Bed and making Shaved Ice. Brewing with Mix into Hot Water does not.

### Hemp Farming
1. Find hemp seeds, then research **Plant Hemp Seeds** or **Hemp Field** (Farming > Agriculture).
2. Build on fertile ground. **Plant Hemp Seeds** takes 2 seeds and a hoe. **Hemp Field** takes 6 seeds and a hoe that loses 3 uses.
3. A plant gives 3 stalks, 3 flowers, 2 to 3 seeds, 1 to 2 Golden Oysters and sometimes a King Oyster or Morel.
   - A field gives 8 to 12 stalks, 8 to 12 flowers, 6 to 12 seeds, 1 to 6 Golden Oysters, 1 to 3 King Oysters and 1 to 2 Morels.
4. Roast the seeds, dry the flowers for powder, or press the seeds into oil.

### Mushroom Logs Step by Step
1. Research the matching **Inoculate ... Log** blueprint after finding that mushroom.
2. Craft it from 1 Tree Log, a Metal Spoon Auger, 10 matching mushrooms and 1 wood shavings.
3. The Growing Fungal Log colonizes for about 5 days.
4. Harvest the ready log for mushrooms, 2 to 3 wood shavings, a piece of firewood and one Spore Print. Harvesting trains Herbalism.

The **from a Spore Print** blueprint unlocks once you hold the print and takes 1 Tree Log, a Metal Spoon Auger, 1 print and 1 wood shavings.

### Herb and Peanut Planting
**Plant Ginseng**, **Plant Yarrow**, **Plant Chamomile**, **Plant Plantain**, **Plant Dandelion** and **Plant Peanuts** (Farming > Agriculture) unlock once you hold the herb or Raw Peanuts. Build on fertile ground with 2 of them and a hoe, which loses one use.

| Crop | Plant with | Grows for | Harvest |
|------|-----------|-----------|---------|
| Ginseng | 2 Wild Ginseng | 14 days | 3 to 4 Wild Ginseng |
| Yarrow | 2 Yarrow | 5 days | 3 to 4 Yarrow |
| Chamomile | 2 Chamomile | 5 days | 3 to 4 Chamomile |
| Common Plantain | 2 Common Plantain | 5 days | 3 to 4 Common Plantain |
| Dandelion | 2 Dandelion | 5 days | 3 to 4 Dandelion |
| Peanuts | 2 Raw Peanuts (roasted ones will not sprout) | 10 days | 3 to 5 Peanut Pods |

Harvesting uses the plant up, so replant from the harvest. It trains Herbalism for the herbs.

**Raw Peanuts** roast by themselves in a lit fire (campfire, fire pit or fireplace) into Roasted Peanuts in about 15 minutes. Left in the fire about 4 hours, they char into Charred Remains.

**Make Mossbed** (Farming > Agriculture) unlocks once you hold Healer's Moss. Build it on fertile ground from 2 Healer's Moss and 1 Moss, no tool. After 5 days it gives 3 to 4 Healer's Moss per Harvest and trains Herbalism.

Vanilla gives Healer's Moss only from foraging, so this is a camp source.

### Oil Press (Multi-Stage Build)
1. Craft the parts. **Oil Press Legs**, **Press Bed Frame** and a **Pressing Screw** (which takes a **Press Plate**) go into the **Oil Press Frame**. A **Pressing Bag** and a second Press Plate go into the **Prepared Press Bag**. You also need **Oil Press Utensils**.
2. Combine the Frame, the Utensils, the Prepared Press Bag and 15 Stone, with a hammering tool, in the **Oil Press Kit** blueprint (Furniture tab).
3. Place the kit to set up the **Oil Press**.
4. Choose a recipe (Press Hemp Seeds, Press Peanuts, Press Flax Seeds, Press Pine Nuts, Press Truffle or a herbal oil) and add the ingredients.

The **Oil Press** perk starts you with the Kit.

### Pickle Vat (Long Fermentation)
1. Craft the **Unfired Pickle Vat** and fire it in a kiln.
2. Fill the vat with water and drag salt onto it (**Make Brine**) to make Pickle Brine.
3. Choose a pickle blueprint: **Pickle Frogs**, **Pickle Meat**, **Pickle Mushrooms** or **Pickle Vegetables**. Each uses 500 Pickle Brine, 5 of its raw food and a Clay Bowl as the lid.
   - Frogs: raw frog.
   - Meat: raw meat, offal, fillings and raw fish.
   - Mushrooms: Golden Oyster, King Oyster, Morel Mushroom and Shiitake Mushroom.
   - Vegetables: Ginger, Wild Ginseng and vanilla fireroot and turnroot.
4. The vat becomes, for example, **Pickled Frogs (Closed)**. Drag water onto it (**Seal**). **Uncap** asks before it throws away the packed food.
5. After 7 days it becomes **Pickled Frogs** (or Meat, Mushrooms or Vegetables) with **5 servings**. **Eat** them from the vat.
6. Emptying the last serving, or letting the pickles spoil, gives back the fired vat and an **Open Pickle Jar**. **Return Bowl** on the jar returns the clay bowl lid.

**Spiced pickles.** Drag **Dried Wild Garlic** or **Dried Ginger** onto a vat of plain brine (**Spice Brine**) to make **Spiced Pickle Brine**. Four **Spiced Pickle** blueprints then appear. They accept only spiced brine, and the plain ones only plain brine.

Spiced pickles taste stronger (Pungent Strong, Savoury Medium). Servings, spoilage and returns match the plain ones.

**Watercress and the Cress Bed.** Foraging at a riverside location from spring to autumn has a 10% chance of 1 to 2 **Watercress**. Riverside means the vanilla River locations, Misty Falls and, with Community Mod Chest, Clay Flats.

Eat it raw or cook it. The **Cress Bed** blueprint (Farming > Agriculture, 4 fresh Watercress) can only be built at a river location.

It regrows about every 3 days, faster in spring and not in winter, and Harvest by hand, scythe or sickle gives 2 to 4 Watercress.

**Fungal Bloom.** Rarely, after a wet spell in autumn, the forest fruits all at once.

Once autumn is running and the swamp water stat has passed 300 (a few hours of moderate rain), each wet spell has a one in three chance to start it.

For about 2 days, the first arrival at each of the six clearings leaves a **Fungal Bloom** patch there.
- Harvest it for 3 to 4 Chanterelles, 2 to 3 Black Trumpets, 2 to 3 King Oysters, 1 to 2 Golden Oysters and 1 to 2 Morels. It trains Herbalism and is used up.
- An unharvested patch rots after about 3 days. Only one stands on a board at a time.

### Cooking
- Put raw mushrooms on any heat source (campfire, stove or copper stove). Watch the cook stat and take them off before they overcook. Cooked mushrooms are more nourishing.
- Morel Mushroom and Chicken of the Woods give a short bout of food poisoning raw, and the morel adds nausea, so cook them. An Unknown or False Chanterelle eaten as it is does the same. Identify the unknown one first.
- **Pasties**: drag a culinary mushroom (not Reishi, Chaga, Death Cap or Truffle) or a berry onto greased rye or wheat dough in daylight, then bake it in an oven (heat gauge 178 or more, one cooking step). Take it out before it burns.
- **Stew**: 92 cards from this mod have **Add to Stew**. They cover fresh, cooked, dried and ground forms of the mushrooms, berries, herbs, roots and vegetables, plus the powders, Oatmeal, Truffle Butter and Truffle Salt.
  - Drop one onto a Cooking Pot, Clay Cauldron, Clay Bowl or Advanced Copper Tools' Copper Cauldron holding at least 290 units of water at Liquid Temperature 100 or more and not yet at full thickness.
  - It is stirred in at once, and the stew takes on the card's flavours.

  Per 300 units of water, the stew gains the numbers of vanilla's nearest ingredient:

  | Ingredient | Vanilla match | Thickness | Calories | Other |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Mushroom (also Camas, Fiddleheads, Water Lily, Watercress, Truffle Salt) | Forest Caps | 16.66 | 10 | 24 Vitamins |
  | Berry or wild fruit (also crabapples, wild pears, wild plums, elderberries) | bilberry | 25 | 26 | 96 Vitamins |
  | Leafy herb or vegetable (chamomile, dandelion, yarrow, elderflowers, carrot, kale, leek, rhubarb, broad beans, goosefoot, thistle, turnroot greens) | bog myrtle | 33.3 | 2 | 44 Vitamins |
  | Oatmeal | | 33.3 | 2 | 60 Vitamins |
  | Root (ginger, ginseng) | fireroot | 33.3 | 10 | 24 Vitamins |
  | Truffle Butter | vanilla fat | 6.66 | 12 | 48 Proteins |
  | Ground powder | its plant | | | |

  Cold, too thick or too shallow water says "Not hot enough", "Too thick" or "Not enough liquid". Reishi, Lion's Mane, Chaga and Ginseng (every form) also add Herbal Tonic, and Yarrow adds Herbal Analgesic and Clotting.

  Each adds +8 per full 300-unit portion, less than a tea's dose, and a stew holds one dose of each kind.

### Truffle Condiments
- **Cooked Truffle**: **Coat with Fat** on **Dried Truffle Slices** (drag Fat, a Fat Chunk, a Butter Chunk or Butter onto them) makes Fat-Coated Dried Truffle Slices, which finish as a Cooked Truffle when heated. Uncoated slices char to ash.
- **Truffle Butter**: drag Fat, a Fat Chunk, a Butter Chunk or Butter onto a fresh **Truffle**. The fat is used up (Strong Earthy, Strong Savoury, Medium Buttery). Eat it or add it to a stew. It keeps about a week.
- **Truffle Salt**: drag vanilla **Salt** onto a whole **Dried Truffle** and grind them together, no tool. It is a finishing salt that never spoils (Strong Earthy, Strong Savoury, Subtle Salty). Add it to a stew.
  - Its flavour, no-spoilage and stew use are not yet confirmed in game.
- **Herb Butter and Herb Cheese**: drag Butter or a Butter Chunk onto Black Trumpet Powder, Shiitake Powder, King Oyster Powder or Dried Ginger for Herb Butter.
  - Drag Soft, Firm, Hard or Aged Cheese or Cheese Curds onto the same four for Herb Cheese. The dairy is used up.
  - It is plain food, keeps far longer than plain dairy, and with Sirus23's Mod Collection its fats and Sheep Cheese also work. Not yet confirmed in game.

## World Map Locations

Herbs and Fungi adds six forest locations west of Primeval Woods, and a seventh, the Old-Growth Grove, that opens once you have explored all six.

| Location | Access | Description |
|----------|--------|-------------|
| **Foraging Path** | West from Primeval Woods, behind an Overgrown Forest Trail | The hub that connects the other clearings |
| **Pine Clearing** | North from Foraging Path | Pine forest with pine mushrooms and a Cloudberry Patch |
| **Highland Meadow** | North from Pine Clearing | Pine meadow with ginseng, ginger, morels, chanterelles, shiitake, black trumpets and blackcurrants |
| **Oak Clearing** | West from Foraging Path | Oak forest with oak mushrooms and a Lingonberry Patch |
| **Misty Falls** | North from Oak Clearing | A waterfall grove at the west edge of the forest. A hidden path leads back to Waterfall Caves |
| **Alder Woods** | South from Foraging Path | Alder forest with alder and birch mushrooms and berries |
| **Old-Growth Grove** | West from Misty Falls, once all six are explored | An old oak grove like the vanilla Old Grove, with common truffles, morels and cloudberries |

An **Overgrown Forest Trail** blocks the foot route from Primeval Woods for every character. **Hack Through** with a blade, axe, shovel or antler, from either side, opens it. It grows back after about 10 days. A portal reaches the forest directly.

**Old-Growth Grove.** A clearing counts as explored once you have arrived and then left, so the path opens just after you leave the last new one.

Foraging there can turn up a **Truffle** (6%, spring to autumn), a **Morel Mushroom** (12%, all year), **Cloudberries** (12%, spring to autumn) and a **Mandrake Root** (4%, summer and autumn), on top of the vanilla Old Grove finds.

Each location copies a vanilla forest, with its usual trees and forage. With Water Driven Infrastructure installed, its mill race network includes them.

The Foraging Path, Pine Clearing, Oak Clearing and Alder Woods each hold a **Wild Bees' Nest**, the source of Honeycomb.

Drag a Lit Torch onto it to calm the bees, then **Take the Comb** for 1 to 2 Honeycomb without stings. A lit Bee Smoker also works, though nothing in this game version makes one.

Taking the comb unsmoked stings you. The comb grows back in about 7 days, not in winter.

## Allied Partners Eat Herbs and Fungi Food

An allied Partner, the companion a Partner perk gives you, eats this mod's food and drinks its plain drinks as it does vanilla food. Hand it the card or leave it where it can reach it.
- When hungry it will **Eat** any of 107 foods: berries, cooked and dried mushrooms, vegetables, breads, pasties, sausages, oils, butters and other dishes.
- When thirsty it will **Drink** any of 18 drinks: Chamomile and Dandelion Tea, the Chanterelle, Lion's Mane and Mushroom Broths, Tendon Soup, Nut Milk, Elderflower Cordial, and the ales and wines (Gruit, Hopped and Needle Ale, Grape Wine, Sloe Wine and the five herb-spiced ales and wines).

This uses vanilla's own Partner duties, so it works with or without Partner Overhaul. 25 foods and 7 drinks gained the human food or drink tag for it.

The only container of this mod that reads those tags is the Wooden Pantry, which now also accepts Oats.

**A Partner leaves alone:**
- medicines: Death Cap Tincture, Healer's Moss Tincture, Herbal Tincture and the Mandrake Draught;
- anything that gives or cures food poisoning, nausea, parasites or a systemic infection: Death Cap, raw Morel Mushroom, raw Chicken of the Woods, raw Fiddleheads, the Unknown and False Chanterelles, Raw Liver Sausage, Wild and Dried Ginseng, the Yarrow cards and Anti-Nausea Tea;
- anything that gives a timed effect (herbal tonic, pain relief, clotting, cooling, soak, toxin tolerance, Chicory Lift, Goosefoot Purge): the medicinal powders, Reishi, Lion's Mane and Shiitake dishes, medicinal teas, Herbal Ferment, Vigor Elixir, Bowl of Truffle Oil, Shaved Ice, the iced teas, Chicory Brew and Goosefoot Greens;
- Sleep Tea and Stimulant Tea;
- Fish Sauce, which does not quench thirst;
- the ready pickle vats, which are placed cards a Partner does not reach.

## Foraging Integration

The mod adds its herbs, mushrooms and berries to Forage at vanilla forest locations:
- oak and alder groves, thickets and clearings;
- pine forests and meadows;
- river banks, the two willow groves (Willow Swamp and Willow Ways) and the northern lake shore;
- Primeval Woods, Lake Island, the Lost Woods, Green Grove, Green Glade and Oaken Grove.

Other changes:
- Primeval Woods' Clear can also turn up Hemp Seeds.
- Digging up mud at a river rarely turns up a Truffle, spring to autumn.
- The 51 vanilla oak and alder locations get **Dig for Truffles**, with a 25% chance of a Truffle all year.
- The caves at Old Hollow, Shady Thicket and Still Hollow have no Forage action, so nothing is added inside them. The forest locations of those names do get forage. The mod's own locations have no Dig for Truffles.

Berries follow their biomes: Blackcurrants and Redcurrants in oak and birch areas, Lingonberries in pine areas, and Cloudberries, rarely, in northern pine meadows.

"Birch areas" are the 17 vanilla forest spots with a large birch tree, where Chaga also turns up. There Blackcurrants and Chanterelle drop at all 17, Chamomile at 16, Common Plantain at 13 and Redcurrants at 12, each in its own seasons.

At the mod's own forage spots, a few cards are twice as likely in their best season: Wild Flowers and Common Plantain in spring at the Foraging Path and Alder Woods, and Chanterelle and Black Trumpet in autumn at the Highland Meadow.

This needs CSFFModFramework 2.28.0. It and seasonal forage at the mod's own locations are not yet confirmed in game.

## Technical Information

### Requirements
- **BepInEx 5.x**
- **CSFFModFramework** 2.28.0 or newer
- **Card Survival: Fantasy Forest** EA 0.69c

### Installation
1. Install BepInEx 5.x.
2. Install CSFFModFramework to `BepInEx/plugins/CSFF_Mod_Framework/`.
3. Extract this mod to `BepInEx/plugins/Herbs_And_Fungi/`.
4. Launch the game.

### Configuration
One option, in `BepInEx/config/` after the first launch:
- **`[Performance]` `ForageDropDensityScale`** (default `1.0`) multiplies every forage and dig drop chance this mod adds. 0.25 to 1.0 is the recommended range; lower it if forage feels crowded. Above 1.0 makes finds more common, but no chance goes past 100%.
  - A `.cfg` edit needs a full game restart.

### Compatibility
- Works alongside other CSFF mods, such as Advanced Copper Tools, Water Driven Infrastructure and Repeat Action.
- Safe to add to existing saves.
- Removing it mid-save makes this mod's items in the world disappear on load.
- **Community Mod Chest:** fresh Chamomile, Dandelion, Wild Flowers, Elderflowers and Hemp Flower, the Flower Garland and the Herbal Incense Bundle count as flowers and fill CMC's Painted Clay Vase (Arrange Flowers). Dried herbs do not.
- **Advanced Copper Tools:** its Render Hemp Seed Oil blueprint uses this mod's Hemp Seed Oil, and ACT hides that blueprint when Herbs and Fungi is not installed. Neither mod needs the other.

### For Modders
Three patches ship in `GameSourceModify/`:
- vanilla **Rotten Remains** gains Collect Maggots;
- the **Clay Cauldron** gains the medicine-brew-station tag that the Vigor Elixir reads;
- vanilla **Honeycomb** (`33cf4067563dac649b026a87ba0766bd.json`) gains the **Stir into Tea to Ferment** interaction behind Herbal Ferment.

The mod adds forage and dig actions to vanilla locations and tags vanilla Turnroot and Fireroot `tag_Fermentable`. `GameLoadPatch` also gives the vanilla Drying Rack a Tendon drying recipe, lets the vanilla Pouch hold powders, and adds Lanolin as an alternate Herbal Salve ingredient.

Most vanilla locations are named by ID in `DropInjections.json` (river places match `tag_River`), so a location a game update adds gets nothing until it has an entry.

The companion remedies carry `tag_x_AnimalRemedy`, every pressed oil carries `tag_Oil`, and the Herbal Bath Soak reads Advanced Copper Tools' `tag_x_Bathtub`.

Patch classes in `Patcher/`:
- **GameLoadPatch** adds Dig for Truffles at the 51 vanilla oak and alder locations (a `DropInjections.json` rule cannot create an action, and every forage drop is a rule). It also applies `ForageDropDensityScale`, tags Turnroot and Fireroot as fermentable, and fills the pickle-vat content tags.
- **PickleVatRoutePatch** handles the in-place liquid swaps for Make Brine and Spice Brine, and the brine requirement on the pickle blueprints.
- **ApothecaryQuestGatePatch** unlocks the Stimulant Tea and Anti-Nausea Tea blueprints separately, when you obtain Ground Ginseng or Dried Ginger, instead of through Community Mod Chest's Apothecary quest.
- **GpTagContentPatch** fills the pickle-vat `GpTag` content, called from `GameLoadPatch`.
- **ForagersNotebookPatch** listens for newly created wild finds from this mod and rewrites the carried Forager's Notebook's saved description so each species is listed once, under the place it was first found (at most 12 place lines).
  - If the saved description cannot be read, it stops updating and logs one warning.
- **AcademyHerbalismBridgePatch** sets the hidden `herbs_fungi_stat_academy_herbalism` stat to 1 while the player holds Community Mod Chest's Herbalism graduate perk, and 0 otherwise, so Identify (Academy training) can appear without this mod naming a CMC card. Without CMC the stat stays 0.
