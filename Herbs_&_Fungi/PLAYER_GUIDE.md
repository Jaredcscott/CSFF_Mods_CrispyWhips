# Herbs and Fungi — Player Guide

A complete how-to for every item, plant, and structure in the mod.

---

## Getting Started

Most content is foraged — start exploring forest biomes (oak, alder and pine woods, meadows, river banks and the mod's own clearings) to discover mushrooms and herbs. The caves have no forage, so they hold none of this mod's finds. Build a **Drying Tray** early (Support crafting tab) so nothing rots before you can process it.

**Suggested early priorities:**
1. Find Yarrow → dry → grind → Yarrow Tea (free wound treatment)
2. Build a Drying Tray and collect herbs
3. Plant Hemp Seeds when you have fertile soil
4. Research Oil Press once you find Oil Press Utensils

## Mushrooms

All foraged mushrooms are found in forests. Most spawn on the surface; Truffles must be dug up.

### Edible Mushrooms

| Mushroom | How to Eat | Notes |
|----------|-----------|-------|
| **Chanterelle** | Raw or cooked | Golden funnel-shaped; common, safe raw |
| **Giant Puffball** | Raw or cooked | Large white sphere; very filling |
| **King Oyster** | Raw or cooked | Dense and meaty; high protein |
| **Golden Oyster** | Raw or cooked | Nutty cashew-like flavor |
| **Velvet Shank** | Raw or cooked | Winter only, from foraging oak, alder and willow woods. Mild and slippery; shares the Golden Oyster pictures, not checked in game |
| **Shiitake** | Raw or cooked | Immune-boosting; best cooked |
| **Chicken of the Woods** | **Must cook** | Toxic raw (short food poisoning); cooked it is safe and feeds you much better |
| **Morel** | **Must cook** | Toxic raw (short food poisoning and nausea); always cook before eating |
| **Black Trumpet** | Raw or cooked | Rare, gourmet smoky flavor |

**Cooking mushrooms:** Place a fresh mushroom on any lit fire (campfire, stove, copper stove) and watch the cook progress. Remove when done. Cooked versions are safer to eat and often more nutritious.

**Unknown Chanterelle:** about a quarter of the chanterelles you forage turn up as Unknown Chanterelles, which may be the real thing or a False Chanterelle look-alike. Identify one before eating it, in any of three ways: **Identify (Herbalism)** once your Herbalism skill is 25 or more; **Identify (Field Guide)** by dragging a Botanical Field Guide onto it (the guide is kept); or, **only with Community Mod Chest installed**, **Identify (Academy training)**, which appears once you have graduated the Academy's Herbalism course. Each takes 15 minutes and turns it into a Chanterelle (3 times in 4) or a False Chanterelle (1 time in 4). **Eat Unidentified** fills you a little but gives food poisoning, and a False Chanterelle is not food at all (**Eat Anyway** gives food poisoning too). The share, the skill threshold and the odds are placeholder starting values, not tuned; both cards share the Chanterelle picture; not checked in game.

**Ripe Puffball:** a Giant Puffball left uneaten until its spoilage runs out ripens into a Ripe Puffball instead of rotting. You cannot eat it. Use its **Dust a Wound** action (1 daytime) to raise Yarrow Clotting by 24, half of what Ground Yarrow's Apply to Wound gives, so bleeding slows for a while. Each puffball holds 3 dustings, and a ripe one rots away after twice as long as the fresh one takes to ripen. These numbers are starting values, not tuned.

### Medicinal Mushrooms

These are primarily used for grinding and brewing rather than eating directly.

**Lion's Mane**
- Chew raw for a mild boost, or dry → grind → brew into **Lion's Mane Tea** for a cognitive boost.
- Found in forests; relatively uncommon.

**Reishi**
- Bitter taste raw. Best dried → ground → brewed into **Reishi Tea** for immune support and stress relief.
- Long spoilage time — one of the few mushrooms you don't need to rush to process.

### Truffle

The rarest and most valuable mushroom.

- Found underground: at a vanilla **oak or alder** location (oak and alder groves, thickets and clearings) use the location's **Dig for Truffles** action. The mod's own locations do not have it. Digging up mud at a river can also turn one up, rarely, from spring to autumn. Foraging at the **Old-Growth Grove** (west of Misty Falls; it opens once all six of the mod's clearings have been explored) finds one 6% of the time from spring to autumn, a placeholder chance.
- Spoils in 1 day (fastest of any mushroom) — dry immediately or use right away.
- Eat raw for a huge hunger boost, or dry → slice → cook with fat (see Truffle Cooking below).
- Press in the Oil Press to produce **Truffle Oil** — a luxury cooking ingredient.

**Truffle Cooking:** When you heat Dried Truffle Slices alongside **Fat**, **Fat Chunks**, **Butter Chunks**, or **Milk Butter** in the same cooking container, the truffle consumes one fat and transforms into a **Cooked Truffle** instead of burning to ash. Without fat in the same container, dried truffle slices will char.

### Death Cap

**Do not eat.** Eating even one Death Cap will cause severe poisoning and is likely fatal.

**Safe uses:**
- **Death Cap Tincture** (craft at a kiln) — a bitter amatoxin extract that kills gut parasites when consumed in small doses.
- **Dried Death Cap** (dry a Death Cap on the Drying Tray): **Taste a Sliver** gives a short, mild bout of food poisoning (+4) and builds **Toxin Tolerance**, which makes later food poisoning (raw Morel, raw Chicken of the Woods, an unidentified Chanterelle) fade faster for about two days. It does not stop the poisoning itself, and each sliver uses up the dried cap. The numbers are starting values, not tuned; the card uses the fresh Death Cap picture; not checked in game.
- **Poison a weapon** (Apply Poison): drag a Death Cap onto a spear, a cutting tool, an axe or a stack of arrows (or the weapon onto the cap) to coat it with the game's own poison. Handling the cap gives you a little food poisoning.
- **Death Cap Ward** (Support tab, once you hold a Death Cap): lash 5 Death Caps to 2 long sticks and Place the ward where you camp. While it stands where you are, a bear, wolf, wolf pack, primeval wolf or boar that attacks you is turned away 45 percent of the time, and so are other wild-animal encounters that start without a creature on the map. It never keeps people away, never stops a fight you start, and does not reach the Forest Beast, the Moontouched or other mods' animals. Pick it up to move it; Dismantle gives the sticks back. The 45 percent is a starting value, not tuned, and it has not been checked in game.

Handle them like tools, not food.

## Herbs & Roots

### Yarrow

The primary wound-treatment herb. Found in meadows and forest edges.

**Spoilage:** ~3 in-game days fresh → 60 days dried.

1. Place fresh Yarrow on a **Drying Tray** until fully dried (~8 dry ticks).
2. Drag a grinding tool (or use a Rotary Quern / Tea Blending Station) onto the dried yarrow → **Ground Yarrow**.
3. Options:
   - **Eat** the powder directly for wound-healing effects.
   - **Apply to Wound** action (3 daytime) — reduces infection and bleeding on open wounds.
   - **Brew into Yarrow Tea** — drag ground yarrow onto a hot bowl of water (50%+ heat) to create medicinal tea.

### Ginseng

An energy and stamina herb. Found in shaded forest areas.

**Spoilage:** ~5 in-game days fresh → 60 days dried. Lasts longer than other herbs.

1. Dry on a Drying Tray (~16 dry ticks — takes longer than yarrow).
2. Grind into **Ground Ginseng**.
3. Options:
   - **Eat** for a significant energy/stamina boost.
   - **Brew into Ginseng Tea** — drag ground powder into a hot bowl of water.

### Wild Ginger

A spice and food ingredient.

**Spoilage:** ~1 day fresh — dry quickly.

- **Fresh:** Eat for hunger + warming effect.
- **Sliced:** Cut fresh ginger to create slices for recipes.
- **Dried:** Greatly extends shelf life. Good for trading and long-term storage.

### Fiddleheads, Thistle Greens and Camas

Three more wild foods for the mod's own clearings. Forage chances, food values and spoilage are tunable placeholders, Fiddleheads have their own picture and the others share vanilla or existing ones, and none of this has been checked in game yet.

- **Fiddleheads** (spring only, Alder Woods and the Oak Clearing): curled fern shoots. **Eat Raw** gives a short bout of food poisoning (+8, the raw-morel dose); cook them by a fire into **Cooked Fiddleheads**, which are safe to **Eat**. Either can go into a stew.
- **Thistle Greens** (spring and summer, the Foraging Path and the Highland Meadow): peeled thistle stalks you **Eat** raw or add to a stew.
- **Camas Bulb** (spring and summer, the Highland Meadow and the Foraging Path): cannot be eaten raw. Roast it by a fire into **Roasted Camas** (**Eat**, or add to a stew). Holding Roasted Camas unlocks the **Camas Cake** blueprint (Cooking tab, 2 Roasted Camas). **Grind** the cake with a stone mortar or any grinding tool (or Water Driven Infrastructure's Grinding Mill) into **Camas Flour**, drag the flour onto a bowl or pot holding at least 150 of cool fresh water to **Soak** it into **Camas Dough**, and bake the dough near a fire into **Camas Flatbread** (**Eat**).

### Mandrake, Rhubarb and Mustard

Three more wild finds for the mod's own clearings. Forage chances, doses, food values and spoilage are tunable placeholders, Mandrake Root, Rhubarb and Mustard Seeds have their own pictures, and none of this has been checked in game yet.

- **Mandrake Root** (summer and autumn, the Oak Clearing and the Old-Growth Grove): a forked root, not food. Drag a bowl of hot water onto it (**Steep in Hot Water**) to make a **Mandrake Draught**. Its **Drink** gives the same soporific effect as vanilla Fairy Tea: stress and wakefulness fall while it lasts, and it fades over about a day. It is a medicine, so an allied Partner never drinks it.
- **Rhubarb** (spring and summer, Alder Woods and the Foraging Path): sour red stalks you **Eat** raw or add to a stew. With 2 Honeycomb, 3 Rhubarb make a **Rhubarb Preserve** (Cooking tab, unlocks once you hold Rhubarb and Honeycomb) that keeps for weeks and is eaten like the Berry Preserve. **Plant Rhubarb** (Farming > Agriculture, 2 Rhubarb and a hoe that loses one use) grows a patch on fertile ground that you **Harvest** for 3 to 4 stalks after 5 days.
- **Mustard Seeds** (summer and autumn, the Highland Meadow and the Foraging Path): not food. Drag them onto a plateable food such as cooked meat, fish or eggs (**Add**) to season it, pungent with a bitter edge. A handful seasons two plates, then it is used up.

### Horseradish, Chicory and Goosefoot

Three more wild finds for the mod's own clearings. Forage chances, doses, effect rates, food values and spoilage are tunable placeholders, Sliced Horseradish and Chicory Root have their own pictures and the others share vanilla or existing ones, and none of this has been checked in game yet.

- **Horseradish Root** (spring and autumn, the Pine Clearing and Alder Woods): a fierce, pale root, not food. **Cut** it with a knife into **Sliced Horseradish**. Drag the slices onto a plateable food such as cooked meat, fish or eggs (**Add**) to season it with Horseradish, sharply pungent with a little heat; that uses the slices up. Left alone the slices dry (slowly by themselves, quickly on a Drying Tray) into **Dried Horseradish**, which keeps and seasons three plates.
- **Chicory Root** (summer and autumn, the Highland Meadow and the Foraging Path): a bitter taproot, not food. Roast it by a fire into **Roasted Chicory**, which keeps well, then drag a bowl of hot water onto it (**Steep in Hot Water**) for **Chicory Brew**. Its **Drink** quenches thirst and gives **Chicory Lift** for a few hours: you tire more slowly, and stress rises a little faster. A second cup soon after makes you **Jittery** (both effects doubled). Allied Partners leave it alone.
- **Goosefoot Greens** (spring and summer, the Foraging Path and the Oak Clearing): mild, spinach-like leaves you **Eat** raw or add to a stew. Eating them gives **Goosefoot Purge**, which slowly lowers gut Parasites (5 a tick, 10 after a second helping close together) and wears off over about a day. It is the gentle path next to Death Cap Tincture: slower, but with no nausea or food poisoning. A stew made with goosefoot gives food only, not the purge. Allied Partners leave it alone.
- **Water Lily Rhizome** (spring to autumn, at the river places): a starchy rootstock, not food raw. Cook it by a fire into a **Cooked Water Lily Rhizome** you **Eat**, or add either to a stew.
- **Turnroot Greens**: harvest a mature turnroot field by hand and about half the time you also get a handful of greens (spring to autumn; the sickle harvest does not give them). **Eat** them raw or add them to a stew.
- **Crabapples** (autumn, the Foraging Path and the Oak Clearing), **Wild Pears** (summer and autumn, Alder Woods and the Oak Clearing) and **Wild Plums** (autumn, the Foraging Path and the Pine Clearing): eat them fresh, let them dry into a dried form that keeps much longer, or add them to a stew. Cook 3 Wild Plums with 2 Honeycomb into a **Plum Preserve** (Cooking tab).
- **Chestnuts** (autumn, the Foraging Path and the Oak Clearing) and **Hazelnuts** (autumn, the Foraging Path and the Alder Woods): eat them raw or roast them on a fire. Grind a roasted chestnut with any grinding tool into Chestnut Flour, soak it in water for Chestnut Dough, and bake the dough near a fire into Chestnut Flatbread. **Sloes** (autumn, the Foraging Path and the Highland Meadow): drag them onto a container of water to crush them into Sloe Must, which turns into Sloe Wine after 4 days. **Elderflowers** (spring and summer) and **Elderberries** (autumn), at the Foraging Path and the Alder Woods: dry the flowers, then steep them in hot water for an Elderflower Cordial; eat, dry or stew the berries.

## Drying & Preservation

### Drying Tray

Crafted from the **Support** tab. Holds 6 items simultaneously.

1. Place fresh herbs, mushrooms, hemp flowers, ginger, or meat on the tray.
2. The tray auto-ticks — each item dries over time.
3. When an item reaches its dry threshold, it transforms into its dried form (Dried Yarrow, Dried Shiitake, etc.).

**Tip:** Dry quickly — most fresh mushrooms spoil within 2–3 days. A Drying Tray can save items you can't process immediately.

### Drying Stack

Craft from the **Furniture** tab. A placed structure that holds **up to 3 Drying Trays** vertically.

- Place the stack indoors or outdoors.
- Slot 3 trays into it for 18 drying slots total.
- Dismantle when empty to recover 4 Planks.

### Wooden Pantry

A sealed cool-storage cabinet that slows spoilage to **75% of normal**. Built from the Furniture tab; place it indoors.

- Not animal-safe — foxes, badgers, and other wildlife can raid it.
- Best for storing dried herbs, preserved foods, and anything with a moderate shelf life.

### Apothecary Shelf

A narrow slatted shelf that takes **only** herbs, fungi, powders and medicines, and slows their
spoilage to **60% of normal**, better than the pantry, over a much narrower range. Built from the
Furniture tab once you have dried yarrow: 3 Planks, 2 Twine and a hammer.

- Ordinary food, tools and building materials are refused outright.
- Wild Ginseng and Wild Flowers, fresh and dried, count as herbs and go on the shelf. The Herbal Incense Bundle does not (a bundle already on a shelf stays there), and neither does Wild Ginger, which is a spice.
- Cheaper than the Wooden Pantry and holds less, so it complements the pantry rather than replacing
  it: put your materia medica here and your food there.
- Dismantle when empty to recover 2 Planks.

## Hemp Farming

Hemp provides fiber (from stalks), food (from seeds), smokeable material (from flowers), cooking fat (as butter), and press oil.

### Getting Seeds

- Forage in meadows and forest edges.
- Harvest from a mature Hemp Plant (yields more seeds than you planted).

### Planting

1. Research **Hemp Field** or **Plant Hemp Seeds** blueprint (Farming Agriculture tab) after finding seeds.
2. Build it on fertile ground with 2 seeds and a hoe (the hoe loses one use). The blueprint is the only way to plant hemp.
3. The same way, **Plant Ginseng** (2 Wild Ginseng, 14 days), **Plant Yarrow** (2 Yarrow, 5 days), **Plant Chamomile** (2 Chamomile, 5 days), **Plant Plantain** (2 Common Plantain, 5 days), **Plant Dandelion** (2 Dandelion, 5 days), **Plant Rhubarb** (2 Rhubarb, 5 days) and **Plant Peanuts** (2 Raw Peanuts, 10 days) grow the herbs and peanut pods at home. **Make Mossbed** (2 Healer's Moss and 1 Fresh Moss, 5 days) grows Healer's Moss the same way (placeholder timing and art). **Cress Bed** (4 fresh Watercress, buildable only at a river) grows Watercress, which you can also forage at riversides from spring to autumn (placeholder values and art, not checked in game).

### Harvesting

When the plant or field matures, harvest:
- **Hemp Flower** — dry, then grind for smokeable powder
- **Hemp Stalks** — use the **Extract Fibers** action to get plant fiber
- **Hemp Seeds** — replant or press into oil

### Hemp Processing Paths

| Input | Process | Output |
|-------|---------|--------|
| Hemp Flower | Dry on tray | Hemp Flower Dried |
| Hemp Flower Dried | Grind | Hemp Flower Powder |
| Hemp Flower Powder | Load into Herb Pipe | Herb Pipe (Hemp) |
| Hemp Flower Powder + Fat | Cook together | Hemp Butter |
| Hemp Seeds | Oil Press | Hemp Seed Oil |
| Hemp Stalks | Extract Fibers | Plant Fiber (×4) |

## Herb Pipe

A reusable carved wooden pipe for inhaling herbal powders.

**Loading the pipe:** Drag a herbal powder onto the pipe to load it. Powders and their effects:

| Powder | Mood | Relaxation | Notes |
|--------|:----:|:----------:|-------|
| **Hemp Flower Powder** | Moderate | Moderate | Balanced |
| **Fairyweed Powder** | High | Moderate | Best mood boost |
| **Appleweed Powder** | Moderate | Moderate-High | Balanced |
| **Frostleaf Powder** | Moderate | High | Best relaxation |

**Using the pipe:** Drag a fire source (any lit flame) onto the loaded pipe to smoke it. Quality degrades with each use — a fresh pipe holds 3 uses.

**Empty:** Use the "Empty Pipe" action to recover the unloaded pipe and discard leftover powder.

## Herbal Incense Bundle

Where the pipe is a personal dose, incense fills the room. Bind **2 Dried Wild Flowers, 1 Dried
Chamomile, 1 Dried Yarrow and 1 Twine** from the Support tab, unlocked once you have dried wild
flowers.

**Lighting it:** Drag a fire or any lit flame onto the bundle.

**While it smoulders:** Stress falls faster and the room reads as more sheltered. The effect comes
from the card sitting on your board, so it works whether the bundle is in your hands or set down
nearby, but not from another location.

**Burn time:** 12 units, about three hours, then the bundle is consumed for good. Use **Snuff Out**
to pinch it out and keep whatever is left for another evening.

## Mushroom Log Cultivation

Grow your own mushrooms indoors from inoculated logs.

**Supported species:** Shiitake, Lion's Mane, Reishi, Chicken of the Woods, Golden Oyster, King Oyster.

**Process:**
1. Find at least 5 of the target mushroom type (you need them to inoculate the log).
2. Research the matching **Inoculate Mushroom Log** blueprint (Farming Agriculture tab). Blueprint becomes researchable after you possess the mushroom type.
3. Craft from: 1 log + 1 spoon auger + 5 matching mushrooms + 1 wood shavings.
4. The log becomes a **Growing Fungal Log** — keep it indoors or sheltered while it colonizes (about 5 in-game days).
5. Harvest the ready log for fresh mushrooms and one Spore Print of that species. Harvesting also trains Herbalism.
6. Optional: research the matching **Inoculate ... Log from a Spore Print** blueprint (it unlocks once you hold the print) and craft a new log from 1 log + 1 spoon auger + 1 spore print + 1 wood shavings. The 10-mushroom recipe still works.
7. Golden Oyster and King Oyster also have a **Pack ... Grow Bag** blueprint: 1 wood shavings + 5 matching mushrooms + 1 Small Cloth, no log or tool, and it can be set down indoors. It colonizes in about 2.5 days and Harvest gives 12 mushrooms and 1 to 2 wood shavings back. These numbers are untuned starting values and the bags are not yet checked in game.

**Tip:** Inoculated logs are an excellent source of medicinal mushrooms (Reishi, Lion's Mane) that are harder to find in the wild.

## Oil Press

A multi-stage heavy workstation for pressing oils from seeds, truffles, and herbal plants.

### Building the Oil Press

The press is built in parts — each component has its own blueprint:

| Component | Where to Research |
|-----------|-----------------|
| Oil Press Legs | Farming Materials tab |
| Oil Press Frame | Farming Materials tab |
| Press Bed Frame | Farming Materials tab |
| Pressing Screw | Farming Materials tab |
| Pressing Bag | Farming Materials tab |
| Press Plate | Farming Materials tab |
| Oil Press Utensils | Farming Materials tab |
| Prepared Press Bag | Farming Materials tab |

Once all components are in hand, research and build the **Oil Press Kit** (Furniture tab), then place it to deploy the structure.

**Shortcut:** The **Oil Press** character-creation perk starts you with a Kit — skips the entire build chain.

### Oil Press Recipes

Drag the ingredient onto the placed Oil Press to see and start the recipe:

| Ingredient | Output |
|------------|--------|
| Hemp Seeds | Hemp Seed Oil |
| Truffle | Truffle Oil |
| Fairyweed | Fairyweed Herbal Oil |
| Appleweed | Appleweed Herbal Oil |
| Frostleaf | Frostleaf Herbal Oil |
| Hemp Flower | Hemp Herbal Oil |
| Flax Seeds | Linseed Oil, plus a Linseed Cake |
| Raw Peanuts | Peanut Oil |
| Pine Nuts | Pine Nut Oil |
| Chamomile | Chamomile Herbal Oil |
| Ginseng | Ginseng Herbal Oil |
| Yarrow | Yarrow Herbal Oil |

Every pressing also takes a Clay Bowl.

**Linseed Cake** - the pressed flax seed comes out as a cake beside the Linseed Oil. Drag it onto a placed Dry Fodder (Add to Fodder) and the fodder gains 6 Amount and 25 Nutrition, so your cattle eat longer and are better fed; Amount still stops at 12 and Nutrition at 100. Add it before the fodder runs out, because an empty fodder is gone. The numbers are starting values, not tuned, and it has not been checked in game.

The Appleweed, Fairyweed, Frostleaf and Hemp herbal oils provide the same mood/relaxation effects as their pipe equivalents: an alternative for players who prefer not to smoke.

Every pressed oil carries the `tag_Oil` lamp-oil tag, so with Advanced Copper Tools 1.17.0 or later installed it refuels ACT's Metal Lantern, Copper Brazier and Copper Oil Flask.

**Wax Leather Dressing** - drag a Linseed Oil onto Beeswax (from honeycomb, for example a Wild Bees' Nest) to make a Wax Leather Dressing; the oil is used up and you keep the clay bowl. Drag the dressing onto a leather garment (tunic, trousers, coat, gloves, shoes, helmet and the like) and its Quality goes up by 5, to a maximum of 100; the dressing is used up. Higher Quality means slower wear and a warmer garment, and a needle repair costs the garment 5 Quality, which this gives back. The 5 is a starting value, not tuned, it shares the Herbal Salve picture, and it has not been checked in game.

## Pickle Vat (Long Fermentation)

Preserves fermentable foods for months using salt brine.

### Building the Vat

1. Craft the **Unfired Pickle Vat** (Utensils tab).
2. Fire it in a kiln → **Pickle Vat** (item, CardType 0).

### Making Brine

1. Fill the pickle vat (item form) with water from any container.
2. Drag **Salt** onto the water-filled vat → water transforms into **Pickle Brine** in-place.

### Fermenting

1. Add fermentable ingredients to the vat. Ginger, Turnroot, and Fireroot are tagged fermentable by this mod; pickle blueprints in the crafting journal gate specific content recipes (Pickled Vegetables, Pickled Meat, Pickled Mushrooms, Pickled Frogs).
   - *Spiced version:* drag **Dried Wild Garlic** or **Dried Ginger** onto the vat of plain Pickle Brine (**Spice Brine**) to make **Spiced Pickle Brine**, then choose one of the four **Spiced Pickle** blueprints instead (they accept only spiced brine). The result has a stronger taste and is otherwise handled the same.
2. **Cap with Clay Bowl** → Closed Pickle Vat.
3. Drag water onto the closed vat to **Seal** it → Sealed Pickle Vat (placed structure).
4. Wait ~7 in-game days for fermentation to complete.
5. When the timer expires → **Ready Pickle Vat**. Harvest to spawn the pickled goods plus an empty fired vat for reuse.

**Important:** The pickle blueprints require brine — water alone won't start fermentation.

## Medicinal Teas

Brew ground medicinal powder in hot water.

**Required:** A bowl, cup, or kettle of water with **50%+ heat** (fully boiling or very hot).

| Tea | Ingredient | Effect |
|-----|-----------|--------|
| **Yarrow Tea** | Ground Yarrow + hot water | Wound healing, antiseptic, bleeds reduction |
| **Ginseng Tea** | Ground Ginseng + hot water | Energy, mental clarity, fatigue reduction |
| **Reishi Tea** | Ground Reishi + hot water | Immune support, stress relief |
| **Vigor Elixir** | Reishi Tea in a Clay or Copper Cauldron + Ground Ginseng (drag the powder onto the tea) | Strong Herbal Tonic dose (placeholder 72 of 96), long immune boost. Its picture is the clay bowl in its own colour |
| **Lion's Mane Tea** | Ground Lion's Mane + hot water | Cognitive boost |
| **Sleep Tea** | (combined herbs) | Lowers stress and fills you a little |
| **Stimulant Tea** | (combined herbs) | Lowers stress a little |
| **Anti-Nausea Tea** | (combined herbs) | Settles stomach |

**Brewing:** Drag the ground herb powder onto a hot water container (must be heated on a fire — campfire, copper stove, tea kettle, etc.).

## Other Medicinal Items

**Herbal Salve** — Craft from herbs + fat. A topical wound treatment applied directly to injuries. With Sirus23's Mod Collection installed, Lanolin can stand in for the oil.

**Herbal Tincture** — An alcohol-based herbal extract; more potent than tea in some situations.

**Herbal Honey Syrup** - One Honeycomb (from a Wild Bees' Nest), two Dried Chamomile or two Dried Common Plantain and a Clay Bowl, from the Medical tab once you hold Honeycomb. **Take a Spoonful** raises the Heather Effect (the same stat vanilla heather tea raises, capped at 48, so they do not add past it), which slows chest and throat infections for a while; three spoonfuls to a bowl, then you get the empty bowl back. The dose and spoonful count are starting values, not tuned, and it has not been checked in game.

**Botanical Field Guide** - Hemp Stalks make Hemp Pulp (Support tab; it unlocks once you hold Hemp Stalks). Leave the pulp alone for about a day and it becomes Soaked Hemp Pulp, 2 of which press into 3 Hemp Paper. Six paper and a Twine bind into the guide. Choose Read to see where and when each plant can be found; the guide is not used up. The quantities and times are starting values, not tuned, and it has not been checked in game.

**Gruit Ale** - Make a Gruit Bundle from the Cooking tab (Dried Bog Myrtle, Dried Heather Flowers, Dried Yarrow and Twine; it unlocks once you hold Dried Bog Myrtle and Dried Yarrow), then drag it onto a bowl of FINISHED rye ale, not onto wort. The ale becomes Gruit Ale: bitter and herbal, and it spoils half as fast as plain rye ale. It does not freeze and does not age further in a barrel. The half-speed spoilage and the price are starting values, not tuned, and it has not been checked in game.

**Herb-Spiced Wines and Ales** - Drop Dried Chamomile, Dried Yarrow or Dried Ginger onto a bowl of FINISHED billberry wine, birch wine, springberry wine, rye ale or wheat ale (not onto wort) and choose the matching Spice action. The herb is used up and the bowl becomes a mulled, herbal version of the drink that drinks like the original. It does not freeze and does not age further in a barrel. Its picture is the clay bowl in its own colour, and it has not been checked in game.

**Needle Ale** - Make a Needle Bundle from the Cooking tab (2 Dry Needles and Twine; it unlocks once you hold Dry Needles), then drag it onto a bowl of FINISHED rye ale. The ale becomes Needle Ale, a resinous spruce-beer style drink that drinks like rye ale and does not freeze or age in a barrel. The bundle shares the Gruit Bundle picture and the ale is the clay bowl in its own colour; it has not been checked in game.

**Forager's Seasoning** - Make it from the Cooking tab (one Ground Black Trumpet, one Ground Shiitake, one Ground King Oyster and one Salt; it unlocks once you hold all three powders). Drag the pouch onto a plateable food such as cooked meat, fish or eggs: the food takes on all three mushrooms' flavours at once, lightly. Each drag uses one of the pouch's 3 pinches, then the empty pouch is gone. It does not spoil.

**Fish Sauce** - Make Salted Fish Mash from the Cooking tab (two raw fish, lean or fatty, and one Salt; it unlocks once you hold the raw fish). Drag the mash onto a pot or bowl of water (at least 100): the water becomes Young Fish Sauce, and after about 5 days Fish Sauce. ## Forager's Pasties

**Making one.** In daylight, drag a mushroom or a berry onto **greased rye dough** or **greased wheat dough** and use
**Fill a Pasty**. The filling is used up and the dough becomes a **Raw Mushroom Pasty** or a **Raw Wild Berry Pasty**
(rye and wheat give the same pasty). The mushroom pasty takes Black Trumpet, Chanterelle, Chicken of the Woods, Golden Oyster, King Oyster, Lion's Mane, Morel, Shiitake and Giant Puffball. The berry pasty takes
Blackcurrant, Redcurrant, Lingonberry and Cloudberry. Reishi, Chaga, Death Cap and Truffle cannot be baked into one. Like vanilla's own
Add actions on that dough, Fill a Pasty needs light (Light 10 to 100), so it is meant to work only in daylight, and it adds a
little Filth and Focus while you do it.

**Baking.** Put the raw pasty in an **oven**. It cooks once the oven's heat gauge is at 178 or more, in one cooking
step, and becomes a **Mushroom Pasty** or **Wild Berry Pasty**. A raw pasty spoils quickly (96 spoilage points) and
loses quality on an open fire, so use the oven. Left in the oven, a baked pasty slowly burns, as vanilla's do.

**Eating.** Use the baked pasty's **Eat** button (1 daytime). The berry pasty has vanilla's Rye Bilberry Pasty's
nutrition; the mushroom pasty has the same without the fruit weariness. All the numbers are copied from vanilla's
pasties, not tuned for these, and none of it has been checked in game yet.

## Fungal Bloom

Rarely, after a wet spell in autumn, the forest fruits all at once. For about 2 days afterwards, the first time you arrive at each of the six Herbs and Fungi clearings a **Fungal Bloom** patch appears there. **Harvest** it for 3 to 4 Chanterelles, 2 to 3 Black Trumpets, 2 to 3 King Oysters, 1 to 2 Golden Oysters and 1 to 2 Morels (it trains Herbalism and is used up). An untouched patch rots away after about 3 days, so pick it quickly. The odds, timings and yields are placeholder starting values, not tuned; the patch shares the Raw Morel picture; not checked in game.

## Forage Hotspots and the Forager's Notebook

**Hotspots.** A Lingonberry Patch (Oak Clearing), a Cloudberry Patch (Pine Clearing) and a Chamomile Patch (Alder Woods) stay at their place. Choose **Pick** for 1 to 2 of the plant; each pick uses up a quarter of the patch. It must be at least a quarter ripe to pick (the Ripeness bar), refills in about 7 days from empty and does not grow in winter. These numbers are placeholders, and each patch shares its plant's picture.

**Forager's Notebook.** Bind 3 Hemp Paper and Twine (Support tab). Keep it in your inventory: each wild plant or mushroom of this mod that turns up is written in it, under the place, the first time only. Open the card to read the list. It shares the Botanical Field Guide picture.

## Wild Bees' Nest

**Where.** A Wild Bees' Nest sits at four of the mod's locations: Foraging Path, Pine Clearing, Oak Clearing and Alder Woods (not Highland Meadow or Misty Falls). Honeycomb has no other source in the vanilla game data of this version (checked 2026-10-02), so the nest is what makes the Berry Preserve craftable. Honeycomb is also food, and vanilla's own Extract Honey action turns it into Beeswax.

**Taking the comb.** The nest has a Comb bar and a Torpor bar. Once the Comb bar is at 98 percent or more, **Take the Comb** (3 daytime points) gives 1 to 2 Honeycomb and empties the bar. The nest stays where it is.

**Avoiding stings.** Drag a Lit Torch onto the nest (**Smoke**); a lit Bee Smoker works too, but nothing in this version of the game makes one (its vanilla blueprint is unused). That fills Torpor, and the bees stay drowsy for about an hour and a half, long enough for Take the Comb to cost no stings. With Torpor at zero only **Take the Comb Unsmoked** is offered, and it adds 4 to 16 to vanilla's Bee Stings effect, the figure vanilla's own hive and skep use for an unsmoked harvest. Bee Stings raises Pain while it lasts and drains by 1 every quarter hour, so a full sting wears off in about four hours.

**Regrowth.** The Comb bar grows by 1 every quarter hour, 672 for a full comb, which is about 7 days, and it does not grow while the Winter Counter is running (winter).

The 7 days, the 1 to 2 yield, the sting size, the 3 daytime points and the choice of these four locations are starting values, not tuned, and none of it has been checked in game yet.
