# Community Mod Chest

**Version:** 1.69.20

**Language:** English, Simplified Chinese

**Requires:** CSFFModFramework 2.28.0 or later. Advanced Copper Tools is optional (see Requirements).

Community-suggested content for Card Survival: Fantasy Forest. The centrepiece is a village east of the River Clearing, with residents, a Town Watch, a jail, an Academy, boards and seasons, plus traits, apparel, decor and pets. Amounts marked as placeholders may change in later updates.

## Items and building

- **Apparel:** Cloth Scarf, Cloth Mask, Hand Wraps, Foot Wraps, Leather Sandals, Leather Apron, Cloth Coat, Long Johns, Straw Hat, Chaperon, Quilted Cap, Quilted Vest and Cloth Hood.
  - **Dyeing:** drag Charcoal, Ochre or Billberry Pigment onto a plain Chaperon, Cloth Coat, Cloth Scarf, Quilted Cap, Straw Hat, Leather Apron or Cloth Mask. Dyed garments are cosmetic, keep their protection and wear, and trade for a little more.
  - **Disguises:** the Cloth Mask and the Cloth Hood (researched once the Weaver's Trust is 25 or more). A guard who spots you in the Wanted band adds 2.5 Suspicion instead of 5. It is a soft edge, not invisibility.
  - **With Sirus23 Mod Collection:** Wool-Quilted Cap and Vest (Tailoring > Equipment) add raw Wool padding for +2.0 and +3.0 perceived temperature and Comfort +10 and +12 (plain pieces give +1.0, +1.5, +8 and +10). They come undyed.
- **Layered warmth** - wearing three or more cold-weather pieces adds +1.0 perceived temperature (placeholder). Pieces that count: scarf, mask, hand and foot wraps, coat, long johns, chaperon, quilted and wool-quilted cap and vest, hood, in any dye. The Straw Hat, Sandals, Apron and Bone Lamellar do not. The bonus lands a few seconds after you dress.
- **Pigments:** ground on the Utensils tab with a hammering tool, three per grind: Charcoal Pigment (2 Charcoal), Ochre Pigment (2 Crushed Bog Iron) and Billberry Pigment (2 Dried Billberries). Charcoal Pigment also paints pottery.
- **Dye Vat:** Construction > Furniture; six Planks and two Rope. Put plain garments and the matching pigment inside and press **Dye Charcoal**, **Dye Ochre** or **Dye Billberry**. After an hour every plain garment becomes its dyed form, one pigment each, keeping wear. If pigment runs short the rest stay plain. **Take Apart** returns four Planks and a Rope. The Weaver also offers a one-time dye run (hand her a dyed garment with **Give Dyed Cloth** for 3 Salt and 3 Trust).
- **Carpentry decor:** eleven Construction > Furniture blueprints behind the Academy's Carpentry degree. Each adds Sanctuary while it stands (at most four of one kind count on a board).
  - **Boar Tusk Trophy** and **Stag Antler Trophy** (+25), **Cloth Rug** (+25), **Painted Canvas** in three colours (+25), **Log Bench** with a **Sit** button (+10), **Bowl of Potpourri** (+10, fades after about four days), **Framed Map** (+25), **Ice Sculpture** (+25, melts in about three days unless the base temperature is 0 or below) and **Stone Cairn** (+10).
  - At the cairn, **Meditate** takes an hour and eases 15 Stress. **Meditate in Peace** eases 30 for the Spiritually Harmonious. **Meditate Uneasily** eases 4 once you are hollowed (Hollowness 672 or more, always true for Spiritually Troubled).
  - **Take Apart** returns the materials except pigment, ice and tool wear.
- **Stockpiles**, the Stone, Twig and Bone kinds (three Planks and a Rope each, researchable from the start), hold up to 50 loose cards of one kind as a **Fullness** count. Drag cards on, use **Take One** or **Take Five** to get them back, and **Take Apart** to recover everything plus two Planks. A full stockpile, or a part-boiled Bone, is refused. Only these three materials are stored, because each comes back identical. No saving in frame rate or lag is claimed.
- **Graduation diplomas:** passing an Academy course's Final Exam hands you a framed diploma, once per course (Sanctuary +5, no trade value). Earlier graduates get missing ones when they stand in the Academy. A lost diploma is not replaced.
- **Wall paintings and curtains** - two more pieces of decor for your rooms.
  - **Wall paintings:** Wall Painting (Charcoal), (Ochre) and (Billberry) are room improvements costing two pigments and an hour for Sanctuary +50. They are offered in the six lived-in caves, the mud hut and the cabin, one painting per room.
  - **Curtains:** Construction > Furniture, behind the Carpentry degree; two Large Cloths. They need a room with a built window (this mod's seven interiors and the cabin's construction rooms). **Draw Curtains** and **Open Curtains** swap states. Drawn adds +1 perceived temperature but cancels the window's cabin-fever relief.
- **Weapons and armor:** the Stone Mace (blunt, trains Melee Fighting), the Fire-Hardened Spear (full vanilla spear moves plus a Charred-Tip Strike with bonus Char damage), the Wooden Shield, and bone-tier armor with working damage reduction. The Carpentry course's **Outfit Wardrobe** gives the set somewhere to hang.
- **Soaking Trough:** Construction > Working Tools; four Planks and a Rope. Soak Quicklime in water in it to make limewater. Drag a fleshed hide on to loosen it: the hide gains a quarter of the progress it needs to tan, once per hide.
- **Pottery:** painted bowls, cups, plates and vases, clay figurines (bear, castle, dragon) and clay beads. Drag a fresh flower onto a Painted Clay Vase for more Sanctuary that lasts about three days.
- **Decor and rest:** Heather Wreath, Wind Chimes, Bedroll, Straw Mat, Incense Burner, Woven and Tricolor Wall Hangings, Stone Tiles and the **Comfortable Bed**.
  - Since 1.69.17, decor you carry or set down raises **Sanctuary**, not Comfort: as in the base game, Comfort comes from what you wear. Toys, figurines, pottery, hangings, chimes and the lit candle and burner give +5 or +10, placed pieces +10 or +25, the Forest Throne and the room improvements +50, and at most four of one kind count on a board. The Bedroll is the light bedroll (half the weight of the base game's, weaker sleep) and gives no Comfort. The Comfortable Bed warms you (+3 perceived temperature) only while you rest in it.
  - The bed is a Down Mattress (100 Feathers, 50 Fibers, 4 Large Cloth, 20 Twine) plus a Copper Bed Frame (three copper Metal Sheets, 6 Wood, 2 Planks). The sheets ship in the framework. The frame unlocks at Inn Keeper friendship 30 with a Metal Sheet on hand, the bed at friendship 15. Assembled it has Nap and Sleep actions, stronger rest than the Bedroll and a warmth bonus. **Take Apart** splits it.
- **Lighting:** the Ceramic Lamp and Scented Candle. Refuel the lamp with fat or a bowl of any oil (ACT's Oil, or any Herbs & Fungi oil); the empty bowl comes back. It holds 96 fuel and a refuel adds 24 (four to fill it), so a full lamp burns about 48 hours. The candle burns beeswax.
- **Toys and games:** Bone Dice, Spinning Top and Wheeled Horse (Entertainment tab). Each has a 15-minute pastime that eases Stress (and gives Focus, or less Loneliness for the horse). Repeating it too often in a day fades it. Drag one onto a tamed cat to play together. **Loaded Dice** and the **Morris Board** are for Inn games.
- **Fishing:** the Fishing Net and the Iron Fishing Rod (from Iron Fishing Rod Fittings). The net lasts 8 casts, comes back empty about two casts in five and brings in about 0.7 fish a cast, a Perch in about one cast in four. The rod's Fishing Aid (+1.2) works only while you hold it. With Far Territory, the net also casts on its seas. On the river's Ice Hole it casts through the ice instead: about half the casts come back empty, most of the rest bring a Minnow (about 0.55 fish a cast), and below freezing (base temperature 0 or lower) each cast wears the net twice as much (4 casts instead of 8). The odds and the 2x wear are placeholders.
- **Herb Paste:** ground from Wild Garlic and Old Growth Bark. Drag it onto cooked, boiled or dried meat, bird, fish or sausage (or the food onto the paste) to **Season** it. The food takes the Pungent, Herbal and Tannic flavours and the paste is used up. Tannic pairs well with Savoury and, more weakly, Earthy, so seasoned food gives more Gratification. Season too often and it turns too strong.
- **Hunting Stand:** a reward blueprint from the Miller's grain errand chain. Build the kit (3 Planks, 4 Long Sticks, 2 Rope) and set it up. While you are there, a roaming predator heading onto your board is turned back about 25% of the time, once per animal per day. It does not move a predator already there or stop a fight that has begun.
- **Garden Trellis:** 6 Long Sticks and 8 Twine, researched after you visit the Village Farm. It weathers away after 6 days. On the same board as planted crops it adds 0.25 Growth a tick to each of 22 herb, mushroom, berry, flax and grain crops. Trellises stack. Sown fields and trees are not helped.
- **Village Home Sign:** 2 Planks, 2 Long Sticks and a Rope, researchable from the start. A plain board gives Sanctuary +10. **Carve the Face** (1 hour) gives it new art, and **Plane the Face Smooth** reverses it. **Take Down Sign** recovers the materials.
- **Stone Tile Floor:** a room improvement for cabins, mud huts and all seven village interiors. 12 Stone Tiles and 4 Clay, 2 hours, Sanctuary +50 and +1 perceived temperature.
- **Trading values:** prices for 653 base-game items and liquids that had none, so NPCs no longer hand them out free. Salt is 15, dried meat 40, a metal nugget 250, cloth clothing 1500. Abundant materials (stones, leaves, twigs, grass, snow, water) and waste stay at 0.

## Traits

48 character-creation traits, listed as positive or drawback. The seven Graduate perks from the Academy never appear at character creation. Opposite traits exclude each other (Strong and Bad Kidneys, Green and Black Thumb, Swimmer and Sinker, Spiritually Harmonious and Troubled, Nyctophobia and Nightcrawler, among others). The retired Higher Education, Peaceful Farmer, Prospector and Sensitive Skin traits remain only so old saves stay valid.

- **Positive:** Angler, Claws, Abundant Growth, Exiled Artist, Green Thumb, Mineral Rich, Natural Trader, Past Life Memories, Spiritually Harmonious, Strong Kidneys, Swimmer, Trapline, Troglodyte, Trollblood and Wide Hands, plus:
  - **Born Mark:** raised Luck. It lowers the chance of being caught searching a chest or of your stall being robbed (full effect at Luck 36), and improves Iron Fishing Rod catches.
  - **Giant Stomach** (15 Suns, designed by Chiwei): your stomach holds two and a half times as much food, empties faster when very full, and eating never makes you sick.
  - **Founders Kit** (100 Suns): Place it to unpack a Cabin Kit, two Rain Cistern Kits, 15 Planks, 15 Mud Bricks, 20 Stones, 6 Heavy Stones, 6 Tree Logs, 10 Clay, 5 Rope and 20 Copper Nails.
  - **Extra Rain Cistern Kit** (10 Suns), **Potter's Apprentice** (5 Suns: five Clay, two ground pigments, Charcoal and a Basket) and **Caravan Peddler** (25 Suns: an assembled Market Stall Kit and two Bear Figurines).
  - **Weak Courage:** a milder Brave (+50 Courage).
  - **Village Pathfinder:** opens the way into the village area.
  - **Village Inn:** +5 starting friendship with the Inn Keeper, who skips the formal introductions.
  - **Village Founder** (3 Stars): both cottages, the Village Hall and the Apothecary's Cabin already stand, the residents have moved in, and their first errands are settled.
  - **Graduate** (3 Stars): a quick study. Every Academy course takes half its sessions (and half its tuition); you still sit each final exam for the course's graduate perk and blueprints.
  - **Quiet Village:** the Miller, Weaver, Apothecary, Professor and Potter stay at home, the Academy or the Inn, and the Town Watch never takes its posts. You give up that content for less simulation work.
- **Drawback:** Aged, Agoraphobia, Bad Kidneys, Black Thumb, Bleeder, Deadly Disease, Drunkard, Insomniac, Lactose Intolerant, Leper, Lost Tourist, Lunacy, Nightcrawler, Nyctophobia, Seasonal Allergies, Sinker, Spiritually Troubled, Weak Stomach and Weakened Growth, plus:
  - **Fugitive:** your first village visit raises Village Crime to 12 (Suspected), fading over about twelve days.
  - **Cowardice:** a milder Fainthearted (-100 Courage).
- The starter building-kit perks (Cabin, Mud Hut, Log Bed, Furnace, Forge, Oven, Tanning Pit) live in the separate HomesteadPerks mod.

## The village area

Twelve explorable locations east of the River Clearing. Build the **River Bridge** at the River Clearing (its 40 Metal Nails ship in the framework), hold the Village Pathfinder trait, or swim across. Swimming needs Stamina 20 or more, costs 20 Stamina (12 at Swimming 50 or more), soaks you, takes half an hour and trains the skill. Village Crime never closes the crossing.

- **Village Path:** the staging point. By day it carries distant sounds of the settlement.
- **Hunter's Crossing:** pine hunting ground and a shortcut to Pine Trail. Badgers den here.
- **Village Farm:** forage **Wild Garlic** (15%), find the Garden Trellis blueprint, and harvest a seasonal field (Flax in spring, Turnroot in summer, Rye in autumn). A Partner can harvest the fields.
- **Village:** the Inn, Academy and Town Wood Pile stand here from day one, and the Market Stall can be set up here.
- **Moss-Grown Clearing, Pine Trail, Highland Pines:** the northern trail.
- **Stillwater Meadow and Sett Warren:** hunting ground north of the Village. Sett Warren borders Greenfalls across a cliff that stays locked until you finish the Weaver's final quest.
- **Clay Shoal, Sodden Hollow and the Foraging Forest:** the southern trail. Clay Shoal has a Clay Barrow. The Foraging Forest gives **Old Growth Bark** (10%) and Wild Garlic (8%), plus rare mushrooms with Herbs & Fungi.
- **Seasonal blockers:** winter snow drifts block all three roads out of the Village until dug clear with a shovel. An autumn deadfall blocks Pine Trail to Highland Pines until cleared with an axe. A summer Bramble Thicket blocks Stillwater Meadow to Sett Warren until cut with a metal sickle (three cuts) or torn through by hand. A spring flood shuts the Clay Shoal to Sodden Hollow ford: wait on the bank three times or build the **Plank Causeway** at Clay Shoal. Each stays open until its season returns.
- **Wildlife:** bears, boars, wolves, the Wolf Pack, the Primeval Wolf, the Forest Beast and the Moontouched never walk onto the Village, Village Path or Village Farm, or into the village buildings. Hunter's Crossing, Pine Trail, Highland Pines, Stillwater Meadow and the Foraging Forest are hunting grounds: they turn them back about half the time. The guard refuses moves only: it does not move a predator already there, stop a fight an animal opens from elsewhere, or stop a boar, Forest Beast or Moontouched fight you start yourself.
- **Wild Garlic** is modest food and drink. **Old Growth Bark** goes into Herb Paste, and its **Tan Hide** action starts the tanning of a fleshed hide (a quarter of its tanning, once per hide, before it is a fifth tanned); finish it in a Tanning Pit.

## Village buildings

- **Village Inn:** enter it like a cabin. Drop Salt, metal Nuggets or a Duros coin on the Inn Counter to fund an Inn Account (up to 500). **Purchase Meal** and **Stay the Night** cost 50 (60 while Wanted, 75 while Banished). **Purchase Meal** is a hearty bowl of stew, bread and water, about as filling as a good meal of your own. **Work Odd Jobs** is a 4-hour shift for 2 Salt, once a day, with no balance needed. While you have a Village Crime record, **Community Service** is 4 unpaid hours that take 6 off it. **Sell Firewood** pays a Salt for every fourth bundle. The fireplace is kept lit from the Town Wood Pile.
- **Village Academy:** study opens after the Inn Keeper's introduction. Fund a Tuition Account (up to 1,000) at the lectern. Sessions are 2 hours and cost 100 (120 Wanted, 150 Banished; a Banished student is never turned away). Courses: Architecture (72 h), Metallurgy (60 h), Herbalism (30 h), Fishing, Armorer and Medicine (24 h each) and Carpentry (24 h). Every session, the final exam included, also teaches the course's skill +3, up to +15 from each course (Architecture: Crafting, Metallurgy: Smithing, Herbalism and Medicine: Herbalism, Fishing: Fishing, Armorer: Metalworking, Carpentry: Woodworking). Passing a Final Exam grants a hidden Graduate perk:
  - **Architecture** gives the Water-Driven Sawmill and Grinding Mill recipes with no research (anyone can still research them; with Metallurgy it also unlocks the Forge and Workshop Kit), all from Water-Driven Infrastructure, plus the Beaver Blessing.
  - **Metallurgy** gives the Metal Sheet and Bronze Sheet recipes with no research.
  - **Herbalism** gives each herb or mushroom you forage a one-in-four chance to come in double. **Fishing** unlocks the Iron Fishing Rod recipe and improves its catches.
  - **Armorer** gives every Advanced Copper Tools armor recipe with no research (copper, tin, ghost bronze, tin bronze, white bronze and iron suits, both iron breastplate fits, and the Copper, Iron and Ghost Bronze Shields); without the degree they stay in normal research. It needs Advanced Copper Tools and is hidden without it.
  - **Medicine** speeds clotting and pain recovery and unlocks the **Herb Poultice** and **Herb Tincture**, and lets you brew a **Spring Tonic** (two dried Heather Flowers and a dried Willow Bark, ground with a Mortar and Pestle you keep; for about a day it adds Comfort and slows upper respiratory infection, and the Apothecary stocks it too).
  - **Carpentry** unlocks the decor above, a **Wicker Chair**, a **Training Post** (2 Planks, 2 Rope, 2 Stones; Practice Strikes: an hour for Melee Fighting +2 and Stamina -10, once a day) and the **Outfit Wardrobe** (three 18-slot sections; use **Equip Outfit N** to change a whole outfit at once), and the graduate counts Woodworking 15 higher.
  - Metallurgy's two sheets, the Architecture mills and the Armorer armor are only handed over early and stay in normal research for everyone else; every other blueprint above stays locked until its course is passed. An older save may hold the retired Carpentry Bench: its **Transfer to Lecture Hall** moves its balance and hours to the lectern.
  - **Sit In and Study Alone** at the Academy: when the Miller, Weaver, Apothecary or Professor is there with you, **Sit In** gives two hours of listening for +3 to that resident's craft (Cooking, Weaving, Herbalism, Insight), once a week each. The **Academy Bookshelf** offers **Study Alone** (+1 Insight, once a day).
- **Market Stall:** a kit of 6 Planks, 4 Small Cloth, 6 Rope and a Storage Chest (ACT's Copper Chest also fits). Research it with a Storage Chest on hand. Set it up only in the Village. One item sells each day you spend in the Village with it. Sales build Sales Revenue, which you collect as copper nuggets (250 each), salt (15 each) or, with DurosCoinage, coins.
  - The daily sale gets a Reputation bonus growing to +25% at full Reputation, halved while Banished. A stall has a 5% chance of being robbed every 7 such days (down to 1% with Luck 36); the count survives a relaunch.
  - **Report the Theft:** after a robbery, choose it in Talk with any guard. About three days later the item comes back half the time, in the Village (not the stall), otherwise nothing is found.
  - **Set Up Awning** and **Take Down Awning** toggle a free cosmetic look. **Pack Up Stall** hands back the kit (not yet confirmed in game).
- **Town Wood Pile:** drag Twigs, Long Sticks, Wood or Firewood on to fill its Wood Stockpile bar (starts at 96, one hearth). Wood counts about 35, Pine or Alder 21, Twigs 7, a Long Stick 4. The same fuel fills a Player's Pay bar you withdraw with **Collect as Copper** or **Collect as Salt**. The Inn, Academy, Village Hall, both cottages and the Apothecary's Cabin burn from this pile and go out if it runs dry.
- **Village Well:** the Inn Keeper gives you **Well Plans** in his week-3 backstory. Keep them in the Village and the blueprint appears. Built, drag an empty water container on to **Draw Water** for free. It counts toward Village Reputation.
- **Miller's Cottage** and **Weaver's Cottage**: seven build stages each, cannot be taken down. A week after each is finished, its resident moves in.
- **Village Hall:** the civic capstone (20 Plaster, 16 Clay, 10 Heavy Stone). It reports **Village Reputation**: standing from both cottages, the Well, the River Bridge and a Market Stall milestone, less your Village Crime. Reputation also shows in the Mental tab. The first time it reads full, a **Founder's Monument** is owed to you: it appears once in the Village the next time you are there, adding 25 Sanctuary while you are in the village. It cannot be taken down.
  - **Boards room:** eight boards. The Inn Keeper, Miller, Weaver, Apothecary, Professor and Potter each have one showing the current errand and hint, a teaser, a relationship note, recent errands, and a live line saying where that villager is right now. The **Village Standing** board reads your Reputation, and **Village Projects** tracks the seven structures.
- **Cozier interiors:** the seven interiors work like a cabin room: Insulation (+6 perceived temperature), Cabin Fever, and Sanctuary 200 in the six residential ones. Windows and the Stone Tile Floor can be built in all seven. The Jail Cell alone can build a **Fireplace**: a prisoner gets enough stone, mud brick, clay and twigs once per sentence (plus a Long Stick to dig and dry grass to insulate), peat with each day's food and one lit tinder a day once it stands. Build it by day.
- **Apothecary's Cabin:** buildable in the Foraging Forest after graduating Herbalism. Its last stage takes six rare herbs (any mix of Silverwort Blossom, Hollow Sage and, with Herbs & Fungi, Dried Hemp Flower, Ginseng or Reishi), three Dried Nettle Leaves and a tamed Shadow, who is consumed.
- **Village timeline:** until you finish the Inn Keeper's introduction, most of the village stays closed (the Professor redirects you, trading and study are refused, and the cottage, Hall and Well blueprints stay hidden). Finishing it starts a hidden Village Clock: his settled-in line at week 2, odd jobs from week 2, backstory at week 3, fireside tales from week 4 and Ash's disappearance no earlier than week 6. Ask him to "remind me how the village works" for a recap.

## Town Watch, crime and jail

- **The guards:** Captain Reeve Sterling holds the square and moves only when summoned. Guards Nella Thorne and Old Corrin work the day shift (05:00 to 20:00). Guard Iris Vane keeps the night watch (20:00 to 05:00). Once Village Crime reaches Banished she hunts you anywhere, day or night, and calls the rest of the Watch rather than fighting you.
- **Village Crime bands:** Suspected (1 to 24), Wanted (25 to 59), Banished (60 or more). It rises when you attack a guard (+35), kill one (+100, Banished for good) or are caught robbing a chest (+10) or digging the tunnel.
  - Sterling gives one formal warning at Suspected. From Wanted, a guard who shares your spot raises her Suspicion by 5 per sighting (2.5 in a mask or hood). Fights and chases start at 60 or more.
  - Each guard carries a hidden **Suspicion** rating (Iris the sharpest, Corrin the slowest to suspect) that the Copper Chests' night-time search roll now reads. It falls about 1 a day while your crime is under 25.
  - **Escort:** while Sterling does not suspect you (his Suspicion at his usual), drag a Salt onto him to hire an escort across the hunting grounds (Hunter's Crossing, Pine Trail, Highland Pines, Stillwater Meadow). For about six hours predators are turned back there instead of about half the time; walking back into the village ends it. It stops predator fights and predator arrivals only, never a fight you start. One-Salt fee and duration are placeholders.
  - **Bribes:** drag Salt or a metal nugget onto Sterling, Thorne or Corrin to lower their Suspicion. On Sterling a Salt is a bribe only while his Suspicion is above his usual (otherwise it hires the escort); a nugget always is. Iris Vane takes none, and bribes never touch your crime.
  - **Community service** is the lawful way back. While your crime is 1 to 59, ask Sterling for honest work: carry wood to the Town Wood Pile (one stocking worth 20 fuel or more) or, in winter, dig a snow drift clear. Each job takes 6 off. Three jobs with a crime of 0 earn a **Deputy** line (+5 to Reputation's civic half).
- **Fighting the Watch:** any guard can be attacked from her inspection panel after a confirmation. All four have real Blood and armor, and a fresh character should expect to lose. Hits cause Bruising, Fear and wound items to Clean or Stitch. Only one fight runs at a time, and a guard keeps her wounds into the next. Guards usually break and flee; a routed guard returns after a season, and a killed one after 7 days, in the Jail. Put all four down and the Inn Keeper, who has heard, can clear your Village Crime in a private talk.
- **Pursuit:** at 60 or more, Thorne and Corrin chase one location per move on village-side ground. Cross the river and they stop at the Village Path and wait, coming after you again only if you step back over. Iris Vane chases anywhere.
- **Arrest:** once badly hurt (seriously bruised, shaken, bleeding heavily or exhausted), Thorne or Corrin may grab you and send for the Captain. Sterling would rather talk you down and lets you walk away up to three times, then takes you in unhurt. Attack him, or be caught once the whole Watch has converged, and he fights for real.
- **Jail:** the Village Jail sits beside the Village Hall. A sentence is at least 3 days, 7 per guard killed since your last sentence (capped at 8), or about a day per 8 points of crime. Each day served pays crime down, and walking out clears the record. There is no "Walk out" until it is served.
  - Once per sentence, with a warden on the step and 1.5 days or more left, the cell's **Pay the Warden** button takes a day off for 3 Salt (4 Wanted, 5 Banished). It clears no crime.
  - **Break Rocks** is the other way to work time off: four hours and 30 Stamina for a quarter day off and 2 crime points, twice a day at most, with at least 2 days left.
  - **The tunnel:** the four guards stand warden in shifts (Sterling 05:00 to 11:00, Thorne 11:00 to 17:00, Corrin 17:00 to 23:00, Vane 23:00 to 05:00). About every twelve hours the whole Watch is off the step for a couple of hours. Shove the bed aside for a **Hidden Tunnel** and **Dig** (30 minutes a dig, only while the step is empty). Caught with it exposed, it caves in and the sentence grows by half plus a little crime. Dig enough and **Crawl through the tunnel** leads not out but into the **Skylit Cave** beneath the jail; the escape is two projects. The tunnel gets you down; getting out is the second: a **Branch Ladder** (2 Long Sticks, 8 Wood, 4 Alder Bark over three stages of an hour and a half each) built in the cave opens a Rock Wall climb to the Ledge, where clearing the Collapsed Tunnel (16 hauls of an hour each, shored up as you go, by torchlight: a pitch torch from the cave burns about nine hours) and crawling out cuts the sentence by 8 days and brands you an escaped convict (+15 crime). Measured with a test bot playing a prisoner with no stash who does nothing but escape, it took 3.5 to 4.8 in-game days from the first dig to the way out: about three days of work and sleep, plus however long you wait in the cell for the Watch to leave the step. Below, the risk is the **headcount**: the Watch counts the prisoners at 06:00 and 21:00 (placeholder hours), even if you sleep through it. Be in the cave or on the ledge then, with a day or more left to serve, and you are caught once (half the sentence again plus a little crime) and the hole under your bed is filled in. Whatever you built in the cave and on the ledge stays, but the crawl back to the cell is closed: the only way out is forward, so do not go down without a way to make light (the cave lets you make a pitch torch and fire from what grows there).
  - **Ration:** a bowl of gruel and a jug of water appear daily, with an emergency top-up (on by default). **Meditate**, Nap and Sleep pass time.
- **Residents and your standing:** while Wanted, every resident's Trade charges up to 0.5 more on buying and pays up to 0.25 less. At Banished it holds at that maximum but they still trade. Their chests still buy from you and the Miller's takes restitution. Inn prices, tuition and the stall rise or fall as described above. Chest sales, restitution, Player's Pay, odd jobs, deposits and firewood are never marked up. Residents also greet you coldly while Wanted and warmly again below 25, with every Trade still working and thanks for handed-in errands still played.

## Residents

- **Prices on your standing:** every resident's Trade also reads your Socials (up to 0.06 off buying and 0.06 more on selling, full at 150) and Facial Harmony (up to 0.015, full at 100). They only help, and the Wanted markup still outweighs them. Natural Trader gets 0.03 from day one. A Leper misses the average face's discount.
- **Trust and gifts:** Chat gives the Miller, Weaver, Apothecary and Potter +1 Trust, and trading a little more; their prices improve as Trust climbs (buying toward 1.1 times, selling toward 0.75). Once a day, give the Miller bread (Wheat, Rye or Rye Round), the Weaver a painted figurine or vase, or the Apothecary a rare herb for +3 Trust. At Trust 80 or more with crime under 25, the Miller, Weaver and Apothecary each give a keepsake once (five Wheat Flour, an Ash-Grey Cloth Scarf, a Powerful Healing Potion).
- **Inn Keeper:** **Chat** (1 hour) eases Loneliness, Stress and more. **Trade** buys pantry food, spices and salt at 1.5 times value and pays 0.5. The pantry restocks every 4 days by season. With Advanced Copper Tools it also stocks a few Focus, Calming and Warming Teas and, only with Herbs & Fungi installed, its Mushroom Pasty, Wild Berry Pasty and Forager's Trail Mix; without H&F those three simply do not appear. With Sirus23 it adds Sheep Cheese. **Talk** offers fireside tales, errands and remarks on your achievements, finished buildings and Reputation (each once, +1 friendship for achievements). On an evening with two or more residents at the Inn, his first Talk passes on a line of what the table is talking about. At friendship 15 he greets you warmly and grants the **Inn Regular** perk; friendship also improves his prices.
  - **Ash, the inn's lost cat:** agree to help and Ash prowls the Foraging Forest from 17:00 to 06:00. Win him over with meat or fish. Around village week 6 he may track a boar toward Hunter's Crossing; follow and face the boar to send him home.
  - Give a **Wild Garlic** gift to unlock the Market Stall blueprint.
- **Miller and Weaver:** each moves in a week after their cottage is finished, sleeps at home (22:00 to 06:00), works there by day, visits the Inn each evening and spends one day a week at the Academy. The Miller sells Planks and flours, the Weaver Spindle, Bone Needle, Twine and Yarn Fiber. Their cottage blueprints stay available at any time. After the Weaver's seven-errand chain, **Talk** to her for the **Climbing Rope** (four Rope and a Plank): build it at Greenfalls, past Sett Warren, to open the cliff path both ways.
- **Apothecary:** once you gift her a Market Stall Kit she commutes: her cabin overnight, the Market Stall by day, the Inn every evening (19:15 to 21:00) and one Academy afternoon a week. **Trade** restocks her herbs and tinctures. Drop a **Rare Herb Mixture** on her and she brews a Powerful Healing Potion over two days. From 9:00 until she leaves for the Inn, if your Market Stall stands in the Village, her notice board says she is minding the Market Stall.
- **Professor:** lodges at the Inn, forages the outskirts by day until his satchel fills, then holds his post at the Academy. His **Trade** stock improves with course graduates and his prices with Trust. Errands: a weekly specimen request (Nettle Leaves), Field Samples from day 10 (five Billberries) and, after Herbalism, Applied Herbalism (four Dried Nettle Leaves). He gives gifts for your first degree, all six original courses (Carpentry does not count) and Trust 80, and remarks on village news.
- **Potter:** takes a room at the Inn once the Inn Keeper has introduced you. On the summer market day he works the Village's market corner from 08:00 to 18:00. **Trade** sells painted bowls, cups, plates, glazed plates, beads, vases and raw Clay, restocked daily at the Inn. Ask **Need anything fired?** and hand him a plain fired bowl, plate, jar or vase with **Give Fired Pottery** for 3 Salt and 3 Trust, once.
- **Chance meetings on the road:** meet the Apothecary on her commute or the Professor while he forages for a word (+3 Morale, +1 Trust), at most once every 18 hours each. The Miller and Weaver never meet you this way, since the only outdoor tile they use is the Village. With the Quiet Village perk the residents do not commute, so nothing happens.
- **Commissions** let you pay for goods: drag a metal Nugget or Duros coin onto the Miller (150 for ten Wheat Flour) or Inn Keeper (150 for a Doe Carcass) and press **Commission Flour** or **Hire a Hunter**; the Weaver's Large Cloth costs 360, paid down card by card until **Commission Cloth** opens the order. The goods wait in their room a day or two later. No resident takes commissions in your first week in the village, and after you collect an order the Miller waits 3 days, the Weaver 4 and the Inn Keeper 7 before the next. Each resident takes one order at a time, and an uncollected order keeps waiting. The cottages' own grinding and weaving blueprints stay available to you. Not yet confirmed in game.
- **Hireling chore (Miller):** drag four Log Pieces onto the Miller (**Hire Log Splitting: Hand Over a Log**) and pay two Salt (**Hire Log Splitting: Pay a Salt**, one per drag). The next day, standing in his cottage, you get twelve Firewood, the same as splitting the logs yourself (three per log), at the lowest Quality of your logs. One chore at a time; it stays open in winter. Drying meat and scutching flax are not built yet.
- **Copper Chests:** the Miller, Weaver, Apothecary, Inn Keeper and Professor each keep a chest in their interior, restocked about weekly with Salt and trade stock (Inn Keeper 500 salt-value, Miller and Weaver 300, Apothecary and Professor 180 but rarer). Drag a valuable on to **sell** it, paid from the chest's contents (selling raises Trust). **Search for valuables** takes everything, with a 15% catch chance (5% more per earlier theft that season, outright if the owner is in the room). Iris Vane's night watch adds to the roll. A **Burglar's Kit** and a Born Mark's Luck trim it, never below 1%. Getting caught adds 10 Village Crime and costs the owner's Trust (20 points, 6 of the Inn Keeper's 30 Friendship; an unseen theft costs nothing). Afterwards they raise it themselves when you Talk, or you ask with **About your chest...** (not for a day after you are caught, then once every 3 days; **Another day.** puts it off for a day): bring one named item (Firewood to the Miller, Twine to the Weaver, Wax to the Apothecary, Dried Meat to the Inn Keeper, an unlit Candle to the Professor) with **Make Amends** and they win back 10 Trust per errand (3 for the Inn Keeper), never above where it stood before the theft. **Make It Right:** while you have any crime, drag Salt or a Metal Nugget onto the Miller's chest to pay some down.

## Events and pastimes

- **Evenings at the Inn:** with two or more of the Miller, Weaver, Apothecary and Professor present (usually 18:00 to 21:00), **Join the Table** gives an hour of company (+2 Trust each, and a Warm Evening: Comfort +10 for about half a day), or drag a Wooden Flute, Bone Flute, Frame Drum or Clay Ocarina on for **Play for the Room** (+3 Trust each, instrument kept). One use per evening.
  - **Games** (a resident at the Inn, a 25 stake from your Inn Account, wins pay double): **Wager on the Dice** (honest dice win 45%; Loaded Dice win 60% but are caught 15% of the time, costing the stake and 8 crime), **Play Morris** with the Morris Board (an hour, 30% plus up to 20% from Focus) and **Arm Wrestle** (8 Stamina, 15 minutes, 30% plus up to 20% from Fitness). Stakes and odds are placeholders and never marked up.
  - **Odd-jobs board:** the counter posts one small job each morning (a Stone, Rope, Ash or a Clay Bowl). Drag the goods on for two hours, 10 Energy and 7 Stamina and a Salt (the same pay per hour as Work Odd Jobs).
- **Village seasons** - three beats, each fired once when its season arrives and never again on a reload. In autumn the Inn Keeper asks for dried food (**Stock the Pantry** for 3 Salt and 3 friendship). On summer's first day, residents ease prices a little for a day (about 1.3% to 2%, never enough to beat the Wanted markup). In winter the Inn offers **Join the Snowed-In Supper** (17:00 to midnight, Morale +35), and from first snow to thaw the Inn Keeper's pantry, Purchase Meal and all three commissions are closed, so the Inn sells no food in winter (an order placed before the snow still arrives).
- **Town Achievement Board:** in the Inn, it tracks Finding a Friend (meet a travelling trader), Full Kit (six metal tools), Right to Bear Arms (kill a bear), Master Hunter (twelve animals), Forest Explorer (ten groves), Happy New Year, Master Angler (six fish), Spelunker (eight caves), Master Shaman (nineteen spirits), Order Up! (fill a trader's commission), Spiritual Overcrowding and the Stinky Jar (unexplained; the Inn Keeper explains them once earned) and **King of the Forest**. With Harsh Wilds it adds **Northern Quarry** (kill a lynx, a wisent and a musk ox; not yet confirmed in game).
  - King of the Forest needs the Inn Keeper's friendship at 24 or more of 30, the Miller, Weaver, Apothecary and Professor at Trust 80 or more, and Reputation 100. It teaches the **Forest Throne** (six Planks, a Large Leather, two Twine; Sanctuary +50) and **Forest Crown** (a copper Metal Sheet and a Small Cloth; Comfort +10 worn). It does not end the run.
- **Cats:** Ash and Shadow each have a **Care** stat with four description tiers. A **Treat** or playing with a toy raises it. Each drink from a Rain Cistern lowers it, so tend them yourself. A **Cat Bed** (Sanctuary +5) set down on a cat's board slowly mends that cat's Care, about 4.8 a day. At Care 90 or more a cat is **Content**: a doubled Loneliness aura and a 10% daily chance of a small gift. A **Cat Bowl** (a Clay Bowl) holds three pieces of meat or fish and a hungry cat eats one by itself at half a cistern drink's Care cost.
  - **Shadow the Cat:** after Herbalism he turns up in the Foraging Forest from 17:00 to 06:00. Food will not win him: play a flute, drum or Clay Ocarina near him. He is the last requirement for the Apothecary's Cabin. There is only one Shadow per game, wherever you set him down; if lost on no board he can return, and once given to the cabin he is gone.
  - **Shadow's night hunts** happen at night (17:00 to 06:00): while Shadow is set down on your board with his Care at 70 or more, he may slip off and leave **Shadow's Trail**. About six hours later, the next time you are on that board, he is back with a Squirrel or Partridge carcass. He goes at most once every three days.

## Requirements

- BepInEx 5.x.
- **CSFFModFramework 2.28.0 or later.** It loads the content, places the village on the map and ships the Metal Sheet, Metal Nail and Tin Solder. On an older framework, dialogs may open nothing, graduate perks may appear at character creation and seasonal road blockers may not appear.
- **Advanced Copper Tools** (optional): its Copper Chest fits the Market Stall, its armor is handed over early by the Armorer course, and its teas, tools and armor join the Inn Keeper's pantry and the Professor's stock. Without it the Armorer course is hidden.
- **DurosCoinage** (optional): a Duros coin becomes a third currency at the Inn Counter and Academy lectern, the Inn Keeper's chest adds one, and both Market Stalls offer **Collect as Coins**.
- **Water-Driven Infrastructure** (optional): its Sawmill and Grinding Mill come early from the Architecture course and its Forge and Workshop Kit are unlocked by Architecture with Metallurgy, and the Professor's stock may offer its Small Copper Gear and Iron Wrench. His Metallurgy stock also offers a Metal Nail, which needs only the framework.
- **Herbs & Fungi** (optional): its Dried Hemp Flower, Ginseng and Reishi count as rare herbs and daily gifts. It adds three foods to the Inn Keeper's pantry, Hemp Seeds, Herb Pipe, Drying Tray, Flower Garland and Reishi to the Professor's stock, six remedies to the Apothecary's shelf, forage at all twelve locations and the Weaver's Process Hemp recipe.
- **Sirus23 Mod Collection** (optional): Lanolin Salve in the Professor's stock, Sheep Cheese at the Inn, Wool Yarn and Felted Wool at the Weaver (with a one-time **Running short of yarn?** errand for 2 Salt and a little Trust), and the Wool-Quilted pieces. Without Sirus23 those goods and that errand simply do not appear. You can sell its goods to any resident, since each card carries its own trade value. A researched Wool-Quilted recipe survives removing Sirus23, though its empty Wool slot then pads with nothing (read from the game's code, not played).

Load order does not matter. Every optional mod can be absent.

## Under the hood

Pieces for modders, with the files that hold them:

- **Tannic flavour:** `FlavourTag/CMC_FlavourTannic.json` plus two pairings (`FlavourMatrix/CMC_TannicSavoury.json`, `CMC_TannicEarthy.json`), used by Herb Paste.
- **Tree regrowth** (`Patcher/TreeRespawnPatch.cs`): the village locations are cloned from vanilla woodland tiles whose replanting actions the clone cleanup strips. This patch restores regrowth on all twelve.
- **Stray improvement cleanup** (`Patcher/StrayImprovementsPatch.cs`): removes the vanilla Path and Hunting Fences improvements the clones inherited, which would have created unintended travel routes.
- **Iron Fishing Rod catch table** (`Patcher/IronRodFishingPatch.cs`): an extra Fish interaction for the rod on minnow-type water. Vanilla's Fish interaction excludes the rod, so no second button appears.
- **Nightcrawler's burn:** a hidden sunlight-exposure counter whose band fires `SelfTriggeredAction/Tgr_NightcrawlerBurn.json` and the `CMC_EventNightcrawlerBurned` event. `Patcher/TraitCompositeResyncPatch.cs` resyncs the composite trait stats after a light source changes.
- **Village chronicle** (`Patcher/VillageChroniclePatch.cs`): date-stamps each of the seven tracked structures when first found, for the Hall boards.
- **Trading table:** `TradingValues.json`, the 653-item price table, loaded by the framework.
- **Armor and graduate perks:** `Patcher/ArmorCardsPatch.cs` applies bone-tier armor damage reduction through the framework's APIs. The seven graduate perks use `CharacterPerkPerkGroup: "None"`, so they are granted only at runtime.
- **Water source tag:** the built Village Well carries the cross-mod tag `tag_x_WaterSource` by name (no CardTag file ships). Nothing in the suite reads it yet.
- **Data patches** (`GameSourceModify/`): the Stomach stat's Digestion status text, a Sunburn rate change, the Spear Throw success log line and the `GpTag_SpiritHerb` group. `cmcEnvVillagePath.json` adds the Village Path's daytime sounds, and the twelve `cmcLoc*` files patch the cloned map locations.
- **Debug-only code:** `Patcher/AshPartner*.cs` is an unfinished Partner-companion spike for Ash, spawned only when `EnableAshPartnerSpike` is on. `CompanionFollowDiagnostics.cs` and `GuardCombatDiagnosticPatch.cs` only add log output.

## Configuration

Four BepInEx options in the `Debug` section of this mod's file in `BepInEx/config/`. An edit to the file takes effect after a full relaunch. The optional [BepInEx ConfigurationManager](https://github.com/BepInEx/BepInEx.ConfigurationManager/releases) (BepInEx 5 build) changes them in game: press F1 and find "Community Mod Chest". Made there, a change applies at once, except `EnableAshPartnerSpike`, which still needs a relaunch.

| Option | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `EnableJailSafetyNet` | on | Emergency ration top-up if a jailed player's Weight or Hydration drops dangerously low. |
| `ForceClearVillageCrime` | off | Holds Village Crime at 0, to recover a run locked out of the village. Turn it off afterwards. |
| `EnableGuardDiagnostics` | off | Logs each Town Watch guard's duty list every poll. |
| `EnableAshPartnerSpike` | off | Spawns the unfinished Ash Partner test NPC. Not shipped content. |
