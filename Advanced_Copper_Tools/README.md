# Advanced Copper Tools

**Quality of Life & Advanced Metalworking**
**Version:** 1.18.15
**Author:** Jared (crispywhips)
**For:** Card Survival: Fantasy Forest (EA 0.69c)

---

## Overview

Advanced Copper Tools turns copper, and the other metals where it makes sense, into crafted gear for comfort, throughput and light. It runs on [CSFFModFramework](https://github.com/jscott3/CSFF_Mods), so blueprints appear in the right crafting tabs and perks appear on the Situational tab by themselves.

The mod ships 63 blueprints, 74 items, 16 location and structure cards, 7 character perks, 1 effect stat, 1 equipment slot.

| Area | What it covers | Section |
|------|----------------|---------|
| Base materials | Metal Sheet, Metal Nail and Tin Solder in every metal (shipped by the framework), Iron Sheet, cast nails | Base Materials |
| Fire and cooking | Small Copper Stove, Copper Brazier, Copper Tea Kettle, Copper Cauldron, Wearable Metal Pan | Small Copper Stove and following |
| Tools | Large Saw (every metal, iron included), Tool Belt, Cautery Iron, Copper Spigot, Copper Sap Bucket, Grappling Hook, Copper Shears, Copper Watering Can | Large Saw and following |
| Armor | Helmet, Armor, Bracers, Gloves, Greaves and Shield, each one card in copper, tin, ghost bronze, tin bronze, white bronze or iron | Armor, Shields |
| Comfort | Copper Bathtub, Bedwarming Pan, Copper-Rim Chamberpot, Metal Lantern, Wheelbarrow | Copper Bathtub and following |
| Food and drink | Rendered Oil, Tea Station and its three teas (Salt-Cured Meat and Fish now come from CSFF Mod Framework) | Salt-Cured Meat and Fish, Tea Station, Teas |
| Storage | Copper Chest (slow spoilage), Tinned Storage Pot, Ore Bin | Copper Chest, Tinned Storage Pot, Ore Bin |
| Tin | Stream Tin panning with the Wearable Metal Pan, the only source of Tin Ore | Tin Ore |
| Perks | Seven starting-kit perks | Character Perks |

Nearly every finished recipe needs 1 Tin Solder. Tin Ore, the only source of tin, comes from panning river gravel with the Wearable Metal Pan. Sheets, nails, the pan blank and wheel parts do not need solder.

## Base Materials

These blueprints and the Metal Sheet, Metal Nail and Tin Solder cards ship in CSFFModFramework 2.28.0 or later. A save keeps its sheets, nails, solder and researched blueprints. They are listed here because ACT's recipes are built from them.

| Item | Recipe | Build | Research |
|------|--------|------:|-------:|
| **Metal Sheet** | 1 heated bar of copper, ghost bronze, tin, tin bronze or white bronze + hammer (not used up); the sheet takes the bar's metal | 30 min | 4 h |
| **Forged Metal Nail** | 1 copper, tin, bronze or iron nugget + hammer (not used up); the nail takes the nugget's metal | 30 min | 4 h |
| **Cast Copper Nails** | 1 heated copper, tin or bronze bar + 1 mud brick + 1 clay as the mould; makes 6 nails of the bar's metal | 45 min | 4 h |
| **Iron Sheet** | 6 iron nuggets + hammer (not used up); makes a Metal Sheet of iron | 1 h | 4 h |
| **Forge Tin Solder** | 1 tin nugget (smelted from Tin Ore) + hammer (not used up); makes 2 Tin Solder. Researchable once you hold a tin nugget | 1 h | 2 h |

**One sheet card, one nail card.** Each is named and drawn for its metal: Copper, Ghost Bronze, Tin, Tin Bronze, White Bronze or Iron. A Metal Nail weighs 30 whatever its metal. Both sell by their metal, so an iron sheet is worth four times a copper one.

**Old Iron Sheets and Iron Nails** from an earlier save, or from an older Water-Driven Infrastructure Workshop, turn into the new iron cards within 15 minutes. Their **Reforge** button does it at once. Until then they still fit the iron recipes and every nail slot.

**Which sheets fit where**

- Iron recipes take only Iron Sheets.
- The Small Copper Stove, Copper Cauldron, Copper Oil Flask, Forged Pan Blank and Wheelbarrow Bucket take any non-iron sheet, because their products have no iron form.
- The Metal Lantern and Lantern Oilwell take any sheet, iron included, and become an Iron Lantern or Iron Lantern Oilwell from an Iron Sheet.
- The Copper Brazier, Copper Watering Can, Copper Tea Kettle, Copper Chest, Ore Bin, Copper-Rim Chamberpot, Copper Spigot and Copper Sap Bucket take any sheet, iron included.
- A nail of any metal fits every nail slot.
- Each armor piece takes sheets of any one metal, iron included, and comes out in that metal.

**What carries a metal.** The Metal Sheet, Shaped Metal Pan Head, Wearable Metal Pan, Stove Top, Copper Cauldron, Copper Oil Flask, Metal Lantern (all four forms), Lantern Oilwell, Large Saw, nails and Small Copper Stove take their metal's name and picture. Art by Chiwei.

- The saw and stove take the metal of their sheets, and every sheet in the slot must match. A nail takes its nugget's metal. The saw also refuses a nail of another metal than its sheets.
- Nails of every metal work the same, except in the Large Saw, which wants nails of its sheets' metal. The saw and stove differ by metal (see their sections).
- A Shaped Metal Pan Head hotter than 150 Temperature shows one glowing picture whatever its metal.
- Saws, nails, stoves and lanterns from before 1.18.0, any handed out by another mod, and any taken back out of a bathtub or Tea Station are copper.

## Small Copper Stove

A portable fireplace that holds cooking like a campfire. It burns longer than one, makes ash and charcoal, keeps its fuel when picked up, and can be placed anywhere. It weighs 600, and placing or picking it up takes about 3 minutes.

**Recipe** (two stages, 1 h 15 min each). Stage 1 casts the stove top: a small molten crucible (kept, emptied), 2 mud bricks and 2 clay. Stage 2: 4 metal sheets of one metal, 4 copper nails, 1 Tin Solder, and a forge hammer, mallet, stone, antler or shovel (not used up). The stove takes the sheets' metal.

**Research:** holding a Metal Sheet makes the stove researchable (24 hours). A Stove Top Mold or Stove Top you already hold still loads, and the Stove Top's **Cut Up** button turns it into 2 metal sheets of its own metal.

- Holds 600 weight of cooking, like a vanilla Campfire: one cooking pot or two clay bowls. A clay or copper cauldron does not fit.
- Holds 144 fuel (oak +42, twigs +7, charcoal +8), the same scale as a vanilla Campfire (96). The fuel stays in the stove when you pick it up.
- Sanctuary while placed, lit or not: +75 copper, +80 Ghost Bronze, +65 Tin, +75 Tin Bronze, +90 White Bronze.
- A full lit stove burns about 36 hours in copper or White Bronze, 40 in Ghost Bronze, 29 in Tin and 45 in Tin Bronze.
- It keeps its metal through Place, Pick Up, lighting, putting out and burning out. Dismantling returns its 4 sheets in its metal, but not the cast top.
- While lit it dries you out, keeps bugs down, lights the area and makes you easier to spot (Stealth -150), like a vanilla fire. **Warm Up** and **Wait by Fire** warm you beside it.
- It heats any liquid container on it (kettle, wearable pan, clay bowl) and goes into the Copper Bathtub and Tea Station recipes.
- A recruited Partner can be given the vanilla Fire keeping duty on it. Assigning and engaging the duty works. A Partner actually refuelling it is not confirmed yet.

The per-metal Sanctuary and burn times are placeholders for tuning.

## Wearable Metal Pan

A pan you can wear, cook in, and use to purify water on a stove or campfire.

| Step | Recipe | Build |
|------|--------|-----:|
| **Forged Pan Blank** | 1 Metal Sheet of any metal + forge hammer, mallet, stone, antler or shovel (not used up) | 45 min |
| **Wearable Metal Pan** | 1 shaped pan + 3 twine + 1 wood + forge hammer, mallet, stone, antler or shovel (not used up) + 1 Tin Solder | 30 min |

**Research:** holding a Metal Sheet makes the pan researchable (8 hours) and unlocks the Forged Pan Blank outright.

- Put it on a stove or fire to cook in it. A filled pan on a lit fire boils river or dirty water safe, as in vanilla.
- Holds 1200 ml of liquid and 1600 weight. What it holds adds nothing to your load, as with the vanilla Cooking Pot.
- **Melt Fat:** drag animal fat onto the pan once it is at least half hot, while an empty clay bowl is on the board. It makes one Oil and uses up the bowl, which the Oil gives back when you use it.
- Weighs 300 in a bag and nothing when worn, like the vanilla waterskin. Wear it in a quiver slot to carry it hands-free.
- Comes in copper, Ghost Bronze, Tin, Tin Bronze and White Bronze, from whichever sheet you use.
- Dismantles into the shaped pan blank, 3 twine and 1 wood.
- **Pan for Stream Tin** wherever a river is on the board: see Tin Ore.

## Large Saw

A two-handled saw that fells large trees faster than the vanilla advanced axe while it is sharp.

- **Recipe:** 3 metal sheets of one metal + 2 Wood + 2 metal nails + 1 Tin Solder + hammer (not used up). Build 1 hour, research 8 hours. The saw takes the sheets' metal, iron included (the sheets are of one metal, and the 2 nails must be the same metal as the sheets: a nail of another metal is refused).
- **Counts as** an axe and an advanced axe.
- **Bonus:** on a large pine, oak, birch or willow, each pull takes extra Progress on top of the vanilla Cut Tree's -25 and wears the saw by its metal's amount. Vanilla wear is 1 of 100 uses a pull. The numbers are placeholders for tuning.

| Metal | Extra Progress | Per pull | Pine / Willow, Birch, Oak | Wear per pull | Lasts about |
|-------|---------------:|---------:|---------------------------|--------------:|------------:|
| Copper | −12 | −37 | 2, 3, 3 pulls | 1 | 100 pulls |
| Ghost Bronze | −12 | −37 | 2, 3, 3 pulls | 0.6 | 166 pulls |
| Tin | −15 | −40 | 2, 2, 3 pulls | 1.5 | 66 pulls |
| Tin Bronze | −12 | −37 | 2, 3, 3 pulls | 0.5 | 200 pulls |
| White Bronze | −25 | −50 | 1, 2, 2 pulls | 1.25 | 80 pulls |
| Iron | −25 | −50 | 1, 2, 2 pulls | 0.667 | 150 pulls |

**Dullness:** each pull that gets the bonus adds 1 Dullness. At 25 the saw is blunt and fells at the axe rate (2, 3, 4 pulls) until you drag a heavy stone, a stone or a whetstone onto it to **Sharpen** it, as you would a metal axe. Saws made before 1.18.9 start sharp.

The saw cuts small trees like any axe. The bonus and the per-metal wear apply only to the four large trees, and any other axe use wears it 1 whatever its metal.

**Parts' quality:** a saw built since 1.18.15 remembers the average smithing Quality of the 3 sheets and 2 nails it was made from and shows it under its description ("Its sheets and nails were smithed to quality 24."). It changes nothing about how the saw fells or wears. Older saws, and saws from parts without a Quality, show no line. It is kept in CSFF Mod Framework's per-card values (2.29.0), beside the save.

### Iron

The Large Saw in iron (a Large Iron Saw) is the same card: build it from Iron Sheets and Iron Nails by the recipe above, no separate recipe.

- **Durability:** about 150 pulls (wear 0.667 of the card's 100 uses; the copper saw wears 1). It weighs 250 against the copper saw's 375 and sells for 150 more.
- **Bonus:** an extra -25 Progress, so -50 a pull while sharp. Pine and willow fall in 1 pull, birch and oak in 2. It blunts and sharpens like the copper saw.
- **Rust:** an iron saw rusts like iron armor (see Rust and the oil coat); **Grease with Fat** and **Oil** appear on it only when it is iron.
- **Older saves:** an Iron Large Saw (and its recipe) turns into this saw by itself within a quarter of an hour, or at once with **Reforge as Large Saw**, keeping its wear scaled to 100 uses, its Dullness and its warmth. Its Oil Coat does not carry over. A big saw from Additional Processing Equipment loads as this saw (always sharp, wear capped at 100).
- The wear, weight, price and recipe figures are placeholders.

## Armor

### One armor card per piece, in every metal

There are six armor recipes: **Metal Helmet**, **Metal Armor**, **Metal Bracers**, **Metal Gloves**, **Metal Greaves** and **Metal Shield**. Build one from Metal Sheets of a single metal, copper, tin, ghost bronze, tin bronze, white bronze or iron, and the piece comes out in that metal: it is named and drawn for it (Tin Helmet, Iron Armor, White Bronze Shield) and has that metal's protection, weight, price and extras. Armor value stays the same at any durability. Every helmet, armor, bracer, glove and greave has 46080 durability, the same as vanilla leather armor, and it wears the same way: each enemy hit on its body zone wears 250 for every 15 minutes of the action the fight broke into (250 out of a Rest, 1000 out of a Relax). So a piece takes about 185 hits from fights that break into a quarter-hour action, and fewer from fights that break into longer ones. Pieces from before 1.18.9 are raised to 46080 when the save loads.

| Piece | Protection (copper) | Recipe | Build | Research |
|-------|------------|--------|------:|-------:|
| **Metal Helmet** | Head 30 | 2 metal sheets of one metal + 4 metal nails + 1 small leather + 2 sinew + hammer | 1 h | 24 h |
| **Metal Bracers** | Arms 15 each | 2 metal sheets of one metal + 4 metal nails + 1 small leather + 2 sinew + hammer | 1 h | 24 h |
| **Metal Gloves** | Arms 2 each | 1 metal sheet + 2 metal nails + 1 small leather + 1 sinew + hammer | 45 min | 24 h |
| **Metal Greaves** | Legs 15 each | 2 metal sheets of one metal + 4 metal nails + 1 small leather + 2 sinew + hammer | 1 h | 24 h |
| **Metal Armor** | Torso 30 | 4 metal sheets of one metal + 4 metal nails + 1 medium leather + 4 sinew + hammer | 1 h 30 min | 24 h |

The hammer is not used up, and no piece needs Tin Solder. Holding any Metal Sheet makes the recipes researchable. Alloy sheets come from the same **Metal Sheet** blueprint: hammer a heated bar of that metal and the sheet keeps its metal. The bronzes come from the vanilla crucible recipes: Tin Bronze is 5 copper + 1 tin, White Bronze 4 copper + 2 tin, and Ghost Bronze 6 copper + 1 Witchstone (its fumes poison you while it smelts).

| Metal | Head / Torso | Arms / Legs (each) | Durability | Special |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Copper | 30 | 15 | 46080 | the cheap first suit |
| Tin | 32.5 | 16 | 46080 | the lightest metal suit |
| Ghost Bronze | 35 | 16.5 | 46080 | wards against spiritual attacks (47.5 head and torso, 24 each arm and leg); each piece makes Fear fall 0.25 faster every 15 minutes |
| Tin Bronze | 37.5 | 17.5 | 46080 | the middle bronze |
| White Bronze | 42.5 | 19 | 46080 | +3 Courage per piece |
| Iron | 45 | 20 | 46080 | the best raw protection; rusts when wet (below) |

Against an attack that is purely spiritual, Ghost Bronze out-blocks iron. No vanilla animal strikes that way (wolves, bears and boars bite and bash; foxes, badgers and beavers bite and claw), so against wildlife Ghost Bronze is plain bronze armor, and its Fear relief is what sets it apart. Worn armor weighs nothing; carried, the copper pieces weigh 250 / 400 / 200 / 300 (helmet / armor / bracers / greaves), and the other metals as they did before the merge.

**Metal Gloves** are worn in the hands slot, so they go on together with Metal Bracers (and take the place of leather gloves). They keep what makes leather gloves worth wearing for work, the hand protection and hand damage reduction, and add a little arm armor that follows the metal: 2 for copper, 2.2 ghost bronze, 2.1 tin, 2.3 tin bronze, 2.5 white bronze and 2.7 iron, each arm. They give no warmth or comfort, which leather gloves do, and they never wear out by time the way leather does; a hit to an arm wears the gloves and the bracers alike. They have no Fear, Courage or spiritual ward (those stay on the helmet, armor, bracers and greaves). Carried, they weigh 150 in copper, 160 ghost bronze, 140 tin, 160 tin bronze, 165 white bronze and 175 iron, and nothing worn. The numbers are first choices and tunable: `Documentation/Research/AdvancedCopperTools/metal_gloves_balance_2026-10-30.md`. The pictures are recoloured copies of the vanilla leather gloves, stand-ins until Chiwei's glove art arrives.

Iron Armor comes out cut for a man. **Refit for a woman** and **Refit for a man** switch the cut for free, picture included.

Dismantling a piece returns its sheets and 4 nails, all of its own metal, plus its leather and sinew; a furnace melts it back into nuggets of that metal (see Smelting Recovery).

**Armor from before 1.18.13.** Until 1.18.12 every metal had its own card and recipe (twenty-eight of them). A piece you already have becomes the new card of the same metal by itself within a quarter of an hour of game time, keeping its wear, and stays on you if you were wearing it; its **Reforge** button does it at once. Old Iron Armor keeps its cut. An old iron piece's Oil Coat does not carry over, so oil it again. A recipe you had learned for any metal counts for the new recipe of that piece.

With Community Mod Chest installed, the Village Academy's Armorer course hands over the five armor recipes early; without it they are researched normally.

### Rust and the oil coat (iron gear)

Iron armor (Helmet, Armor, Bracers, Greaves), the Iron Shield and an iron Large Saw rust while you wear them (the saw while you hold it), or carry them in a bag you are wearing, and your Wetness is above 50: their durability slowly falls. An **Oil Coat** on the item stops the rust and wears off instead, only while you are wet. Drag Fat or Oil onto the item to give it a fresh coat (**Grease with Fat** or **Oil**, 15 minutes; it uses up the Fat, or the Oil and gives back its Clay Bowl). A coat never restores durability that rust has already taken. Copper, tin and the bronzes do not rust. The rates are early placeholders and may change.

- Rust is checked once per action, so a long sleep right after a swim can still charge a little rust.
- A Partner wearing iron rusts by your Wetness, not theirs.

## Shields

The **Metal Shield** is a wooden shield faced with metal, held in hand like the vanilla wooden Shield. It draws blows toward the shield arm. Build it from 3 metal sheets of one metal + 1 plank + 4 metal nails + 1 medium leather + hammer (build 1 h, research 6 h, researchable once you hold any Metal Sheet); it comes out in that metal. It dismantles (30 minutes) into its own sheets and nails, a plank and a medium leather, all in usable form.

| Shield | Left arm armor (spiritual) | Durability | Weight carried (held) | Smelts to |
|--------|:--:|:--:|:--:|:--:|
| **Copper Shield** | 80 (130) | 5500 | 800 (375) | 25 copper nuggets |
| **Tin Shield** | 82.5 (132.5) | 5500 | 825 (400) | 25 tin nuggets |
| **Ghost Bronze Shield** | 85 (165) | 5500 | 850 (425) | 25 Ghost Bronze nuggets |
| **Tin Bronze Shield** | 87.5 (137.5) | 5500 | 900 (475) | 25 Tin Bronze nuggets |
| **White Bronze Shield** | 92.5 (142.5) | 5500 | 950 (525) | 25 White Bronze nuggets |
| **Iron Shield** | 95 (145) | 5500 | 1000 (575) | 25 iron nuggets |

- The vanilla wooden Shield blocks 75 (125 against spiritual attacks) with 5000 durability. The Iron Shield blocks the most against any blow. The Ghost Bronze Shield blocks a little less overall but wards off spiritual attacks better than any other shield.
- Shields stay in your combat armor after a save loads, like the rest of ACT's armor.
- The Tin, Tin Bronze and White Bronze Shields are new in 1.18.13; their numbers are first picks between the copper, ghost bronze and iron shields and, like the rest of the shield numbers, placeholders for tuning.

## Copper Bathtub

A placed tub for cleaning and morale.

**Recipe** (build 3 hours, research 24 hours): 1 wheelbarrow bucket + 1 Small Copper Stove + 4 planks + 8 mud bricks + 1 large cloth + 4 long sticks + 3 small leather + 1 Tin Solder + hammer (not used up).

- **Empty:** place it anywhere and load fuel before filling. Fill it from a bowl or bucket of water, or straight from the river.
- **Full (cold):** take a Cold Bath, or add firewood and **Light Fire** to heat the water.
- **Warm:** Heat holds up to 96 and drops by 1 every hour. Firewood adds 16, charcoal 8. A **Warm Bath** needs 5% to 50% heat and a **Hot Bath** 50% or more. At 0 Heat the tub turns cold again.

Every bath and the soap wash clean you like vanilla's river and Soap washes:

| Wash | Filth | Also |
|------|------:|------|
| Warm Bath, Hot Bath | -500 | a small morale boost (8 to 12), a spiritual boost, body warmth; Hot Bath also lowers Stress |
| Cold Bath | -400 | a little morale (3 to 5) |
| Wash with Soap | -400 | a little morale (4 to 6); uses a Soap; takes 15 minutes |

All four also take Rash and Wolfsbane Exposure down by 100 and Mud Applied and Soothing Cream Applied by 50, and leave you fully wet. None removes bug repellent or sun protection. Dry off with Wait by Fire at a lit Small Copper Stove or Copper Brazier.

- **Wash with Soap:** drag vanilla Soap onto the warm tub. It works at any heat, so the fire need not be stoked.
- **Other buttons:** Empty Water, Pick Up and Dismantle. Picking the tub up keeps its water, fuel, ash and charcoal.
- **With Herbs and Fungi,** drag a Bath Soak bundle onto the full or warm tub to run Soak in the Tub for a long, calming herbal bath.

## Metal Lantern

A portable light, carried or placed, lit or unlit. Each change between the four forms keeps its fuel.

**Recipe** (build 30 minutes, research 12 hours): 1 metal sheet + 1 Lantern Oilwell + 1 copper wire + 1 parchment + 1 fat (the oiled-parchment shade) + hammer. The lantern takes the sheet's metal and has no glass or stone in it.

- **Fuel:** pour any lamp oil in a clay bowl onto the unlit lantern and you get the bowl back, or drag a Copper Oil Flask onto it. A bowl adds 55.2 fuel, 15% more than the 48 a Ceramic Lamp gets. The flask gives up 48.
- It holds 96 and burns 0.5 every 15 minutes, so a full lantern lasts about 48 hours and two bowls fill it.
- The Lantern Oilwell lights on its own. It holds 96 and burns 1 every 15 minutes (24 hours full) for a smaller light (+10 Light against the lantern's +20).
- Light it by dragging a fire source onto it; it needs at least 10% fuel. When the fuel runs out, a lit lantern goes back to unlit where it stands.
- A recruited Partner can be given the vanilla Fire keeping duty on a placed lantern, but may not refuel it, because only the unlit lantern has the refuel actions.

The fuel, burn and light figures are placeholders for tuning.

## Bedwarming Pan

A copper pan on a long wooden handle that keeps a sleeper warm for one night.

**Recipe** (build 30 minutes, research 4 hours): 1 copper sheet + 1 long stick + 2 copper nails; researchable once you hold a Metal Sheet.

- Drag the cold pan onto a burning fire, or the fire onto it, to fill it with embers. It becomes the Hot Bedwarming Pan.
- Put the hot pan into a log bed or an improvised bed (the bedroll takes it too). While you sleep, Perceived Temperature rises by 3, where a large cloth gives 2 and a medium cloth 1.
- The embers last about ten hours, asleep or not, and then the pan turns back into the cold one. It does nothing outside a bed.

The +3, the ten hours and the recipe are placeholders for tuning.

## Copper-Buckled Tool Belt

A leather belt with a copper buckle that carries your hand tools lighter than your pack does.

**Recipe** (build 45 minutes, research 4 hours): 1 copper sheet + 1 medium leather + 4 copper nails, with a hammer (not used up); researchable once you hold a Metal Sheet.

- **Slot:** its own Tool Belt equipment slot, one belt at a time. You can wear the belt and both sheaths together.
- **Holds** six tools (up to 1200 weight): the vanilla metal hammer, mallet and forge hammer; the metal, butchering, skinning, flint, bone and obsidian knives; the metal chisel, sickle and spoon auger; the fire striker and iron striker; the whetstone; and metal or bone needles. Axes, big saws and other tools stay out.
- **Weight:** the belt weighs 300, and 250 less when worn. What is inside weighs 200 less.
- **Counts as on you:** like the knife sheath, it counts as held in hand when worn, so an action that needs a tool in hand (such as Mark Trees with a knife) finds one inside it.
- **Blueprints still need the tool dragged in.** A blueprint's ingredient slot does not take a tool from your belt, because the game's auto-fill ignores equipped cards.

The weights are placeholders for tuning.

## Cautery Iron

A copper rod with a flat oval head in a wooden handle. It closes a cut with heat instead of a needle.

**Recipe** (build 30 minutes, research 2 hours): 1 heated copper ingot + 1 long stick; researchable once you hold a heated metal ingot.

- Drag the cold iron onto a burning fire, or the fire onto it, to make the Hot Cautery Iron. It stays hot about an hour, and each Cauterize uses 15 minutes of that heat.
- Drag the hot iron onto an open laceration (Arm or Leg Laceration, left or right, or Minor Laceration) and choose **Cauterize** (15 minutes). The cut becomes the stitched form of the same wound and heals on its own, and you take one Fire Burn.
- **Cost:** a Cautery Burn that fades in about 15 hours, and 30 Stress. While the burn lasts, Pain is +15 (Sore) or +30 (Raw). Stress fades by itself.
- A needle and thread stay the painless route. Cauterize does not need the Light or Courage that vanilla stitching does.

The pain, stress and heat time are placeholders for tuning. Confirmed in a scripted run.

## Copper Spigot

A short forged copper spout. A vanilla Sap Tap or Resin Tap runs for one in-game day before it needs a Refresh with a knife. With a spigot fitted it runs about three days.

**Recipe** (build 15 minutes, research 4 hours): 1 copper sheet + 1 Tin Solder; researchable once you hold a Metal Sheet.

- Drag the spigot onto a placed Sap Tap or Resin Tap and choose **Fit Spigot** (15 minutes). The spigot is used up and the tap becomes a Sap Tap (Spigot) or Resin Tap (Spigot), whose flow bar drains at a third of the vanilla rate.
- The container in the tap's slot (such as a clay bowl with its sap) and the flow bar's value carry over. The Refresh action still needs a cutting tool and light.
- **Remove Spigot** turns it back into the plain tap with its container, contents and flow bar, and gives you the spigot back.
- It does not help a tap in another room, and a tap that ran out and was destroyed does not come back.

The one-third rate and the recipe are placeholders for tuning.

## Copper Sap Bucket

A small copper bucket with a wire bail that hangs in a tapped tree. It is how a spigot tap is meant to be collected.

**Recipe** (build 45 minutes, research 4 hours): 2 metal sheets + 1 Tin Solder + hammer (not used up); researchable once you hold a Metal Sheet.

- **Sap:** put it in a Sap Tap, plain or with a spigot. It holds 900, the whole three-day flow of a Sap Tap (Spigot).
- **Resin:** put it in a Resin Tap (Spigot). While the resin flows, a lump gathers in it every 18 hours, up to four. **Scrape Out Resin** on the bucket takes one lump out. A plain vanilla Resin Tap does not fill it.
- Nothing a tap already holds is touched. The Sap Tap still takes any liquid container, and the Resin Tap (Spigot) still makes one loose lump when no bucket hangs there.

Confirmed in a scripted run.

## Grappling Hook

Three curved copper prongs forged onto a shank and tied to a coil of rope.

**Recipe** (build 45 minutes, research 4 hours): 3 copper nuggets + 1 rope, with a hammer (not used up); researchable once you hold a metal nugget.

- While the hook sits in your item row itself, it raises Climbing Aid by 72. On the ground, or packed inside a bag or basket, it does nothing. Every vanilla climb reads Climbing Aid, so trees, cliffs and rock faces all get easier.
- On a large oak with no climbing skill, a fall drops from about 1 in 21 climbs to about 1 in 35.
- The hook never wears out. Climbing Aid tops out at 288, and the squirrel spirit's blessing shares that cap.

The +72 and the recipe are placeholders for tuning.

## Copper Shears (with Sirus23 Mod Collection)

## Oil Chain

The **Oil** card is rendered animal fat or fish. It is both food and lamp fuel.

**Any lamp oil burns** in the lantern, the brazier and the oil flask, not only ACT's. With Herbs and Fungi installed, its hemp seed, linseed, peanut and truffle oils and its seven herbal oils all work. Each clay bowl adds 48 fuel (55.2 in the Metal Lantern) and gives the bowl back.

**Liquid oil from a container** works too: drag a clay bowl, pot or other water container holding melted fat (the base game's rendered fat) or another mod's liquid oil onto the lantern, oilwell, brazier or flask (**Pour Oil from Container**, **Fill from Container** on the flask). Each pour takes up to 300 units, a full clay bowl, and adds the same fuel as a bowl of oil; less liquid adds less fuel. The container stays with you.

| Blueprint | Recipe | Build | Research |
|-----------|--------|------:|------:|
| **Rendered Oil** | 2 animal fat or 2 fatty fish meat + 1 clay bowl | 1 h | 12 h |
| **Lantern Oilwell** | 1 metal sheet + 1 twine + 1 Tin Solder + hammer (not used up) | 30 min | 12 h |
| **Metal Lantern** | 1 metal sheet + 1 Lantern Oilwell + 1 copper wire + 1 parchment + 1 fat + hammer (not used up) | 30 min | 12 h |
| **Copper Oil Flask** | 2 metal sheets + 1 medium leather + 1 Tin Solder + hammer (not used up) | 45 min | 4 h |

The flask holds 4 bowls of oil (192 fuel), enough to refill a lantern twice. Drag it onto a lantern, oilwell or brazier to pour in one bowl's worth: 48 out of the flask, and 48 into the oilwell or brazier or 55.2 into the Metal Lantern.

## Copper Tea Kettle

## Copper Cauldron

A large portable cooking vessel for batch cooking and brewing. Put it into a lit Fireplace or Fire Pit to heat it like the vanilla clay cauldron. The Oven refuses it, as it refuses the clay cauldron. It also fits a Campfire, but fills all 600 of its room, so nothing else fits beside it.

**Recipe** (build 1 hour 30 minutes, research 12 hours): 5 metal sheets + 4 copper nails + 1 Tin Solder + hammer (not used up).

- Weighs 600, half the clay cauldron's 1200.
- An 8000-weight basket for cookable or boilable food and a 6000 ml basin, both 25% more than the clay cauldron. Boiling and brewing recipes need liquid in the basin.
- It cools down when taken off the heat, like vanilla cooking containers.
- With Herbs and Fungi installed it counts as a brewing station, the same as the clay cauldron.

The sizes and weight are placeholders for tuning.

## Tea Station

A workbench with six herb and grinding slots, an 8-bowl water reservoir, a built-in copper stove and a Grind All button.

**Recipe** (build 1 hour, research 24 hours): 2 planks + 4 twine + 1 Copper Tea Kettle + 1 Small Copper Stove + 1 rotary quern + 15 heavy stones. Place the kit to set up the workbench.

| Form | Pick up? | Burns fuel? |
|---------|:--------:|:------------:|
| **Tea Station Kit** | yes | no |
| **Tea Station** (placed, unlit) | yes (slot contents are set down) | no |
| **Tea Station (Lit)** | no, put it out first | yes |

Every change between forms keeps the water and fuel. Lighting and putting out also keep what sits in the six slots. Picking it up does not, because the kit has no slots, so anything still in them is set down beside you.

- **Slots:** fresh herbs dry over time, even unlit, at the vanilla Drying Rack pace. Lighting the stove dries them half again as fast and lets the slots cook, heat and boil.
- **Grind All** grinds everything grindable in the slots at once and takes 15 minutes for each item it grinds, stacked items included. Each powder keeps what a hand grind keeps: freshness, quality and the grind's own quality change.
- **Reservoir (8 Water Charges):** drag clay bowls of water onto the station to fill it, and use **Draw Cold Water** with an empty bowl to take some out.
- **Lit:** the station heats its water from cold to hot in about 30 minutes, and **Draw Boiled Water** works once the reservoir is at least half hot.
- **Lighting:** drag a fire source onto it (it needs at least 10% fuel). Put it out with its button. Pick Up appears only on the unlit station.

## Copper Brazier

A copper fire bowl on a stick tripod (build 1 hour, research 12 hours; 3 metal sheets + 4 copper nails + 3 long sticks + 1 Tin Solder). Researchable once you hold a Metal Sheet. Its blueprint is in both **Construction → Furniture** and **Survival → Fire**.

- **Forms:** Copper Brazier Kit (carried), Copper Brazier (placed) and Lit Copper Brazier.
- **Fuel:** drag any lamp oil in a clay bowl onto the placed brazier. Each bowl adds 48 and you get the bowl back; it holds 96, two bowls. Drag a fire source onto it to light it.
- **Light:** it burns 1.5 every 15 minutes, so a full fill lasts 16 hours.
- **Fire effects:** while lit it dries you out, keeps bugs and mosquitoes down, and makes you easier to spot (Stealth -150). **Warm Up** and **Wait by Fire** warm you beside it.
- **Pack Up:** put it out first, or pack up the unlit brazier. The oil left in it goes back into the kit.
- **Partner duty:** a recruited Partner can be given the vanilla Fire keeping duty on it, but does not refuel it while it is lit, because only the unlit brazier has the refuel actions. Confirmed in a scripted run.

## Salt-Cured Meat and Fish

Since 1.18.15 these two foods come from CSFF Mod Framework (2.29.0 or later) under the same IDs, so a save keeps them, and they are made a new way: put raw meat or raw lean fish in a Clay Bowl of Brine (drop Salt on a bowl of water) holding at least 100, and in about two days it becomes Salt-Cured Meat or Salt-Cured Fish. The old recipes (1 raw meat or lean fish + 2 Salt) are gone; a save that learned them simply no longer lists them. The cure time and the brine it uses are placeholders and may be tuned. Eating them is unchanged: the meat gives Satiation 25, Fullness 20, Calories 25, Proteins 96 and Hydration -5, the fish Satiation 19, Fullness 7, Calories 18, Proteins 96 and Hydration -5.

Allied Partners (Partner Overhaul's or vanilla's) eat the meat, the fish and Oil when hungry. They leave the teas and the Watering Can alone.

The eating values, spoilage and Salty strength are placeholders for tuning.

## Tin Ore

Tin Ore comes only from panning. Wherever a river is on the board, the Wearable Metal Pan offers **Pan for Stream Tin**: an hour of work with a 35% chance of one Tin Ore. Each try wears the pan by 1 of its 100 uses. The Shaped Metal Pan Head cannot pan until it is made into the wearable pan.

Smelt Tin Ore in a forge or furnace at 900°C for tin nuggets (Metal Type 120, Metal Quality taken from the ore's Quality), then forge them into Tin Solder with **Forge Tin Solder**. Up to 1.18.12 a tin ore smelted inside a forge or furnace came out as a copper nugget; since 1.18.13 the ore's own drop sets the metal, with no code involved.

Greenstone, bog iron, salt and stone come from their usual places in the base game.

Water-Driven Infrastructure 1.12.3 or later builds with the same Tin Solder, and any of its old Alloy Solder in a save turns into Tin Solder by itself.

## Harmony Patches

For modders. Each hook is scoped to this mod's own cards unless noted.

- **`SawEffectPatch`**: an ActionRouter `AfterWrapped` handler. Adds the per-metal extra Progress (Large Saw, iron included at -25, the same as white bronze) when a saw is dragged onto one of the four large-tree GUIDs; the retired Iron Large Saw card keeps its own -25 until it reforges.
- **`TeaStationPatch`**: two ActionRouter handlers. Resolves Grind All for both station forms once the action's time is spent, and fixes Draw Boiled Water after JSON fills the bowl.
- **`GameLoadPatch`**: a `LoadMainGameData` postfix. Every Metal Nail slot also accepts an old Iron Nail, and only the Large Saw and the five armor recipes accept an old Iron Sheet.
- **`GameLoadPatch` (armor)**: prefixes `EncounterPopup.GenerateAndApplyPlayerWound` and subscribes to `GameManager.OnGMInitialized`, keeping equipped and carried ACT armor and shields in the game's combat armor list, including after a save loads. It also raises a piece saved before 1.18.9 to leather-parity durability once, on the old card or the merged one.
- **`ArmorWardPatch`**: a postfix on `CardData.ArmorValueMultiplier`, active only while `GenerateAndApplyPlayerWound` is rolling a blow whose every damage type is spiritual. For the five merged armor cards it returns the multiplier that gives each metal its spiritual armor (table in `ArmorWardTable.cs`); any other blow, card or caller is left alone.
- **`HammerGroupPatch`**: fills `GpTag_ACT_ShapingHammer`, the hammer slot of the pan blank, wearable pan and stove blueprints, with the forge hammer, mallet, stone, antler and shovel.
- **`PartnerBlueprintGatePatch`**: a `GameManager.InitializeStatsAndActions` postfix over (blueprint, partner card) rows. Copper Shears needs Sirus23's `sh_shears`. A missing partner hides that blueprint unless it is already researched.
- **`HeatHeldLiquidPatch`**: off by default. Enable `Compatibility.EnableLegacyStationLiquidHeater` only for old beta layouts where a lit Tea Station stores liquid on the station card itself.
- **`VanillaFireKettlePatch`**: the one hook that edits vanilla cards, described under Compatibility.

## Installation

**Requirements:** BepInEx 5.x, CSFFModFramework 2.29.0 or later (it ships the Metal Sheet, Metal Nail and Tin Solder this mod builds with, gives the solder its five uses, moves a save made inside one of the caves removed in 1.18.9 to the Waterfall Caves, and carries the oil tag group that lets the lamps take liquid oil from a container), and Card Survival: Fantasy Forest (EA 0.69c).

1. Install BepInEx if you have not already.
2. Install CSFFModFramework in `BepInEx/plugins/CSFF_Mod_Framework/`.
3. Put this mod's folder at `BepInEx/plugins/Advanced_Copper_Tools/`.
4. Launch the game. `BepInEx/LogOutput.log` should show `Advanced_Copper_Tools v1.18.15 loaded.`

### Configuration

One BepInEx option, in `BepInEx/config/` under the section `Compatibility`:

| Option | Default | Effect |
|--------|---------|--------|
| `EnableLegacyStationLiquidHeater` | `false` | Heats liquid stored directly on a lit Tea Station card, for old beta layouts. Leave it off for the current reservoir. |

The optional [BepInEx ConfigurationManager](https://github.com/BepInEx/BepInEx.ConfigurationManager/releases) (BepInEx 5 build) shows it in game: press F1 and find "Advanced_Copper_Tools". The option is read once at startup, so relaunch the game after changing it there too.

## Compatibility

- Works alongside Herbs and Fungi, Water-Driven Infrastructure, RepeatAction and other framework-based mods. Load order does not matter.
- Needs CSFFModFramework and no other content mod.
- With Sirus23 Mod Collection installed, the Copper Shears shear its tame sheep. Without it their recipe is hidden. ACT changes no Sirus23 file.
- **Vanilla fires:** ACT adds the Copper Tea Kettle and Copper Cauldron to the cards that the Campfire, Fireplace, Fire Pit, their put-out forms, the Hearth, the Awakened Hearth and the lit Oven accept, plus a heating recipe for those two cards. The lit Oven takes the kettle only.
- ACT never changes a fire's weight capacity, never touches the Sauna Stove, and never lets a fire accept or heat other vanilla items.
- Safe to add to an existing save. Safe to remove: the mod's items disappear without corrupting the save.

### Depended on by

- **Water-Driven Infrastructure** (optional): fully playable without ACT, and both mods build with the framework's shared Metal Nail, Tin Solder and Metal Sheet. With ACT installed, WDI's Blast melts ACT's metal items, a lit Copper Brazier counts as winter heat for its stations, its two bronze gears appear, and a running outlet fills an empty placed Copper Bathtub.
- **Community Mod Chest** (optional): loads without ACT. With ACT, the Copper Chest can stand in for the Storage Chest in the Market Stall kit, and the Village Academy's Armorer course hands over ACT's five armor recipes early.

## Troubleshooting

**Blueprints not appearing?** Check that CSFFModFramework loaded: `LogOutput.log` should have `[CSFFModFramework]` lines and `Advanced_Copper_Tools v1.18.15 loaded.`

**Pan or kettle won't boil?** It must sit on a lit fire with fuel left, and hold a liquid the game can heat.

**Stove fuel not going down?** That is correct on the unlit stove. Once you light it by dragging a fire source onto it, it burns fuel at the normal rate.

**Tea Station won't pick up?** Pick Up appears only on the unlit station, so put it out first. Anything left in the six slots is set down beside you.

**Items show `[MISSING]` text?** `Localization/SimpEn.csv` is missing or damaged. Extract the mod folder again.
