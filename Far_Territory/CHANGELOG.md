# Changelog

## [0.1.7] - 2026-10-15

A balance pass to bring Far Territory in line with the base game instead of outdoing it.

### Fixed

- **A forage, wash, dive or search can come up empty again.** Every "nothing" outcome was being skipped by the game, so every island forage always found something, and a wash at Coral Inlet always ended in a shark or sea krait bite or an urchin, never the plain wash it should almost always be. All 82 of them now count.
- **Moose, walrus and seal give a hide.** Their six (or two) Fresh Skins were the scrap kind. They now give one Fresh Skin that fleshes into a very large hide (moose, walrus) or a large hide (seal).
- **Rust Grotto bog iron has a quality.** It now gives Fresh Bog Iron at the base game's quality, which you dry yourself, instead of quality-less Dried Bog Iron.

### Changed

- **Garden plants need care.** A Far Territory plant in the Garden Plot now has Health and Care, like the base game's field crops: **Tend** it once a day. Left untended it dies in about ten days, and in winter it stops growing and dies in about two. A dead plant becomes a **Dead Plant** you clear away. Plants already in a plot load healthy.
- **Raw seafood carries the base game's raw-fish risks:** parasites, a little filth and fish fatigue, and no pleasure in eating it. Shellfish carry more parasites, and Oyster and Conch can upset your stomach.
- **Sea fishing is about half as rich.** Each species bites half as often on a line, net or spear, and each catch takes twice as much from the stock. The Fish Trap is unchanged but takes 12 hours to research instead of 2.
- **The outrigger crossing takes effort.** It costs 2 stamina every 15 minutes and you arrive wet. About 1 crossing in 20 is rough, and in winter about 1 in 5 meets a storm; you always arrive.
- **Large carcasses give real meat.** A moose gives 40 to 50 Meat and 6 to 8 Fat Chunks, a walrus 30 to 40 Meat and 6 to 8 Fat Chunks, and both now rot in 3.75 days instead of 3.
- **The carcass searches run on a stock.** Search the Grazing Grounds and Search the Snowfield each work once per 3 days (a Herd Sign bar). With Harsh Wilds installed a wisent turns up about 2 searches in 100 and a winter musk ox about 3 in 100.
- **Island warmth follows the season:** +9 in spring and autumn, +5 in winter, and in summer +13 at midday (11:00 to 16:00). Fire Mountain and Sour Crater warm you by +10 instead of +16.
- **Three copper veins instead of seven** (Greenstone Grotto, Glittering Hall and Middle Hall). Caves you had already visited keep the veins they had.
- **Washing in the sea** removes 300 filth and eases a rash a little less, like washing in river water.
- **Seashells**: about every second handful you grind makes a Bonemeal.
- **The Coconut Shell Bowl** no longer catches rain, and what it holds slowly dries off like a clay bowl's.

### Added

Needs CSFF Mod Framework 2.28.3.

### Changed

## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-12

First public release, as a pre-release: on GitHub, on Nexus Mods and in the Mod Update Manager suite. The changes since the earlier suite builds come first; everything the mod adds is under Added.

### Changed

- Card art in the vanilla form: 57 card pictures are now cropped to the subject and saved as small palette images, the way the game's own art is, so each item fills its card like a vanilla item does and the pictures take about a sixth of the space they did.

- **Every fishing tool now works on the sea** (owner, 2026-10-11: "all fishing implements must be accepted"). **Cast the Net** on every sea card takes Community Mod Chest's Fishing Net (or any card tagged `tag_FishingNet`): the line's cost and odds, two of a fish per catch, and the net loses a cast. **Set the Funnel Trap** on the four calm coasts takes the base game's Funnel Trap: it fishes like the set Fish Trap, and **Haul Out the Trap** gives the Funnel Trap back with its catch spilled beside it (new card `ft_funnel_trap_placed`). Lines, rods and spears were already accepted by their base-game tags, so Community Mod Chest's Iron Fishing Rod and Fire-Hardened Spear already worked; the README now says so. Spear fishing and both traps stay on the calm coasts only, as before. Not yet confirmed in game.

- **The climate no longer uses cards.** Island Air, Mountain Air, Fjord Chill and Volcanic Heat are gone: they sat on every board as visible cards and did not reliably change how warm you feel. The mod now adjusts the base game's Temperature Perceived (and hides snow on the island and the volcano) itself, from the place you are standing on, with the same numbers as before: island +9, volcano +16 (and no snow on either), mountains -6, fjords -8, caves unchanged. It stops the moment you leave, and a save that held the old cards simply loses them. The island is still milder than the forest in winter.

- **The Wild Owl (Sirus23 Mod Collection) can no longer fly onto the island.** Its night visit teleports it to whichever board you are on, which put it on Cliffbound Cove. Far Territory now refuses that move onto every island board (`Patcher/OwlIslandGuard.cs`); with Sirus23 absent it does nothing. An owl already standing on an island board in an older save is not removed by this. Not yet confirmed in a real night on the island.

- **Our own card art for nearly every Far Territory card, in the style of the base game's cards** (owner-picked, 2026-10-10 and 2026-10-11): the coast, the sea cards and the island shores; the island interior boards and caves; the palms, coconuts, sea fish, tropical foods, medicine, spice and fibre plants with their garden and growing stages; the island animals, their carcasses and the seagull nest; the canoe, Homeward Sand, the grassland, the mountains and their caves, the hot spring, salt seam, rope and pitons, and the fjords with their animals. No footprints in any scene. The deep cave halls are drawn without torches, the Lightless Hall in near-black. A felled palm is just its stump on the beach. Only the effect icons stay on fitting base-game icons. The Fish Trap, the set trap and the two Sinkhole climbs got their own pictures last (2026-10-11); only the River Mouth's map picture is still the base game's river shore. No card data changed beyond the pictures.

- **New pictures for Landfall Strand (no footprints in the sand), the landing beach and the shell-strewn sand, closer to the base game's card art.** No card data changed.

- **Seashells picture redrawn closer to the base game's card art.** No card data changed.

### Fixed

- **Leaving a dark cave now ends the dark.** The Cave Darkness card of every dark cave you had visited kept its in-a-dark-place mark on you after you left, one more for each dark hall (six after walking the chamber chain), so daylight stopped counting toward Light everywhere. Each cave's darkness now ends when you leave it: the mark is 1 inside a dark cave and 0 outside (measured on the harness, T2.1395 and T2.1396).
- **Footwear now keeps every urchin spine out.** The urchin outcome of a wash or a dive weighs one more than its numbers suggest (the game counts a zero base weight as 1), and footwear took off only the population part, so Leather Shoes still let the odd spine through on Coral Inlet, Boulder Shore, Bleak Strand and Cliffbound Cove (measured on the harness, T2.1399). Footwear (FootProtection 5 or more) now takes the whole weight away, as the README always said; barefoot nothing changes.
- **The Fish Trap now keeps what it catches.** Neither the trap nor the set trap had anywhere to hold a fish (no slots, no weight capacity), so a set trap never caught anything you could haul out (a 400-tick harness wait saw no catch at all). Both now hold four fish (tunable), and Haul Out the Trap still carries the catch over.
- **The Fish Trap blueprint can be researched.** It had no unlock condition at all, which the game treats as never researchable, so the Fish Trap could not be made in play. It is now researchable from the start, as its description always said.

- Island Air is milder: it now raises how warm you feel by 9 instead of 30 (tunable). At +30 a forest-winter body sweated after four hours on the island, because +30 lifted a cold winter morning into the base game's Extremely Hot band; +9 lifts it into the Comfortable band instead, so the body holds steady on the island while it grows numb in the forest. The card's description no longer promises high summer.
