## Card art (`Resource/Picture/`)

The pictures come from two sources.

**Card Survival: Tropical Island.** From version 0.1.13, 111 pictures are that game's own card art,
used with the permission of the Card Survival developers (Plissken, replying for the developers on
2026-10-19: "you can use CSTI's art for it, no problem"). They are the island's places, paths and
sea, its plants and the island food, fish and finds that game draws. Each is the Card Survival:
Tropical Island card sprite enlarged from 341x512 to the 512x768 card canvas and stored as a palette
PNG, with nothing else changed. `.art/<picture>.json` names the sprite each one comes from. The
permission covers that game's art in this mod only: no text or data from Card Survival: Tropical
Island is included, and the art is not offered for any other use.

**Made for this mod.** Every other picture (the mainland and its animals, the boats, the animated
places, and the island cards that keep a picture of their own) was generated with the Nano Banana 2 image model through
OpenArt from a written description of the card, chosen by the maintainer, and then finished in
GIMP: the white paper keyed to transparency and the subject framed on the card canvas. Exported
base-game card sprites were passed to the model as style references, so the pictures follow the
base game's flat, outlined look; no base-game image is copied into this package. The generation
record for each of these pictures (its prompt and model) is in `.art/`, one JSON file per image.

The island recreates Card Survival: Tropical Island's island as a tribute. Its names, its map's
links and the card art above are carried over; every description and number here is new.

## Base-game sprites used directly

A few cards draw a sprite that ships with Card Survival: Fantasy Forest itself, referenced by name
and never included in this package: the River Mouth's paper-map picture (`RiverShore`) and the
status icons of the remedy effects.

## Adding artwork

When adding external, commissioned, AI-generated, or derived artwork, record the source,
author/tool, license or terms, and any attribution text here before publishing the mod package.
