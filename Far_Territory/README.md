# Far Territory

**Version:** 0.1.5
**Author:** Jared
**Requires:** CSFFModFramework 2.28.0 or later
**Status:** pre-release. Every number below is a placeholder to be tuned, not a balanced value.

Far Territory adds lands beyond the forest, reached by ordinary travel. Follow the river to the sea and cross to a warm island that recreates Card Survival: Tropical Island's island as a tribute (all text and art are new). Past the forest lie a grassland, mountains and fjords.

- **Coast and island:** three new shore places at the river's end, an outrigger crossing, and an island with seven shore places, a gull islet, a whole interior, a volcano and sixteen caves (eight of them a chain of dark halls).
- **Far lands:** a grassland west of the forest with a moose, snowy mountains north of it with a hot spring and rock salt, and cold fjords beyond with seals, walrus and puffins.
- **Things to do:** fish every coast, climb and fell coconut palms, gather tropical food, medicine, spice and fibre plants (all also grown in the base game's Garden Plot), and fight island animals.

Every card has its own picture except Brackwater Mouth's map picture, the set Funnel Trap and the effect icons, which use the base game's art.

## Getting there

Follow the river south to Duck Point, which now has a **South** exit. Each step east is one compass hop, and all three places show on the paper map once visited.

| Place | What is there |
|---|---|
| **Brackwater Mouth** (south of Duck Point) | The estuary. Still river water, so it keeps Duck Point's river and mosquitoes. A reed patch, a stone pile, a driftwood log. |
| **Driftwood Shore** (east) | A shingle shore with a stone pile, a driftwood log and Forage. |
| **Seaward Sands** (east again) | A wide beach: shell-strewn sand, a driftwood log, Forage, the sea (wash, dive) and a tide pool. |

### The dugout canoe

A **Dugout Canoe** takes you down the river in one paddle. Its blueprint is in Construction, Advanced Tools: 4 hours to research, unlocked once a Tree Log is with you. Build it from a Tree Log and 2 Oak Bark with a cutting tool such as a flint knife (2 hours). The tool wears a little.

- **Paddle Downstream** appears on any place with a flowing river, other mods' river places included.
- It takes about an hour to Brackwater Mouth, costs 2 stamina every 15 minutes and wets you a little.
- Walking from River Pass is 14 hops. The canoe is faster from Mossy Bank and above; from Muddy Bank, Willow Ways, Clay Banks and Duck Point, walking is as quick or quicker.
- It only goes downstream, so you walk back. The button is missing on lakes, marshes, bogs, swamps, the coast and Brackwater Mouth, and in winter, when the river freezes.
- The canoe is as heavy as a tree log. Keep it with you: one set down on a river place stays there.

## Crossing to the island

An **Outrigger Canoe** lies on Seaward Sands. **Set Sail for the Island** takes you to **Landfall Strand** in 6 hours, with no tool, stamina check or season needed. A second canoe on Landfall Strand sails back with **Set Sail for the Mainland**.

- The canoe is the only way across, and the paper map draws no line over the sea.
- A Partner following you stays behind on Seaward Sands. Animals and other people cannot cross.
- The canoes and the shores they leave from (**Misty Coast**, **Homeward Sand**) cannot be trashed, and a missing one comes back when you arrive on its beach.

### Getting around

- **Compass hops** take 15 minutes and work both ways. The links are: Landfall Strand to Coral Inlet and Boulder Shore; Coral Inlet to Vine Woods; Vine Woods to Westgrass Plain and Reed Marsh; Rootwater Thicket to Westgrass Plain; Westgrass Plain to Eastgrass Plain and Western Uplands; Western Uplands to Eastern Uplands and Misty Ridge; Eastgrass Plain to Eastern Uplands and Bleak Strand; Eastern Uplands to Fire Mountain; Fire Mountain to Sour Crater; Reed Marsh to Green Deep; Green Deep to Misty Ridge and Hidden Vale.
- **Paths** are cards that carry you with one action. The two long shore walks (Coral Inlet to Rootwater Thicket to Bleak Strand), Palm Fringe to Vine Woods and the slope between Bleak Strand and Fire Mountain take half an hour. The short ways to Palm Fringe, Wreck Hollow, Fallen Dwelling and the caves take no time.
- **Swims** run between Gull Stack and each of Boulder Shore, Bleak Strand and Cliffbound Cove. A swim takes half an hour, needs Light 10 and Stamina 12, soaks you, costs 5 stamina every 15 minutes and trains Swimming. No Swimming skill is needed. Gull Stack has no other way on or off.
- **Climbs** link Reed Marsh and Cliffbound Cove with Misty Ridge, and the Sinkhole with the caves below. A climb takes 15 minutes, needs Stamina 11, costs 10 stamina, wears your hands and feet and trains Climbing.
- A climb always gets you there, but it can go wrong. Climbing skill and a climbing aid make a clean climb likelier, and the other outcome is a fall that scrapes or bruises you. A climber under Stamina 16 always falls.

The paper map draws no line for a path, swim or climb. Each place appears on it once visited.

### Caves

The island has sixteen caves, all off the paper map and all at the forest's temperature rather than the island's. You enter a single cave by a path card outside (for example **Path to Sea Grotto** on Boulder Shore) and leave by a card inside.

| Cave | Off | Forage |
|---|---|---|
| **Sea Grotto** | Boulder Shore | stones, heavy stones, seaweed, crabs |
| **Wave Grotto** | Bleak Strand | crabs, prawns, seaweed, sea urchins, stones |
| **Plain Grotto** | Westgrass Plain | stones, bugs, bones |
| **Bat Roost** | Misty Ridge | niter crystals, bones, stones |
| **Monkey Den** | Misty Ridge | bones, feathers, coconuts and more |
| **Black Grotto** | Reed Marsh | stones, bugs, heavy stones (dark) |
| **Sinkhole** | Western Uplands, by a climb | stones, heavy stones, bones |
| **Lava Tube** | Eastern Uplands | stones, heavy stones, flint |

Only the Black Grotto and the eight halls below are dark. In the dark the screen dims, daylight stops counting toward Light, and Forage needs a lit torch, lantern or brazier. The dark ends when you leave. Every cave's forage is a first draft.

**The chamber chain.** Eight dark halls join the surface at the Black Grotto, the Sinkhole, the Lava Tube and Cliffbound Cove, so you can walk underground between Reed Marsh, Cliffbound Cove, Western Uplands and Eastern Uplands.

- Black Grotto, a few steps to the **Dripping Hall** (a Spring that fills with water), then 15 minutes to **The Squeeze** (a Copper Vein).
- The Squeeze to the **Lower Hall** by a half-hour **Narrow Crack** crawl that costs 15 stamina, adds filth and scrapes your hands.
- Lower Hall: a few steps to the **Glittering Hall** (Copper Vein, crystal, now and then a Geode), with a shaft down to the **Drowned Hall** (still sea water: crabs, prawns).
- Shafts climb from the Lower Hall to the **Middle Hall** and on to the **Upper Hall**, a few steps from the Lava Tube. Middle and Upper have Copper Veins.
- The Middle Hall is a few steps from the **Lightless Hall**, which is a few steps from the Sinkhole. Little grows there.
- An **Underwater Passage** joins the Drowned Hall and Cliffbound Cove: half an hour, Stamina 12, soaking, 5 stamina every 15 minutes. It needs no Swimming skill or daylight.
- Shaft climbs follow the climb rules above (up costs 5 stamina, down 10) and need no daylight. Mine the Copper Veins with a pickaxe for greenstone. A mined-out vein does not return.

## Climate

The mod adjusts how warm you feel (the base game's Perceived Temperature) from the place you stand on. It uses no cards, and it stops the moment you leave.

| Place | Change |
|---|---|
| Island places | +9, and no snow shows |
| Fire Mountain and Sour Crater | +16, and no snow shows |
| Scree Slope, Steaming Shelf, Windgap Col | -6 |
| The three fjord places | -8 |
| Caves | none |

- A forest winter's day becomes comfortable on the island, but a forest summer is hotter there and winter nights stay chilly.
- Weather, season and day length are the forest's own: a forest blizzard still shows on the island, though no snow lies there. The forest itself is unaffected.
- Fire Mountain can overheat you, more so in summer.

**Other mods.** With Sirus23 Mod Collection installed, its wild sheep can turn up on island places. Its wild **Owl**, which flies to wherever you are at night, is kept off every Far Territory board, so it never turns up on a beach, in a cave or on the plains. Without that mod this does nothing, and an owl already on a Far Territory board in an older save stays until dawn. This has not been watched through a real night yet.

## Shore and sea cards

- **Driftwood Log**: **Haul Out** turns it into a Tree Log with oak values.
- **Shell-Strewn Sand**: **Comb the Sand** gives 1 to 3 Seashells. It refills about 3 days per comb.
- **Seashells**: drag a Stone onto them to grind them into Bonemeal (half an hour).
- **The Sea**: **Wash in the Sea** (15 minutes) and **Dive for Shells** (half an hour, 4 stamina every 15 minutes, wets you). A dive brings up Seashells, Seaweed or nothing, plus shellfish. There is no salt water or tide.
- **Tide Pool** (Seaward Sands, Boulder Shore, Bleak Strand, Gull Stack): **Forage the Pool** gives a Shore Crab, Rock Prawns or Seaweed, needs daylight and stamina, and restocks in about 2.5 days per forage.
- **Seaweed**, **Shore Crab** and **Rock Prawn** can be eaten raw (spoiling in 2 to 3 days). There are no cooked island foods yet.
- Every island coast has its own sea card with the same wash and dive: **The Reef Water**, **The Rocky Swell**, **The Grey Surf**, **The Brackish Shallows** and **The Cove Water**. Each coast's dive finds its own shells, seaweed, stones, flint or crabs.
- **Forage** works on every Far Territory place, and Forage and combing need some stamina. Outside they need daylight, in the dark caves a light, and in the other caves neither. Each forage uses part of a Gleanings bar that refills over time.

## Fishing

Every fishing tool works on every sea card except the fjords, which have no fishing. Drag one onto the sea.

- **Fish with a Line**: the base game's Fishing Line or Fishing Rod, Community Mod Chest's Iron Fishing Rod, or any card tagged as a fishing line. It takes half an hour and trains Fishing. The tool loses 1 use, and a cast can bring up nothing.
- **Cast the Net**: Community Mod Chest's Fishing Net or any fishing-net card. It lands two fish per catch, and the net loses a cast.
- **Spear Fish**: any spear, including the base game's and Community Mod Chest's Fire-Hardened Spear. It takes half an hour, needs daylight and stamina and trains Spear Fishing. A spear misses Silver Herring and Mudskipper.
- **Set the Fish Trap** (the Fish Trap card) or the base game's **Funnel Trap** (**Set the Funnel Trap**) takes 15 minutes and Stamina 10.
- Spear fishing and both traps work only on the calm coasts: Seaward Sands, Coral Inlet, Rootwater Thicket and Cliffbound Cove.

| Coast | Catch |
|---|---|
| Seaward Sands | Goatfish, Parrotfish, Silver Herring |
| Coral Inlet (richest) | Goatfish, Parrotfish, Bonefish, Reef Grouper, Silver Herring |
| Boulder Shore, Gull Stack | Goatfish, Parrotfish, Bonefish, Reef Grouper |
| Bleak Strand | Goatfish, Parrotfish, Bonefish, Silver Herring |
| Cliffbound Cove | Goatfish, Parrotfish, Bonefish, Reef Grouper |
| Rootwater Thicket | Goatfish, Parrotfish, Bonefish, Threadfin, Mudskipper |

**Stocks.** Each species has a hidden stock that sets how likely it is to bite. Every catch lowers it, and time restores it: about 5 days per catch for the common fish, 10 for Reef Grouper, Threadfin and Conch, and about 21 for Oyster and Sea Urchin. All coasts share the stocks.

**Eating.** Sea fish (12 to 50 satiation) spoil in 2 days. Shellfish (Conch, Oyster, Sea Urchin, 12 to 20 satiation) come from dives and spoil in 2.5 days. Seaward Sands gives Conch and Sea Urchin, Coral Inlet adds Oyster, Boulder Shore and Cliffbound Cove give Conch and Sea Urchin, and Bleak Strand gives Conch. None has a cooked form yet.

**Fish Trap** (Hunting, Fishing blueprint): 2 hours to research, 1.5 hours to build from 4 Twigs and 3 Twine. Set on the sea, it becomes a **Fish Trap (Set)** that springs every 24 to 30 hours. **Haul Out the Trap** (15 minutes) gives you the trap back with its catch, which keeps fresh inside. It holds four fish and never wears out. The **Funnel Trap (Set)** catches the same way, and its haul-out spills the catch onto the shore because the base game's trap holds only Perch.

Net and funnel-trap fishing have not been confirmed in game yet.

## Coconuts and palms

- **Coconut Palm** (Landfall Strand, Coral Inlet, Boulder Shore, Gull Stack, two on Palm Fringe): holds up to 4 coconuts and grows one back every 7.5 days.
- **Climb the Palm** (half an hour, needs a ripe coconut, daylight and Stamina 11) twists one loose. It follows the climb rules above, and a fall loses the nut.
- **Fell the Palm** (any axe, half an hour) gives 2 to 4 Green Coconuts and 6 to 10 Palm Fronds. A shoot rises from the stump after 10 days as a **Young Palm**, which grows into a palm in 30 more. A Young Palm can be cut with any blade for 2 to 3 Palm Fronds.
- **Green Coconut** (spoils in 20 days): drag a knife or axe onto it to **Husk the Coconut** (15 minutes) for a Husked Coconut and 2 Fibers.
- **Husked Coconut**: drag a hammer, hard hammering stone or axe onto it to **Crack Open** (15 minutes) for 1 Coconut Water (Thirst +90), 2 Coconut Meat (Satiation +28) and a **Coconut Shell Bowl**.
- **Coconut Shell Bowl**: a small water container that catches rain like the base game's Rain Catcher. This has not been watched in a real shower yet.
- **Palm Frond**: weak fuel. Whether a campfire accepts it has not been confirmed in game, and fuel is its only use.

## Tropical food plants

Seven wild plants stand on the island where the original game places them. Each gives a raw food with its own button, and each yields 2 or 4 times before it needs 30 days to regrow while you are on that board. All can be grown in the base game's Garden Plot.

| Plant | Where | Action | Gives |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Banana Plant** | Vine Woods, Green Deep, Reed Marsh | **Cut a Bunch** | 3 to 4 Bananas |
| **Mango Tree** | Misty Ridge | **Pick Mangoes** | 1 to 2 Mangoes |
| **Tropical Almond Tree** | most island places | **Gather Almonds** | 2 to 3 Tropical Almonds (keep 30 days) |
| **Wild Jujube** | Eastgrass Plain | **Pick Jujubes** | 2 to 4 Jujubes |
| **Nipa Palm** | Rootwater Thicket | **Cut a Fruit Head** | 1 Nipa Fruit |

It grows only while it sits there, in every season, and ignores the plot's watering, nutrients, acidity and pests. **Harvest** takes 15 minutes and **Uproot** removes it.

|---|---|---|---|

## Medicine and spice plants

Eight wild plants stand where the original game places them. Each has a pick, cut or dig button (15 minutes, half an hour for roots and bark, and digging adds a little filth), holds 2 or 4 yields and regrows in 30 days while you are on that board.

| Plant | Where | Gives |
|---|---|---|
| **Ginger Plant** | Rootwater Thicket, Reed Marsh | 1 to 2 Ginger Root (**Dig up Ginger**) |
| **Kava Plant** | Vine Woods, Green Deep | 1 Kava Root (**Dig up Kava Root**) |
| **Chilli Plant** | Westgrass and Eastgrass Plains | 2 to 4 Chillies (**Pick Chillies**) |
| **Lemongrass** | Landfall Strand, Coral Inlet, Palm Fringe, Westgrass Plain, Western Uplands, Hidden Vale | 2 to 3 Lemongrass (**Cut Lemongrass**) |
| **Jasmine Bush** | Green Deep | 2 to 3 Jasmine Flowers; or a Jasmine Cutting for a garden |
| **Aloe Vera** | Landfall Strand, Coral Inlet, Palm Fringe, Bleak Strand, both Plains, both Uplands | 1 Aloe Vera Leaf (**Cut an Aloe Leaf**) |
| **Cinchona Tree** | Vine Woods, Misty Ridge | 1 to 2 Cinchona Bark (keeps 60 days); or a Seedling for a garden |
| **Coffee Bush** | Misty Ridge | 3 to 5 Coffee Berries (**Pick Coffee Berries**) |

**Remedies.** Using one raises a Far Territory effect that falls by 4 an hour. While it is up, it changes the base game's stats below. Nothing writes your immune system or pain directly, so doses do not stack for the rest of the run.

| Effect | Source | While it is up | At a high level (25 or more) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Aloe Salve** | Apply the Gel (Aloe Vera Leaf) | Pain -25, burns heal 4 an hour faster | Pain -50, burns heal 8 an hour faster |
| **Cinchona Bitters** | Chew the Bark | fever falls 40 an hour faster, Pain -25 | fever 80 an hour faster, Pain -50 |
| **Kava Calm** | Chew the Root | stress falls 4 an hour faster, Pain -25 | stress 8 faster, Pain -50, you grow drowsy |
| **Coffee Buzz** | Eat the berries | wakefulness rises 6 an hour faster, you grow stressed and thirsty 1 an hour faster | all three doubled (at 13 or more) |
| **Ginger Warmth** | Chew the Root | nausea falls 2 an hour faster, Pain -10 | none |

Cinchona Bitters brings a fever down. It does not cure the base game's Swamp Fever infection.

**Spices.** Ginger Root, Chillies, Lemongrass and Jasmine Flowers carry the base game's flavour tags (Pungent, Spicy, Herbal, Sour, Floral). Whether a base-game dish picks these up has not been checked in game.

**In a garden.** Ginger Root (15 days, 2 to 4, used up), Kava Root (30 days, 1 to 2, used up), Chillies (15 days, 3 to 5), Lemongrass (10 days, 3 to 5), Jasmine Cutting (20 days, 3 to 5), Aloe Vera Leaf (20 days, 2 to 3), Cinchona Seedling (30 days, 2 to 3 bark) and Coffee Berries (30 days, 4 to 6) take root like the food plants. All but the first two stay and grow again.

## Fibre and flowering plants

Four more wild plants hold 4 yields each and regrow in 30 days while you are on that board.

- **China Rose** (Misty Ridge): **Pick China Roses** gives 2 to 3 flowers to eat (Satiation +1). **Take a China Rose Cutting** gives a cutting for a garden.
- **Spider Lily** (Reed Marsh): **Cut Spider Lily Leaves** gives 1 to 2 poisonous leaves. **Lift a Spider Lily Bulb** gives a bulb for a garden. **Bind as a Poultice** on a leaf raises **Lily Poultice**, which gives Pain -20 and falls by 4 an hour.
- **Weevil Lily** (Vine Woods, Green Deep): **Cut Weevil Lily Leaves** gives 2 to 3 leaves. **Dig up a Weevil Lily Rhizome** gives one for a garden. **Strip into Fibers** turns a leaf into 1 Fibers.
- **Snake Grass** (Vine Woods, Green Deep, Misty Ridge, Rootwater Thicket, Reed Marsh): **Cut Snake Grass** gives 2 to 3 blades. **Strip into Fibers** turns a blade into 2 Fibers.

The Fibers are the base game's own card: they make Twine, feed a fire as tinder and can be combed like any other. All four plants take root in the Garden Plot and keep growing: Snake Grass 10 days (3 to 5), Spider Lily 15 days (2 to 3 leaves), Weevil Lily 15 days (3 to 5 leaves), China Rose 20 days (3 to 5).

## Animals

Five island animals roam their own boards. Meet one with **Approach** and fight it with the base game's combat. Every bite lands as the base game's **Bite Wound**. There is no venom, and no animal raids your food.

Fights are sized like the base game's animals of similar size: a seagull or bat barely frightens you. Killing small game satisfies the Fox Urge, and the Wolf Urge eases only a little.

| Animal | Where | When | A kill leaves |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Macaque** | Monkey Den, Misty Ridge, Green Deep, Reed Marsh, Vine Woods | 06:00 to 19:00; by night in the Monkey Den | Macaque Carcass |
| **Monitor Lizard** | Westgrass and Eastgrass Plains, Rootwater Thicket, Bleak Strand, Plain Grotto | 07:00 to 18:00; by night in Plain Grotto | Monitor Lizard Carcass |
| **Cobra** | Westgrass and Eastgrass Plains, both Uplands | 06:00 to 20:00 | Cobra Carcass |
| **Seagull** | Gull Stack, Boulder Shore, Bleak Strand | 05:00 to 20:00; by night on Gull Stack | Bird Meat, Feathers, Bird Bones |
| **Bat** | Bat Roost | always | Bat Carcass |

- The **cobra** strikes on its own, once a day between 08:00 and 18:00, when it is on your board. The others fight only when approached.
- The macaque, monitor lizard and cobra leave the base game's tracks. A killed animal comes back after a week (seagull and bat after three days).
- **Carcasses**: drag a knife onto one to **Butcher** it (15 minutes) for raw Meat (macaque 2 to 3 and a Bone, monitor lizard 2 and a Bone, cobra 1, bat 1). They rot in about 2.5 days.
- **Seagull Nest** (Gull Stack): **Search the Nest** (15 minutes, needs daylight and stamina) gives Wild Eggs, feathers and twigs, or Bird Bones. It can be searched twice from full and refills over 10 days.

## Sea dangers

The island's seas bite back, but Seaward Sands stays safe. Each danger is one more outcome of washing, diving or spear fishing, and each lands as a base game wound. There is no shark fight and no poison.

| Danger | Where | Harm |
|---|---|---|
| **Shark** | every island sea (rare when washing, likelier when diving or spearing) | Bite Wound |
| **Sea Krait** | Coral Inlet and Rootwater Thicket | Bite Wound |
| **Urchin underfoot** | Coral Inlet, Boulder Shore, Gull Stack, Bleak Strand, Cliffbound Cove | Puncture Wound, and you keep the Sea Urchin |

- **Footwear** keeps the urchins out. With Foot Protection 5 or more (Bark Sandals, Leather Shoes) you never take the spine: from 5 to 10 you tread on the urchin, your footwear takes the spines and you keep the urchin, and above 10 you never meet it.
- The same footwear, or any Leg Protection from another mod, keeps the sea krait off you. On a dive, Swimming skill lowers its chance too.
- The only defence against the shark is staying out of the water.

## The grassland

Open grassland well west of the forest, with the forest's own weather and seasons.

**Getting there.** Forage in the **Western Wild Woods**, the base game's pine woods at the forest's north-west corner. Each forage has a 35% chance to turn up a **Trail West**, a game trail that stays on the board. Its **Walk West to Wildrye Plain** takes an hour and a half, and a **Trail to the Forest** on Wildrye Plain walks you back. The paper map shows the two grassland places once visited.

| Place | What is there |
|---|---|
| **Wildrye Plain** | Two Meadowgrass Patches, Forage (fibres, stones) and **Search the Grazing Grounds**. West exit to Sedge Fen. |
| **Sedge Fen** | A Cattail Patch and a Reed Patch, Forage (fibres, willow twigs). East exit back. |

**The moose.** A **Moose** browses Sedge Fen and grazes Wildrye Plain, with or without any other mod. It never attacks on its own. **Approach** it to **Leave it alone** or fight it (a hard fight: it swings antlers, knocks you down, charges and stamps, with bruises, sprains, lacerations, fractures and Puncture Wounds). A kill leaves a **Moose Carcass**: **Butcher** it (1.5 hours) for 8 to 10 raw Meat, 3 Bones, 6 Fresh Skins and, half the time, an Antler. The carcass rots in 3 days and a killed moose returns after two weeks.

**Search the Grazing Grounds** (45 minutes, needs daylight and stamina) finds Bones now and then, or nothing.

**With Harsh Wilds installed**, the same search can also turn up a **Wisent Carcass** and, in winter, a **Musk Ox Carcass**. They are Harsh Wilds' own cards, butchered with its own actions. The living animals do not come here. Without Harsh Wilds nothing breaks: the search gives bones or nothing.

**With Forest Lore installed**, its **Standing Stones** also stand on Wildrye Plain, one ring only, with Forest Lore's own actions. Without it nothing is placed.

## The mountains

The snowy heights north of the forest.

**Getting there.** Forage in **Fangvale**, the base game's wolf valley. Each forage has a 30% chance to turn up a **Trail North**, a goat trail that stays on the board. Its **Climb North to Scree Slope** takes an hour and a half, and a **Trail to the Forest** on Scree Slope walks you back in an hour. The paper map shows the mountain places once visited.

| Place | What is there |
|---|---|
| **Scree Slope** | Forage (stones, flint), the way into the Greenstone Grotto and the cliff up to Windgap Col. East exit to Steaming Shelf. |
| **Steaming Shelf** | A **Hot Spring**, a **Rock Salt Seam** and Forage (stones). |
| **Windgap Col** | A snowy saddle: Forage (stones, flint) and ways into the Rust Grotto and the Glimmer Grotto. |
| **Greenstone Grotto** | Two Copper Veins (mine with a pickaxe for greenstone) and Forage. |
| **Rust Grotto** | Forage for Dried Bog Iron ore. |
| **Glimmer Grotto** | Forage for stones and, about 1 search in 9, a **Geode** that cracks into a Calcite Crystal. |

The caves are off the paper map and keep the forest's temperature.

**The Hot Spring.** **Bathe in the Hot Spring** (half an hour) washes off 30 filth and wets you. It also gives **Spring Warmth**, which adds 16 to how warm you feel and fades over 4 hours. While you are wet the net is about +6, and the full +16 holds once you dry.

**The Rock Salt Seam.** **Chip out Salt** (half an hour, needs stamina) gives 1 or 2 Salt. The seam regrows a little under one chip a day. The base game's Salt Bed is not used: its Scrape Salt buttons give nothing in this game version.

**Rope and Pitons.** A blueprint in Advanced Tools, unlocked once you have a Rope. It takes 2 hours to research and an hour to make, from a Rope, 2 metal nuggets and a hard hammering tool. Held in your hand it raises the base game's Climbing Aid by a quarter of its range, which makes every climb that reads it safer. With Advanced Copper Tools' Grappling Hook the two reach half the range, so a climb never becomes certain.

**The cliff.** **Climb the Cliff to Windgap Col** (half an hour) works only with Rope and Pitons in your hand. Climbing down needs no gear. Either way a fall (an abrasion or a bruise) is possible, and you still arrive.

## The fjords

A cold, steep coast beyond the mountains. There is no boat.

**Getting there.** Windgap Col's **North** exit is a 15-minute hop to **Fjord Head**, and Fjord Head's **South** exit leads back. You need Rope and Pitons for the one cliff climb up to Windgap Col.

| Place | What is there |
|---|---|
| **Fjord Head** | **The Fjord**, Forage (stones, kelp, shells) and **Search the Snowfield**. East to Seal Skerry, North to Puffin Cliffs. |
| **Seal Skerry** | The Fjord and Forage (kelp, shells, old bones). Where seals and the walrus haul out. |
| **Puffin Cliffs** | The Fjord and Forage (stones, feathers, now and then a Wild Egg). |

**Fjord chill.** All three places lower how warm you feel by 8.

**The Fjord.** **Wash in the Fjord** (15 minutes) leaves you soaked in icy water. **Watch the Water** (half an hour) is a look out over the fjord: usually grey water, sometimes seals, and now and then a whale far out. The whales are a sight, not an animal, and the water gives no salt.

**Animals.** Seals, walrus and puffins are met with **Approach** and fought with the base game's combat. Every bite is a Bite Wound. None attacks on its own or leaves tracks.

| Animal | Where | When | A kill leaves |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Seal** | Seal Skerry, Fjord Head | always; it mostly slips into the water | Seal Carcass |
| **Walrus** | Seal Skerry | always; a hard, dangerous fight | Walrus Carcass |
| **Puffin** | Puffin Cliffs, Fjord Head, Seal Skerry | 05:00 to 20:00; by night on Puffin Cliffs | Bird Meat, Feathers, Bird Bones |

- **Seal Carcass**: **Butcher** (45 minutes) for 3 to 4 raw Meat, 1 to 2 Fat Chunks, 2 Fresh Skins and 1 to 2 Bones. It rots in 2.5 days, and a killed seal returns after a week.
- **Walrus Carcass**: **Butcher** (1.5 hours) for 8 to 10 raw Meat, 3 to 4 Fat Chunks, 6 Fresh Skins, 3 Bones and 2 Tusks. It rots in 3 days, and a killed walrus returns after two weeks (a puffin after three days).
- **Search the Snowfield** (Fjord Head, 45 minutes, needs daylight and stamina) finds Bones or nothing. With Harsh Wilds installed it can also turn up a **Musk Ox Carcass** in winter. Without it nothing breaks.

## Notes on existing saves

- **No new run needed:** the fjord, mountain and grassland places, the sixteen caves and the interior boards get their save entries when a run starts. The Trail West and Trail North are forage finds, so an older save finds them on its next forage there. Fishing, the Fish Trap, path cards, canoes, and the Reef Water are put back or added on arrival. Growing plants in the Garden Plot needs nothing new.
- **A new run is needed** to see the palms, the wild plants, the Seagull Nest, the Sea and Tide Pool at Seaward Sands: these are among a board's first cards, so a save that already visited the board does not gain them, and lost ones are not put back.
- The animals are not board cards and are expected to appear in an existing save too, but that has not been confirmed in game. An older save's new path cards and caves should appear on its next visit, which has not been watched in a real older save yet.
- Copper Veins and the Spring are not put back, and a mined-out vein does not return.
- The old Island Air, Mountain Air, Fjord Chill and Volcanic Heat cards no longer exist. An existing save simply loses them.
- Each new place adds a small entry (about 5 KB) to the save.
- **Long island stays:** when you return to a place you left, the game catches up on the time that passed, but the framework caps that at 14 in-game days. After a longer island stay your home base catches up for 14 days only: food spoils, fires burn down and crops grow as if you had been away 14 days.

**For modders.** The code is four small classes, one of them a Harmony patch:

- `EnvSaveDataPreCreate` creates each non-clone board's save entry at run start.
- `ClimateService` applies the island, volcano, mountain and fjord temperature offsets from the place you stand on.
- `LandmarkSeed` keeps one ring of Forest Lore's Standing Stones on Wildrye Plain when Forest Lore is installed.
- `OwlIslandGuard`, the Harmony patch, refuses the Sirus23 Mod Collection's wild Owl a move onto any Far Territory board, and does nothing without that mod.

**Optional companions** (nothing breaks without them): Harsh Wilds (wisent and musk ox carcasses), Forest Lore (Standing Stones on Wildrye Plain), Sirus23 Mod Collection (wild sheep on island places), Advanced Copper Tools (its Grappling Hook also raises Climbing Aid).

There are no configuration options. **Language:** English and Simplified Chinese.

## Installation

Download Far Territory from its [Nexus Mods page](https://www.nexusmods.com/cardsurvivalfantasyforest/mods/54), or install it from the Mod Update Manager suite, where it is listed as "Far Territory (Pre-release)". The source is on [GitHub](https://github.com/Jaredcscott/CSFF_Mods_CrispyWhips/tree/main/Far_Territory).

### Requirements
- [BepInEx 5.x](https://github.com/BepInEx/BepInEx/releases) for Card Survival: Fantasy Forest
- CSFF Mod Framework 2.28.0 or later (`CSFF_Mod_Framework`)

### Steps
1. Install BepInEx 5 and CSFF Mod Framework.
2. Extract the `Far_Territory` folder into your `BepInEx/plugins/` directory.
3. Launch the game. An existing save works: see the notes above for which cards an older save gains and which need a new run.

Your plugins folder should look like:
```
BepInEx/
  plugins/
    CSFF_Mod_Framework/
    Far_Territory/
      Far_Territory.dll
      ModInfo.json
      CardData/
      WorldMap/
      ...
```
