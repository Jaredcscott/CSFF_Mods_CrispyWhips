# Utility Belt - Changelog

All notable changes to this mod are documented here.

## [0.1.1] - Unreleased

### Added
- Quick Transfer 1.9.0 is included: its own build ships beside `Utility_Belt.dll` under its own GUID, with its overlay text in `Localization/` (English and Simplified Chinese). It needs CSFF Mod Framework, as the standalone does.

- Skill Speed Boost 1.11.0 is included the same way, under its own GUID and settings. Its Area Familiarity XP bonus is on out of the box (as in the standalone); delete `Skill_Speed_Boost.dll` from `Utility_Belt/` to keep XP vanilla. It needs CSFF Mod Framework, as the standalone does.

- Utility Belt's first feature of its own: `Accessibility > Hide Rain And Snow Effects` (default off). When on, the falling rain and snow drawn over the screen while you are outside are hidden, through the same switch the game uses indoors. The weather itself, its sound, its colour tint and its effects on your character are unchanged, and Clear weather has nothing to hide. Utility Belt now applies Harmony patches (one postfix on the game's per-frame special-effects update).

- `Display > Clock Format` (`TwentyFourHour` by default, or `TwelveHour`): shows the main clock in a compact 12-hour form, "9:45p" instead of "21:45" (`a` or `p`; a small 上午/下午 in Simplified Chinese). The text is shrunk by a rich-text size tag when it would be wider than the 24-hour clock, so it stays inside the clock box. Only that clock changes; save-slot times and the log stay 24-hour. One more postfix, on the game's per-frame graphics update.

- `Hotkeys > Cycle Equipped Containers` (backquote by default) opens your next worn card that has an inventory (a sack, a sheath), in equipment-row order, wrapping to the first after the last; it opens the bag exactly as a click does. `Hotkeys > Open Equipped Container 1` to `5` (off by default) open the first to fifth such card directly. Keys are ignored while you type in a text field; none defaults to a number key, since the game uses 1 to 9 for bookmarks.

- Item lock: `Hotkeys > Lock Toggle` (`L` by default). With a container open, rest the pointer on a card inside it and press the key to lock it; a small padlock is drawn on the card. The game's Take out all items button then takes out everything else and leaves locked cards in place (a prefix on `InspectionPopup.EmptyInventory` that makes the game's own per-card move for each unlocked card). The game's Take out all items button, Quick Transfer batches and Repeat Action runs honour a lock (the last two through a lookup of `Utility_Belt.LockApi.IsLocked` that costs nothing when Utility Belt is absent); dragging the card yourself ignores it. A lock is keyed by the card's kind and the tick it was made, so cards that arrived together lock together, and a card that turns into another card loses its lock. Locks are kept per save slot in `BepInEx/config/UtilityBelt/ItemLocks_Slot<N>.tsv` (one line per lock, version column first), pruned on load of cards that no longer exist, and cleared by a new game in that slot. Recipe loading and blueprint popups still empty a container completely. `Utility_Belt.LockApi.IsLocked(object card)` is public, for other mods to call by reflection.

- Sort Container: `Hotkeys > Sort Container` (`O` by default). With a container open, each press changes its order: freshest first (spoilage bar), then highest Quality first, then back to the order it had before the first press (remembered until that container's window closes). It reorders the container's own slots and the cards inside each pile, and moves no card out of the container; a slot holding a locked card keeps its place. The game merges cards of one kind into one pile every time a container opens, so two piles of one item cannot be interleaved by freshness. The order is kept through a save and load (measured). It does nothing in small built-in inventories such as the Cooking Pot, in cooking containers, fixed-slot stations or containers holding a saved recipe. Utility Belt reads the key only (no patch): it reorders the open container's card list and asks the game to redraw it.

- Capacity and warmth readouts: `Display > Show Capacity And Warmth Readouts` (off by default). When on, the Info tab of the inspection popup adds one short line under the card's description: a container's `Weight: 1250 / 4000` and, for a container you fill by pouring (a bowl, a jar, a pot), `Liquid: 300 / 300`; or, for a bed, shelter or hide, `Warmth while sleeping: +2`, read from the card's own passive effects (the Perceived Temperature effects gated on sleeping). No comfort figure is shown (the game has none to read). Garments, tools and arrows never show a liquid figure. The line is a postfix on `InspectionPopup.SetupInventory` and `RefreshDescription`; it appends to the popup's shared description text and removes exactly what it appended before the next write, so it never outlives its card. English and Simplified Chinese.

- Quickswap: `Equipment > Quickswap When Equipping` (off by default). When on, equipping a card whose equipment slot is full (a second coat, a second hat) swaps instead of refusing: the card you wear goes back to where the new one came from (the item bar, the open container it was in, or the ground) and the new one is worn, through the game's own moves, so equipment effects are exactly those of taking one off and putting the other on (measured). Only your own drag or double-click; an NPC's equip is untouched. Nothing swaps when either card cannot be moved by hand, when the worn card is mandatory equipment, or when the container the new card came from cannot take the old one (the game's own refusal shows). One more prefix, on the game's card-move method.

- Sealed mark: `Display > Show Sealed Mark` (off by default). When on, a small red wax seal is drawn in the top right corner of a sealed container's card (a sealed storage pot or fermentation bin), so a sealed one reads apart from an unsealed one on the board; the lock's padlock sits top left. Which cards count as sealed is `Display > Sealed Cards`, a comma-separated list of card IDs defaulting to the Sealed Storage Pot, the placed Sealed Storage Pot and the Sealed Fermentation Bin; the game's Sealed Container tag is not used because it is also on the Unsealed Storage Pot (measured). Display only; no patch, the mark is drawn from the plugin's update.

- Character randomiser: `Character > Show Randomise Button` (on by default, and it does nothing until pressed). The character-creation perk screen gets a **Randomise** button under Submit (English and Simplified Chinese). Each press re-rolls the character's perks through the screen's own equip and unequip calls: it keeps the difficulty rating band the character had, at most one perk per perk group, only unlocked perks, as many perks as the character had (4 to 8 when it had none), and leaves the perks of the mandatory groups (skin, hair, eyes), the start location group and the start season groups alone. Perks from a not-for-distribution mod are left out. One more postfix, on the creation screen's setup method.

- Auto-Pull Materials for Repeat Action: `RepeatAction > Auto-Pull Materials` (off by default). When on, a Repeat Action run that the game refuses because a card it needs in your hand sits in a container instead (a chest on the ground, a bag you carry or wear, in the place you are in) takes one such card into the carried row and tries that repeat once more; the base game's case is the Composite Bow's Train, which needs a Simple Arrow in hand. Locked cards, cards the game will not let you move by hand, cards in another place, and cards in construction sites, cooking pots and other built-in inventories or NPC inventories are never taken, and nothing moves when the carried row has no room (so a carried card is never swapped into the container). A card in an open container moves through Quick Transfer's replay of the right-click; one in a closed container through the game's own `GraphicsManager.MoveCardToSlot`, the call that click ends in, after the same checks. If nothing is found, the run stops exactly as Repeat Action does alone. Repeat Action already uses ingredients and targets that sit in a container where you are (measured: three Fibers thrown into a Compost Bin from a closed Storage Chest, Complete 3/3 with the option off), so the option is for the cards the game wants in hand. It subscribes to Repeat Action's new `ActionPatch.OnRequirementsUnmet` event only while on, and calls Quick Transfer's new `QuickTransferPatch.TryClickMoveOne`; both are found by reflection from the first Update on whichever copy BepInEx loaded, with no dependency either way. Checked by harness scenario ub_c1_autopull.

- Store Products In for Repeat Action: `RepeatAction > Store Products In` (off by default) with `Hotkeys > Pick Store Container` (`V`). With the option on, rest the pointer on a container (a chest on the ground, a bag you carry) and press `V`: it becomes the destination, and a short notice names it. From then on every card a Repeat Action run makes (the slices of a Cut, the products of a drag-and-drop craft) is moved into it as each repeat finishes; `V` on the same container again stops it. When the container is full (by weight or slots) storing stops for the rest of that run with one notice ("... is full: products stay where they land") and the run itself carries on. The destination is forgotten when you change place, when the container is destroyed, and when the option is turned off. Cards you locked, cards the game will not let you move by hand, liquids, pinned cards and cards in an NPC's hands are left where they land. Measured: a crafted product lands on the ground, and Quick Transfer's click replay on a ground card does not put it in an open chest, so a product moves through the game's own `InGameCardBase.DropInInventory` (after the game's own filter, weight and slot checks); Quick Transfer's replay is used only for a product that is in the carried row while its destination's window is open. Repeat Action 2.2.1 carries two new public events for it, `ActionPatch.OnIterationStarting` and `OnIterationDone`, which Utility Belt finds by reflection only while the option is on, so with it off Repeat Action runs exactly as its standalone release.

- Equip Spare Tool On Break for Repeat Action: `RepeatAction > Equip Spare Tool On Break` (off by default). When a worn tool breaks during a Repeat Action run, the next repeat is refused by the game (measured: worn Leather Gloves breaking during a repeated Nettle Patch Harvest stop the run with "requirements no longer met", even with Repeat Action's own Stop On Tool Break and Use Spare Tool On Break on, which cover a dragged tool). With this option on, Utility Belt puts on a spare that does the same job (the card, or a card with the tag, the action needs worn; the one with the most use left), from your hands, the ground or a container in the place you are in, through the game's own equip move (`GraphicsManager.MoveCardToSlot` into the equipment line, the call the game's equip drop makes), and Repeat Action tries that repeat once more. Cards you locked, cards the game will not let you move by hand, cards in another place and cards in construction sites, cooking pots, other built-in inventories or NPC inventories are never used; a spare whose slot is still full is put on only while Quickswap When Equipping is on. It subscribes its own handler to Repeat Action's existing `ActionPatch.OnRequirementsUnmet` event only while on; no new Repeat Action hook was needed. Checked by harness scenario ub_c3_spare_tool.

### Changed
- The included Repeat Action is now 2.2.1 (the module's current build), with its new requirements-unmet event, and the included Quick Transfer 1.9.0 carries its new one-card click replay.
- The ModInfo description and the README's Planned line still said Quick Transfer and Repeat Action do not honour item locks; they have since the lock bridges, and both now say so.
- The README's Planned line still listed storing products in a container and equipping a spare tool; both ship in this version, so it no longer lists them.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-01

### Added
- Repeat Action is included: its own build ships beside `Utility_Belt.dll` under its own GUID, so it replaces nothing and shares the standalone's settings.
- The scaffold: the host plugin loads and prints its one startup line. No features.
- `Diagnostics > Log Module Sources` (default off): when on, logs once at startup which file Quick Transfer, Repeat Action and Skill Speed Boost each loaded from, or that one is not installed.
